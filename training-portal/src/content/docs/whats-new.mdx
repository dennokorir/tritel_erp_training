## 20 November 2026: Linking projects and tenders

Projects and tenders are now easier to connect, and you can link a project to a tender just for reference.

**For delivery project managers, the Head of Delivery and Delivery Finance:**
- **Link only**: when you link an existing project to a won tender, choose **Link as › Link only** to record the tender for reference without making it a delivery project. It stays out of delivery tracking and the delivery KPIs; **Make It a Delivery Project** turns it into one later. See [Link an existing project to its tender](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/link-an-existing-project/).
- New screen **Link Projects to Tenders** (**Project › Delivery › Setup**, and **Accounting › Project Budgets › Tracking** for Delivery Finance): projects with no tender, each with a suggested tender marked **Certain** or **Possible**. See [Link projects to their tenders](/projects/how-to/approvers/link-projects-to-tenders/).

**For everyone who works with tenders and projects:**
- Search projects by **Tender**, and tenders by **Project (code or name)**, with new filters and columns both ways. The tender's **Delivery projects** button is now **Projects**, and lists every linked project. See [Delivery projects](/projects/concepts/delivery-projects/).

## 19 November 2026: Project Delivery, budgets and project codes

Project Delivery now calls the delivery baseline a **Delivery Budget**, its menus follow your role, every project has a code, and purchase orders are checked against the accounting budget. See [Project Delivery menus](/projects/menus/) for every menu and the old names.

**For delivery project managers:**
- "Baseline" is now **Delivery Budget** throughout, and some buttons were renamed: **Submit for Approval** (was Confirm for Approval), **Return for Changes** (was Return to Draft), **Approve as MD** (was MD Approve). See [Delivery Budgets and revisions](/projects/concepts/baselines/) and [Submit a Delivery Budget for approval](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/send-for-approval/).
- Your projects are under **Project › Delivery › Projects › My Delivery Projects**, and **My Submissions** lists your budgets still waiting.
- The **Delivery Project Manager** role now comes automatically when you are Project Manager on an active delivery project. See [Open a delivery project from a won tender](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/open-a-delivery-project/).

**For everyone who works with projects:**
- Every project has a code, such as **P26-049**, shown before its name and carried by its cost account. See [Project codes](/projects/concepts/project-codes/). Only a settings administrator can [correct a project code](/projects/how-to/approvers/correct-a-project-code/).

**For Delivery Finance, Finance and purchasing:**
- A new **Accounting › Project Budgets** menu: budgets to approve, costs after close, Budget vs Actual, Delivery Budgets, Budget Commitments and Cost Accounts to Sort Out.
- Purchase orders show a **Budget status** and a **Budget impact** tab. See [Check a purchase order against the budget](/projects/how-to/approvers/check-an-order-against-the-budget/) and [How orders count against an accounting budget](/projects/calculations/purchase-commitments/).
- New: [Set the cost account on order lines](/projects/how-to/approvers/assign-cost-accounts/), the [Budget Commitments](/projects/reports/budget-commitments/) report, and [Sort out cost accounts with no project](/projects/how-to/approvers/sort-out-cost-accounts/).
- "Odoo budget" is now **Accounting budget**, and the order's **Left in delivery plan** is now **Budget Available**.

## 19 November 2026: Accountability menus by role

The Accountability app's menus now follow your role, and several have plainer names. Nothing about targets, reviews or scores has changed. See [Accountability menus](/accountability/menus/) for every menu and who sees it, including the old names.

**For everyone:**
- **Company Overview** (was Performance Overview) is now its own menu, first in the bar. The app still opens on it. See [Company Overview](/accountability/reports/performance-overview/).
- **My Performance › My Score History** (was Performance Snapshots). **Progress Briefs** only shows while progress briefs are switched on.

**For line managers:**
- **My Team** groups its items under **Set targets** (Team Targets, **Cascades**, **Milestones**, **Next Year's Targets**), **Review** (Review Sessions, Team Reviews, Team Scores) and **Approve** (**Scorecard Changes**, Priority Changes).
- **Organisation › Review Coverage** (was Session Coverage).

**For HR:**
- A new **People** menu: Review Sessions, All Reviews, Scores, Scorecard Changes, Priority Changes and Next Year's Targets. HR sees **My Team** only if they also manage people.
- **Organisation › Compliance** is now open to HR too.
- The separate Scorecard Changes log is gone: use **People › Scorecard Changes** and clear the filters for the full history.
- The Targets Library is seeded with the **Seed from this year** button on the list. See [Maintain the targets library](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/targets-library/).

**For Accountability administrators:**
- **Configuration** (Periods, Metrics, Settings) is a menu of its own, last in the bar. **Board Reviews** has moved to **Organisation**.

## 17 November 2026: Time Off

A new **Time Off** section covers what Tritel has added to Odoo's Time Off app.

**For everyone:**
- [Check my leave and request time off](/leave/how-to/everyone/my-leave/): the **My Leave** page shows your balance first, your requests this year and who in your team is away this week.
- [See who is in with Team Presence](/leave/how-to/everyone/team-presence/), and [who sees whose time off](/leave/concepts/who-sees-what/).
- [Use My Leave and Team Presence on the portal](/leave/how-to/everyone/on-the-portal/), handy on a phone.
- The Time Off menus now follow your role: everyone has **My Leave** (**My Requests**, **My Calendar**, **My Allocations**) and **Team Presence**; approvers get **To Approve**; HR gets **Manage**, **Reporting** and **Configuration**. Several Odoo menus have plainer names. See [Time Off menus](/leave/menus/), which also lists the old names.
- **My Allocations** now shows what kind each allocation is and how many days are left. Maternity and Paternity requests are limited to 90 and 14 days at a time. See [Check my leave and request time off](/leave/how-to/everyone/my-leave/).

**For line managers:**
- [Use My Team](/leave/how-to/line-managers/my-team/): who is in this week, requests waiting for you and your team's balances.
- [Approve, refuse or ask about time off](/leave/how-to/line-managers/approve-time-off/) from the Approvals dashboard or My Actions, or from **Time Off › To Approve › Leave Requests**, which opens on **Waiting for me**, and [delegate your approvals while you're away](/leave/how-to/line-managers/delegate-approvals/).
- [Reminders, escalation and automatic refusal](/leave/calculations/reminders-and-escalation/): what happens to a request nobody decides.

**For HR:**
- New: [Find and fill allocation gaps with Leave Coverage](/leave/how-to/hr/leave-coverage/), under **Time Off › Manage**: who is missing an allocation for each leave type, allocated in one click, and [balance adjustments](/leave/how-to/hr/adjust-a-balance/) kept with a reason.
- New: [Set how staff get each leave type](/leave/how-to/hr/set-up-leave-types/) (earns monthly, yearly grant or on request, and the longest request) and [How staff get leave: allocations](/leave/concepts/allocations/). Annual leave allocations now carry on past 31 December instead of ending.
- [Set reminders, escalation and flags](/leave/how-to/hr/settings/), [give HR the company-wide view](/leave/how-to/hr/company-wide-view/), [hide service accounts](/leave/how-to/hr/hide-service-accounts/) and [download Team Presence to Excel](/leave/how-to/hr/download-presence/).

[Your My Actions lists](/getting-started/my-actions/) now mention the **Time off to approve** group.
