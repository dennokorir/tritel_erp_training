## 17 November 2026

The systems haven't changed. This update fills gaps in the guides for screens that already exist.

**For account managers, finance and purchasing:**
- New: [Link orders and invoices to a tender](/tenders/how-to/bids/link-orders-and-invoices/). How to link a sales order, purchase order, invoice or bill to a won tender, so it shows on the tender's **Sales**, **Purchases**, **Invoices** and **Bills** buttons.

**For the Head of Commercial, the Head of Channel and Tender administrators:**
- New: [Tender Manager configuration](/tenders/configuration/). Every list under **Tender Manager › Configuration**, including tender **Tags**, **Customer Tags**, **Activity Types** and **Last Seen Stages**, and how to add an activity type.

**For everyone who works with customers:**
- [Customer activities and touchpoints](/tenders/concepts/customer-activities/) now explains the customer's form: the **Tenders** button, **Last Seen**, **Last Seen Stage** and the **Go To Market Activities** tab.
- [Account Manager, Bid Liaison and Technical Presales](/tenders/concepts/roles-on-a-tender/) now explains the **Coverage** (named share) and **Touches** lines on the role cards.
- [Market coverage, account ownership and target accounts](/tenders/concepts/market-coverage/) now covers **Target Note**.

**For the Channel team:**
- [OEMs and distributors](/tenders/channel/oems-and-distributors/): how to set a product's OEM on its **Channel** tab.
- [Set the OEM on untagged purchases](/tenders/channel/tag-untagged-purchases/): the **Has OEM** box, the **OEM** column and the **OEM** button on a purchase order.
- [Record vendor opportunities on a CRM opportunity](/tenders/channel/vendor-opportunities/): the two **Tag Type** columns on CRM tags.

**Also updated:**
- [Tenders and Channel FAQ](/tenders/faq/): the weekly Tenders/Commercial digest email.
- [See my performance](/accountability/how-to/everyone/see-my-performance/): the **Performance** tab on an employee's form.
- [Close or reopen a project](/projects/how-to/approvers/close-a-project/): **Closed on** and **Closed by**.
