**My Leave** answers "how many days do I have?" before anything else. It shows your balance, what is waiting for approval, your requests this year and who in your team is away this week, and has a **Request time off** button.

<Illustration name="leave/home-my-leave" caption="My Leave. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the **Time Off** app. It opens on **Home**.
2. If people report to you, Home opens on **My Team**: click **My Leave** at the top. You can also go straight to **Time Off › My Time Off › My Leave**.
3. Read your balance, for example **Annual Leave balance 12 days**. The line underneath shows how it is made up: *15 allocated so far · 2 taken · 1 requested*, and, for leave that builds up on an accrual plan, the total *by 31 Dec*. See [Balances: allocated, taken, requested](/leave/concepts/balances/).
4. Next to it, **Next** shows your next accrual (for example *+1.75 on 1 Dec*), and **Sick Leave left** shows your sick days left and how many you have taken this year.
5. **Waiting for approval** shows your first request still waiting, with its days and dates and whether it is **Waiting for approval** (your manager) or **Waiting for HR**. *+1 more* means another request is waiting too.
6. **Requests in 2026** lists your requests this year with their status: **Waiting for approval**, **Waiting for HR**, **Approved** or **Refused**. Beside it, **Your team this week** shows the colleagues who share your manager, as *In all week* or *Away Mon, Tue*. **Taken each month** charts the days you have taken.
7. To ask for time off, click **Request time off**. Odoo's Time Off **Dashboard** opens. Click **New Time Off** (or click a day in the calendar), choose the **Time Off Type** and the **Dates**, add a **Description**, and save.

## What happens next

- Your request goes to your time-off approver, usually your manager, and shows under **Waiting for approval**. Some leave types also need HR's approval: after your manager approves, the request shows **Waiting for HR**.
- Your **Requested** figure goes up and your **Balance** goes down by the days asked for, until the request is decided.
- Your manager and HR see the request in Team Presence, striped, until it is approved.
- If your approver asks a question, you are notified and reply on the request. See [Answer a question on my request](/leave/how-to/everyone/answer-a-question/).
- If the request is still waiting for your manager when the leave starts, it is refused automatically, and you are told to request it again. See [Time off request workflow](/leave/workflows/time-off-request/).

:::note[Good to know]
- The request list shows up to 8 requests. Use **Time Off › My Time Off › My Time Off** for all of them.
- **Your team this week** only lists colleagues with the same manager as you, and only as in or away. For more, open **Team Presence** at the top.
- The same page is on the portal at **/my/leave**. See [Use My Leave and Team Presence on the portal](/leave/how-to/everyone/on-the-portal/).
:::
