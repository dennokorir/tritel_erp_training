The portal has the same time off pages as the Time Off app, in a lighter layout that works well on a phone: **My Leave** at **/my/leave**, **My Team's Leave** at **/my/team-leave** and **Team Presence** at **/my/presence**. They show the same figures and follow the same rules about who sees whom.

<Illustration name="leave/portal-leave" caption="The portal home page and My Team's Leave. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the portal home page (**/my**) and click **My Leave**. (It is also in the menu under your name, top right.) You see your balance, next accrual, sick leave left, the request waiting, your requests this year, your team this week and the days taken each month, as on [My Leave](/leave/how-to/everyone/my-leave/). **Request time off** opens the Time Off app's **Dashboard** to make the request.
2. If people report to you, click **My Team's Leave**.
3. Click **Team Presence** to see who is in. It works as in [Team Presence](/leave/how-to/everyone/team-presence/): **Week** or **Month**, **‹** and **›**, **Grid** or **Calendar**, and the **Department**, **Type**, **Status**, **Only people away** and search filters. Click a row to highlight it.
4. On **My Team's Leave**, choose whose balances to see: **Direct reports**, **Everyone below me**, or (company-wide list only) **Everyone in the company**. For the wider choices, pick a department from the list. Type in **Search** and press Enter to find someone.
5. Read the table: **Allocated**, **Taken**, **Requested**, **Balance**, **Sick left** and the flag (**OK**, **Low**, **Not taking leave** or **No allocation**). Click a name to open that person's leave; **Back to team** returns to the table.

## What happens next

- Nothing changes: these pages only show time off.
- Team Presence keeps its filters in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view. The person opening it still sees only what they are allowed to see.
- Your browser remembers your last Team Presence filters: opening **/my/presence** on its own brings them back.

:::note[Good to know]
- The portal's My Team's Leave shows the balances table only. **Who is in this week**, **Waiting for you** and **Used this year** are on [My Team](/leave/how-to/line-managers/my-team/) in the Time Off app.
- If people don't report to you, **/my/team-leave** takes you to My Leave instead.
- Opening a team member's leave you may not see is refused. See [Who sees whose time off](/leave/concepts/who-sees-what/).
- HR also sees **Download Excel** on the portal's Team Presence.
:::
