**Team Presence** shows, day by day, who is in, who is away and who has asked for time off. Use it to plan cover, check a week before you book leave, or see how busy a month is. What you see depends on your place in the reporting line: see [Who sees whose time off](/leave/concepts/who-sees-what/).

<Illustration name="leave/team-presence" caption="Team Presence for a week, as a grid. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Time Off › Team Presence**. (The **Team Presence** button on the Time Off home opens it too.)
2. Click **Week** or **Month**. Only working days, Monday to Friday, are shown.
3. Use **‹** and **›** to move to the previous or next week or month. **This week** or **This month** brings you back.
4. Click **Grid** for one row per person, or **Calendar** for one box per day with the names of the people away.
5. Narrow it down if you need to:
   - **Department**: one department, or **All departments**.
   - **Type**: click a leave type to hide or show it. Each type has its own colour.
   - **Status**: **Approved**, **Requested**, or both.
   - **Only people away**: leaves out anyone who is in for the whole period.
   - **Search person or role**: matches a name, job title or department.
   - **Clear filters** puts everything back.
6. Read the summary line: who you are seeing (**Showing: Everyone**, **Your reporting line** or **You and your team**), how many people, how many are away at some point, the person-days away, the busiest day, the number of **busy days** and the requested days waiting. On the current week, **Away today** lists who is away today, or says *Everyone you can see is in.*
7. Read the grid:
   - **Away each day** counts the people away on each day. A count in red is a busy day; **PH** is a public holiday.
   - Each person's day shows their leave type (in the month view, a short code such as **AL**). **AM**, **PM** or **½** is a half day. A striped day with a dashed border (*Requested: …*) is waiting for approval. **Away** is a colleague's approved time off, without the type.
   - **Days** is the days away in the period.
   - Click a person's row to highlight it across every day; click again or press **Esc** to clear it. Click a coloured day to open the request, if you are allowed to open it.

In the calendar, each day shows *2 away*, the names of the people away (click one to open the request), and **+3 more** when there are more than four. A busy day has a red line along the top.

## What happens next

- Nothing changes: Team Presence only shows time off. To request leave, use **Request time off** on [My Leave](/leave/how-to/everyone/my-leave/).
- The page remembers your choice of week or month, grid or calendar, and your filters in this browser, and opens on the current week or month next time.

:::note[Good to know]
- A day is **busy** when at least 20% of the people shown are away, and never fewer than 2 people. HR can change the percentage. See [Busy days, Low and Not taking leave](/leave/calculations/busy-days-and-flags/).
- The legend at the bottom explains each code, for example *AL = Annual Leave*. Codes are the initials of the leave type's name.
- Requests waiting for approval show only for people you see in full, never for colleagues you see as **In or Away only**.
- HR also has a **Download Excel** button. See [Download Team Presence to Excel](/leave/how-to/hr/download-presence/).
- If nobody matches your filters, the page says *Nobody matches these filters.* with a **Clear filters** link.
:::
