Sometimes a balance needs correcting: days given back for working on a public holiday, a correction after a mistake, or days taken off a grant. **Adjust balance** in Leave Coverage raises or lowers the person's allocation by the days you enter and keeps your reason with it, so anyone can see later why the balance changed.

<Illustration name="leave/hr-adjust-balance" caption="Adjusting Employee A's Annual Leave in Leave Coverage. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Time Off › Manage › Leave Coverage** and, if needed, choose the year with **‹** and **›**.
2. Click the person's cell for the leave type. The panel on the right shows **Allocated**, **Taken**, **Requested** and **Left**, the lines that make up the allocation, and the **Adjust Annual Leave** form (named after the leave type).

   If you clicked the person's name instead, the panel lists every leave type: click **Adjust balance** under the one to change.
3. In **Days (negative to take off)**, enter the days to add, for example *1*, or a negative number to take days off, for example *-2*. Half days are fine (*0.5*).
4. In **Reason**, say why, for example *Worked on a public holiday*.
5. Click **Apply**. *Adjusted: 18.5 → 19.5 days allocated.* appears, and the figures update.

## What happens next

- The allocation's days go up or down by what you entered, so **Allocated** and **Left** change at once, here and on the person's **My Leave**.
- The adjustment is logged:
  - in the panel, as a line **Adjustment:** and your reason, with the date, your name and *included above*;
  - in the summary at the top of Leave Coverage, in **N adjustments in 2026**;
  - as a note on the allocation, for example *Adjusted by +1 days by Employee H: Worked on a public holiday (18.5 → 19.5).*

### Which allocation changes

An adjustment changes one allocation: the person's approved allocation of that leave type in force on the **Figures as at** date (today for this year), the earning one first if there are two. If none is in force on that date, it changes the latest approved one in the year.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Enter the number of days to add, or a negative number to take off.* | **Days** is empty or 0. | Enter the days. |
| *Give a reason for the adjustment; it is kept with the balance.* | **Reason** is empty. | Say why you're adjusting. |
| *That would leave Employee A with less Annual Leave than they have already taken (5 days). The most you can take off is 13.5 days.* | You're taking off more days than are left after the approved leave already taken. | Take off no more than the number shown. |
| *That would take the whole allocation away. The most you can take off is 29.99 days.* | Taking off would leave a granted allocation (such as a yearly grant) at 0 days or less. | Take off fewer days. To remove the allocation altogether, open it from **Time Off › Manage › All Allocations**. |
| *Annual Leave earns up to a limit of 30 days unused, and the next monthly run would remove anything above it. The most you can add now is 2 days.* | The accrual plan caps how many unused days the allocation can hold, and the next monthly run would take off anything above it. | Add no more than the number shown. |
| *Employee A has no approved Annual Leave allocation for 2026 to adjust. Allocate it first.* | There's nothing approved to adjust for that year. | Allocate it first (see [Find and fill allocation gaps with Leave Coverage](/leave/how-to/hr/leave-coverage/)), or approve the allocation that is waiting. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Adjusting a past year changes that year's allocation.** For a **Yearly grant** such as Sick Off, that is last year's grant, which has ended, so days added can't be used now. An allocation that **earns monthly** has no end date, so in any year you adjust the same running allocation.
- The check on days already taken counts approved leave only. Requests still waiting for approval aren't counted.
- The adjustment count in the summary goes by the date the adjustment was made, not the year of the allocation.
- **Adjust balance** only shows when there is an approved allocation to change.
:::
