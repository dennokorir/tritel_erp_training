Some employee records aren't people who take leave: the Administrator account, a shared HR mailbox, a test user. They clutter Team Presence and skew its counts. Hiding them takes them out of every Time Off people list; their records are kept and you can bring them back at any time.

<Illustration name="leave/hr-hide-service-accounts" caption="An employee record for a shared mailbox. The header shows one button or the other, depending on whether the record is hidden. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Employees › Employees** and open the record, for example *HR Mailbox*.
2. Click **Hide from Time Off views** at the top of the form.
3. A grey line appears above the name: *Hidden from the Time Off views (Team Presence, the Time Off home and the approvals lists).* A note on the record's chatter says who hid it.
4. To bring the person back, click **Show in Time Off views**.

**Several at once (Time Off administrators):** in the **Employees** list, tick the records, then open **Action** and choose **Hide from Time Off views** or **Show in Time Off views**.

To find hidden records, use the **Hidden from Time Off views** filter in the Employees search. The same setting is also a tick box, **Hide from Time Off views**, on the **HR Settings** tab under **Status**.

## What happens next

Once hidden, the person no longer appears in:

- **Team Presence**: the grid, the calendar, the counts, the busy days and the Excel download, on screen and on the portal;
- the **Time Off home**: **My Team**, its balances and the week strip, and colleagues' **Your team this week**;
- the time off waiting lists on the **Approvals dashboard** and in **My Actions**. The daily reminders and escalations skip their requests too.

Their time off, allocations and employee record are unchanged, and they can still open their own **My Leave**. Showing them again restores everything.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Only Time Off Administrators and people on the Company-wide Time Off View list can hide or show employees in the Time Off views.* | You don't have either right. | Ask a Time Off administrator. |
| You can't see the buttons | They only show to Time Off administrators and people on the Company-wide Time Off View list. | As above. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Hide only records that are not real people. Someone on long leave should stay visible, so their absence shows in Team Presence.
- The daily job that refuses requests whose dates have passed still applies to hidden records. See [Set reminders, escalation and flags](/leave/how-to/hr/settings/).
- The **HR Settings** tab is only shown to HR officers. People on the company-wide list without HR rights use the buttons at the top instead.
:::
