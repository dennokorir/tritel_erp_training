Managers see their own reporting line in the Time Off screens, and everyone else sees only themselves and their close colleagues. HR usually needs the whole company. The **Company-wide Viewers** list gives named people that view, without making them Time Off administrators.

<Illustration name="leave/hr-company-wide-view" caption="The Company-wide Viewers list. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Time Off › Configuration**.
2. Click **Company-wide Viewers**, then **New**. A blank row opens at the bottom of the list.
3. Choose the **User** and, if you like, say **Why** they need it, for example *HR Officer, keeps the leave records*.
4. Click **Save**.

To take someone off the list, tick their row and delete it (**Action › Delete**).

## What happens next

The person, from their next page load:

- sees everyone in **Team Presence** in full, with leave types and waiting requests, and gets **Download Excel**;
- gets the **My Team** page with **Everyone in the company**. If nobody reports to them, the whole company is their team;
- can open anyone's leave history from **My Team**, on screen and on the portal;
- can use **Hide from Time Off views** and **Show in Time Off views** on an employee's form.

It does not change who can approve or refuse time off.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *This person is already on the company-wide view list.* | The user has a row already. | Nothing to do. Edit **Why** on the existing row if needed. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Time Off administrators already see everyone in **Team Presence** and can download it, but they only get **My Team** and leave histories for people below them. Add them to the list if they need more.
- System administrators are treated as being on the list.
- Time Off officer rights don't open other people's leave histories on **My Team**. Use this list for that.
:::
