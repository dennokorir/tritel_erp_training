The download is exactly what Team Presence shows: the same period, the same people and the same filters. Use it for a monthly absence report, or to send a department head their team's month.

<Illustration name="leave/hr-download-presence" caption="Team Presence set to a month and one department, with Download Excel. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Time Off › Team Presence**.
2. Choose **Week** or **Month**, and move to the period with **‹** and **›**.
3. Set the filters you want: **Department**, the leave **Type** buttons, **Approved** and **Requested** under **Status**, **Only people away**, or a name in **Search person or role**.
4. Click **Download Excel**. The file is saved as, for example, *team-presence-month-2026-11-01.xlsx*.

The portal page **/my/presence** has the same **Download Excel** button.

## What happens next

The file has three sheets:

| Sheet | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Presence** | One row per person (**Person**, **Job**, **Department**), grouped by department, and one column per working day, Monday to Friday. The top row, **Away each day**, counts people away; busy days are in red and public holidays show *PH*. Each day off shows the leave type in its colour: the full name for a week, a short code for a month (*AL* for Annual Leave). A **?** marks days requested but not yet approved, **½** a half day. **Days away** totals each row. |
| **Totals** | Per person, the approved days of each leave type, **Requested (days)** and **Total away (days)**. |
| **Filters** | The **Period**, who downloaded it (**Shown to**), the **Scope**, the filters used, the **Busy day** threshold, the meaning of every code, and when it was **Downloaded**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Only Monday to Friday are included. Weekends never appear, even for people who work them.
- **Total away (days)** counts approved time off only. Waiting requests are in **Requested (days)**.
- People hidden from the Time Off views are left out. See [Hide service accounts from Time Off](/leave/how-to/hr/hide-service-accounts/).
- Anyone else opening the download link directly gets a "not found" page.
:::
