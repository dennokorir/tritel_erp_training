Time off requests waiting for you appear on the **Approvals Dashboard**, next to your other approvals, with what you need to decide: the dates, the step, how long it has waited, the balance after, and who else is away then. You can approve, refuse with a reason, or ask the employee a question first.

<Illustration name="leave/approve-dashboard" caption="Time Off to Approve on the Approvals Dashboard, with the Refuse time off window. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the **Approvals** app. It opens on the **Approvals Dashboard**, on the **My Queue** tab.
2. Under **Time Off to Approve**, read the request. The first line is the person, leave type, days and dates; *starts in 2 days*, *starts today* or *dates have passed* warn you when it's close. The second line gives the step (for example *step 1 of 2: manager, then HR*), how many working days it has waited, the **balance after** approving it, and who with the same manager is **also away then**. A third line shows *You cover for Employee D* or *Escalated to you from Employee D* when you decide for someone else, *Question open, waiting for the reply*, and the employee's reason in quotes. Click the name to open the full request.
3. To approve, click **Approve**. A message confirms it, for example *Employee A's Annual Leave approved.*
4. To refuse, click **Refuse**, type the reason in *Refuse time off*, and click **Refuse**. The employee gets the reason on the request.
5. To ask first, click **Ask**, type your question in *Ask about this time off*, and click **Send question**. The message says *Question sent to Employee A.* The request stays waiting for you.
6. When the employee replies on the request, the *Question open* line disappears. Then decide with **Approve** or **Refuse**.

You can also start from **My Actions** (in Accountability or the Tender Manager): the **Time off to approve** group lists the same requests and opens each one. Or start from **Waiting for you** on [My Team](/leave/how-to/line-managers/my-team/).

As a time-off approver you also have **Time Off › To Approve › Leave Requests**, which lists them in Odoo. It opens on the **Waiting for me** filter, which follows the same rule as the dashboard: requests at your step, as a delegate, or escalated to you. Clear the filter to see every request you are allowed to see. Use the dashboard to decide as a delegate: see below.

## What happens next

- **Approved, one step:** the request is **Approved**. It counts as taken and shows in Team Presence.
- **Approved, step 1 of 2:** it moves to **Second Approval** and waits for HR, with a new waiting clock. It leaves your list.
- **Refused:** the request is **Refused**, and the employee is notified with your reason.
- **For someone else:** when you decide as a delegate or after escalation, a note on the request says so, for example *Approved by Employee B for Employee D (delegation).*
- **Asked:** your question is posted on the request and sent to the employee. **Question open** is ticked on the request until they reply there.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *You can't decide Employee A's Annual Leave: it is waiting for Employee D.* | Someone else owns this step, for example the delegation ended or the request moved on. | Refresh the dashboard. If it should be yours, ask the approver to delegate, or ask HR. |
| *You can't decide Employee A's Annual Leave: it is waiting for HR.* | It is no longer waiting for a decision: someone else already approved or refused it. | Refresh the dashboard. |
| *This time off request no longer exists.* | The employee cancelled or deleted it. | Nothing to do. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Requests that are close (starting within 2 days) or already escalated are highlighted, and count as overdue in My Actions.
- A delegate and an escalation recipient should decide on the dashboard. The buttons on the request form follow Odoo's own approval rules, which don't know about delegation.
- Time Off officers can still decide other people's requests in **Time Off › Manage › All Leave Requests**. The dashboard and **Waiting for me** list only the requests waiting for you as approver, delegate or after escalation.
- Undecided requests are reminded, escalated and eventually refused by daily jobs. See [Reminders, escalation and automatic refusal](/leave/calculations/reminders-and-escalation/).
:::
