## Why was my request refused when nobody refused it?

Two daily jobs refuse requests that are still waiting:

- If your request is still waiting for your manager when the leave starts, it is refused, and you get the note *Refused automatically: it was still waiting for approval when the leave started …*. Request it again if you still need it.
- Separately, every request still **To Submit**, **To Approve** or **Second Approval** is refused by a job that runs early each morning once its last day has passed, without a note. This includes requests your manager approved that are waiting for HR.

See [Reminders, escalation and automatic refusal](/leave/calculations/reminders-and-escalation/).

## Why was sick leave I recorded afterwards refused?

Sick leave is often recorded after the days have passed. If it is still waiting for approval when the daily job next runs (early each morning), the job refuses it, because its last day is in the past. Ask the approver, or HR, to approve it on the day you record it.

## My approver is on leave. Who decides my request?

If they have delegated their approvals, their delegate can give the manager's approval. If not, they get a reminder after 2 working days, and after 4 working days (or 2 days before your leave starts) the request is escalated to their own manager, who can then decide it. The timings are set by HR. See [Approval steps, delegates and escalation](/leave/concepts/approval-steps/).

## Why do I only see "Away" for some colleagues?

Colleagues who share your manager show as **In** or **Away** only. Their leave type and waiting requests are private to them, their managers and HR. See [Who sees whose time off](/leave/concepts/who-sees-what/).

## Why is someone missing from Team Presence?

They are outside what you can see, their employee record is archived, or they are hidden from the Time Off views because they aren't a person taking leave. See [Hide service accounts from Time Off](/leave/how-to/hr/hide-service-accounts/).

## Why don't I have My Team?

**My Team** is for people with others reporting to them in Odoo, and for HR on the company-wide list. If someone reports to you but you don't see it, ask HR to set you as their **Manager** on their employee record.

## Why does my balance differ from what I expected?

**Balance** is what you can still request: **Allocated** minus **Taken** minus **Requested**. **Taken** includes approved days still to come, and **Requested** is time off waiting for approval. See [Balances: allocated, taken, requested](/leave/concepts/balances/).

## Why doesn't my team's "Sick left" show?

The column only shows for a leave type with "sick" in its name that the person has an allocation for.

## Why can't I see Download Excel?

The download is for Time Off administrators and people on the Company-wide Viewers list. See [Download Team Presence to Excel](/leave/how-to/hr/download-presence/).

## Why did the Time Off app open on a different page?

**Time Off** opens on **Home**: **My Team** if people report to you, otherwise **My Leave**. Switch between them with the buttons at the top. See [Check my leave and request time off](/leave/how-to/everyone/my-leave/).

## Why can't I request more than 14 days of paternity leave?

Some leave types limit how long one request can be: at Tritel, Paternity leave is at most 14 days and Maternity leave at most 90. A longer request stops with, for example, *Paternity Leave can be requested for at most 14 days at a time; this request is 15 days.* Shorten the dates or split the leave into two requests, and talk to HR if you need more. See [How staff get leave: allocations](/leave/concepts/allocations/).

## Someone new has no annual leave. What do I do?

Ask a Time Off officer to open **Time Off › Manage › Leave Coverage**, find the person's **Missing** cell and allocate it. The allocation starts on their join date and is approved straight away. If their cell says **No join date**, their contract (with its start date) must be added first. See [Find and fill allocation gaps with Leave Coverage](/leave/how-to/hr/leave-coverage/).

## How do I give someone extra days, or take some off?

A Time Off officer opens the person's cell in **Leave Coverage** and uses **Adjust balance** with the days and a reason. The reason is kept with the balance. See [Adjust a leave balance](/leave/how-to/hr/adjust-a-balance/).
