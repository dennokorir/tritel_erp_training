Two kinds of warning help managers plan leave. **Team Presence** flags **busy days**, when too many of the people shown are away at once. **My Team** flags people whose balance is **Low**, or who are **Not taking leave** late in the year. Both use company settings that HR can change.

## Busy days

A day is **busy** when the number of people **away** reaches a threshold: the share of the people shown set in the settings (**20%** by default), rounded up, and **never fewer than 2**.

<Illustration name="leave/calc-busy-day" caption="12 people shown at 20%: a day is busy from 3 people away." />

- **Away** means approved time off, including colleagues you see only as **Away**. Requests not yet approved don't count, and a half day counts as one person away.
- **People shown** means the rows on screen after your filters. Filtering to one department, or ticking **Only people away**, changes the threshold.
- A public holiday is never flagged busy.
- Busy days show in red in the **Away each day** row of the grid, and as a red top edge on the calendar. The summary line counts them: *2 busy days*.

## Low and Not taking leave

My Team flags each person on their main leave type (the one that builds up monthly, usually annual leave, else the largest allocation):

| Flag | When |
|---|---|
| **No allocation** | They have no allocation for any leave type that needs one. |
| **Low** | **Balance** is **3 days** or fewer. |
| **Not taking leave** | From **October** on, **60%** or more of what was allocated this year is still in **Balance**. |
| **OK** | None of the above. |

**Balance** is Allocated − Taken − Requested: what can still be requested. See [Balances: allocated, taken, requested](/leave/concepts/balances/).

<Illustration name="leave/calc-team-flags" caption="14 of 18 days still in balance in November: Not taking leave." />

### Edge cases

- **Low is checked first.** In November, someone with 2 days left is **Low**, not **Not taking leave**, even if 2 days is a large share of a small allocation.
- **Before October**, nobody is flagged **Not taking leave**, however much is left.
- **Allocated** counts everything allocated so far this year, including days carried over or granted, so a large carry-over makes **Not taking leave** more likely.
- **Requested** days count as used: a person with a big request waiting won't be flagged **Not taking leave**.
- Sick leave never decides a flag; it is shown separately as **Sick left**.

## The settings

**Time Off › Configuration › Approvals and Reminders** (Time Off administrators):

- **Team Presence: busy when this % of the people shown are away**: 20 by default (1 to 100).
- **My Team: flag as low at or below (days balance)**: 3 by default.
- **My Team: flag "not taking leave" at (% of allocation in balance)**: 60 by default.
- **From month (1–12)**: 10 (October) by default.

See [Set reminders, escalation and flags](/leave/how-to/hr/settings/). The line under the **Team balances** table always shows the values in use.
