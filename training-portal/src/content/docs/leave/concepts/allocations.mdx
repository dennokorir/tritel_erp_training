Before someone can take a leave type that needs an allocation, they must have been given days of it: an **allocation**. An allocation is a record in the Time Off app that gives one person days of one leave type for a period. Their balance comes from it (see [Balances: allocated, taken, requested](/leave/concepts/balances/)).

Each leave type that needs an allocation says **How staff get it**. That setting tells HR, and the **Leave Coverage** screen, whether everyone should have the leave type or only the people who ask for it.

| How staff get it | What it means | Allocation that staff get |
|---|---|---|
| **Earns monthly (accrual plan)** | Everyone should have it. It builds up over time on an accrual plan (a **Monthly Earning Plan**). | One allocation per person with no end date, starting when they joined. It keeps earning from year to year and carries over as the plan allows. |
| **Yearly grant** | Everyone should have it. A fixed number of days for each calendar year. | One allocation per person per year, of **Days each year**, running to 31 December. |
| **On request** | Nobody is expected to have it until it's needed. | Allocated by HR when someone needs it. |

## Tritel's set-up

This is how the leave types are set up at Tritel today. Time Off administrators can change it under **Time Off › Configuration › Leave Types** (see [Set how staff get each leave type](/leave/how-to/hr/set-up-leave-types/)).

| Leave type | How staff get it |
|---|---|
| Annual Leave | **Earns monthly**, on its accrual plan. Allocations have no end date. |
| Sick Off | **Yearly grant** of 30 days. |
| Maternity and Paternity leave | Need no allocation, but one request is at most 90 days (Maternity) or 14 days (Paternity). |
| Overtime | **On request**. |

There is no Public Holiday leave type: public holidays come from **Time Off › Configuration › Public Holidays**.

## Who counts as staff

For allocations, staff are employees who:

- are active (not archived),
- have a **join date** on or before 31 December of the year you're looking at, and
- are not hidden from the Time Off views.

The join date is the **first contract date** on the employee record: the start date of their first contract. If there was a break of more than a few days between two contracts, it is the start of the contract after the break.

People with no join date (no contract yet, and service accounts) can't be allocated automatically. **Leave Coverage** lists them apart, under **No join date**.

## When an allocation starts

An allocation for a year starts on **1 January**, or on the person's **join date** in the year they join.

## Kind of allocation

Allocation lists (**Time Off › My Leave › My Allocations** and **Time Off › Manage › All Allocations**) have a **Kind** column:

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Earns monthly** | Builds up on an accrual plan. |
| **Yearly grant** | A yearly-grant leave type, from a date in a year to 31 December of the same year. |
| **Granted** | A set number of days given once, for example overtime, or a grant with other dates. |
| **Group grant** | The record HR made for several people at once (a department or the whole company). Each person gets their own copy, which is the one that counts. |

Allocations that have ended are greyed out.

## The figures

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Allocated** | Days given so far: what has been earned, carried over or granted. |
| **Taken** | Approved leave of this type, including approved days still to come. |
| **Requested** | Leave waiting for approval. Shown in Leave Coverage. |
| **Left** | In Leave Coverage: **Allocated − Taken − Requested**, what can still be requested. In the allocation lists: **Allocated − Taken** for that one allocation. |

Leave Coverage shows **Figures as at** a date: today when you look at this year, 31 December for a past year, and 1 January for a future year.

## Most days in one request

Any leave type can limit how long one request may be with **Most days in one request** (0 means no limit). It's how leave that needs no allocation, such as Maternity and Paternity, is kept in bounds. A longer request stops with, for example, *Paternity Leave can be requested for at most 14 days at a time; this request is 15 days.*

:::note[Good to know]
- A **Yearly grant** gives the full **Days each year**, even to someone who joins late in the year. Employee B, who joined in June, gets all 30 days of Sick Off for that year. Take days off with [Adjust a leave balance](/leave/how-to/hr/adjust-a-balance/) if that isn't right.
- The limit on one request is checked when a request is made or its dates change, not when it is approved. A request made before the limit was set can still be approved.
- **Left** in the allocation lists doesn't subtract requests still waiting for approval, so it can be higher than the figure in Leave Coverage or on **My Leave**.
:::
