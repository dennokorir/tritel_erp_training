Every line on the **Costs** tab of a baseline is a cost item. The system works out each item's **Total (before VAT)** in KES, and those totals make up the budget.

## The rule

For an ordinary item:

1. **Qty** × **Unit cost**, in the item's **Currency**.
2. If **Incl. VAT** is ticked, VAT is taken out: divide by 1 + **VAT %** ÷ 100. **VAT %** starts at the **VAT rate (%)** in Delivery Settings (16 by default).
3. Convert to KES. A KES item is not converted. A foreign-currency item uses its **Fixed rate** if it has one, and otherwise today's rate.
4. Round to the cent.

For a contingency item (a cost type in the contingency category):

- With a **% of other costs**, its amount is that percentage of the total of every item that is not contingency. The percentage wins if you also typed a **Unit cost**.
- Without a percentage, it is priced like an ordinary item from its **Unit cost**, in KES.

The **Budget total** is the sum of all items, contingency included. **Contingency** on the baseline is the sum of the contingency items.

## The contingency cap

When you click **Confirm for Approval**, contingency may be at most the **Contingency cap (%)** of the **Budget total** (5% by default). Because the budget total already includes the contingency, the most you can enter with a 5% cap is about 5.26% of the other costs.

<Illustration name="projects/calc-contingency-cap" caption="At 6% of other costs, contingency is 5.7% of the budget and confirmation stops." />

## When the exchange rate is fixed

- While the baseline is a draft or waiting for approval, foreign-currency items use the rate on the day the item was last worked out. The **Costs** tab says *Foreign-currency amounts use today's rate until finance approves the budget.*
- When finance clicks **Approve Budget**, each foreign-currency item gets a **Fixed rate** at that day's rate, and the totals are worked out again. If the baseline does not need finance (a revision with no budget change), the rate is fixed when the last approval is given.
- A revision keeps the fixed rate of items you don't touch. Changing an item's **Qty**, **Unit cost**, **Currency**, **Incl. VAT** or **VAT %** clears its fixed rate, so it goes back to today's rate until the revision's budget is approved.

## The two checks under the cost list

- **Quoted (%)**: the share of the non-contingency total on items marked **Quoted** rather than **Estimate**.
- **Foreign currency (%)**: the share of the non-contingency total on items in a currency other than KES.

## Edge cases

- **Rounding.** 10 × 5,000 including 16% VAT is 50,000 ÷ 1.16 = 43,103.45. The Budget total adds the rounded amounts.
- **Incl. VAT with VAT % at 0.** Nothing is taken out.
- **Zero quantity or unit cost.** Confirmation stops with *"Travel and site costs" needs a quantity and a unit cost.* Starter items arrive with a unit cost of 0 for you to price; delete the ones that don't apply.
- **No payment month.** Every item except contingency needs **Pay from**: *"Switches" needs the month it will be paid.*
- **Contingency with neither a percentage nor a unit cost** stops confirmation: *Give the contingency a percentage or an amount.*
- **Negative values** are refused: *Quantities, costs and percentages cannot be negative (Switches).*
- **A budget of 0** stops confirmation: *The budget total must be above zero.*

:::note[Good to know]
The budget finance approves can differ slightly from what you confirmed. Foreign-currency items are worked out again at the rate on the day finance approves, so if the shilling moved between your confirmation and their approval, the **Budget total** moves with it.

Contingency at a percentage is worked out in KES from the other items. It has no currency of its own and never needs a rate.
:::
