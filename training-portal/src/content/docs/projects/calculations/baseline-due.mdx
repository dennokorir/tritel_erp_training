Every delivery project should have an approved baseline soon after the tender is won. The **Baseline due** date on the **Delivery** tab says by when.

## The rule

- **Baseline due** = the tender's **Award date** + **Baseline due after award (days)** in Delivery Settings (14 by default).
- If the tender has no award date, the count starts from the day the project was created.
- Only delivery projects have a due date.

Each day the system checks active delivery projects whose **Baseline** is **No baseline** or **Baseline in draft**. If the due date has passed (the day after it at the earliest), the project manager and every Head of Delivery get a to-do: *Delivery baseline overdue*. It is marked done when a baseline is confirmed or approved.

## The project's Baseline status

| Baseline | When |
|---|---|
| **No baseline** | No version yet, or every version is withdrawn |
| **Baseline in draft** | No approved version, and one is a draft |
| **Waiting for approval** | No approved version, and one is waiting for approval |
| **Approved** | An approved version, and nothing in progress |
| **Revision pending** | An approved version, and a revision in draft or waiting |

Withdrawn and superseded versions are ignored. In **Delivery Projects** the rows with **No baseline** are shown in red.

## Where it shows up

- **My Actions › Baselines to draft** (project managers): projects with no baseline and first drafts not yet confirmed, marked overdue after the due date.
- **My Actions › Baselines overdue on your team** (Head of Delivery): other project managers' projects past their due date with no baseline or only a draft, with **Remind** (once a day per project).
- The project search filters **Needs baseline** (no baseline or a draft) and **Baseline waiting for approval** (waiting or revision pending).

## Edge cases

- **Waiting counts as on its way.** A project whose baseline is waiting for approval is not flagged, even after the due date.
- **Changing the setting** moves the due date of every project straight away, because it is worked out each time.

:::note[Good to know]
The *Delivery baseline overdue* to-do is sent only once per project. If the baseline is later returned to draft, or withdrawn, the project shows as overdue in My Actions but no new to-do is sent. The Head of Delivery can use **Remind** from My Actions instead.
:::
