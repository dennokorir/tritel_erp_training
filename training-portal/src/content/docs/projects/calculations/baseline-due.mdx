Every delivery project should have an approved Delivery Budget soon after the tender is won. The **Budget due** date on the **Delivery** tab says by when.

## The rule

- **Budget due** = the tender's **Award date** + **Budget due after award (days)** in Delivery Settings (14 by default).
- If the tender has no award date, the count starts from the day the project was created.
- Only delivery projects have a due date.

Each day the system checks active delivery projects whose **Budget status** is **No budget** or **Budget in draft**. If the due date has passed (the day after it at the earliest), the project manager and every Head of Delivery get a to-do: *Delivery Budget overdue*. It is marked done when a budget is submitted for approval or approved.

## The project's Budget status

| Budget status | When |
|---|---|
| **No budget** | No version yet, or every version is withdrawn |
| **Budget in draft** | No approved version, and one is a draft |
| **Waiting for approval** | No approved version, and one is waiting for approval |
| **Approved** | An approved version, and nothing in progress |
| **Revision pending** | An approved version, and a revision in draft or waiting |

Withdrawn and superseded versions are ignored. In the **My Delivery Projects** and **Delivery Projects** lists, the rows with **No budget** are shown in red.

## Where it shows up

- **My Actions › Budgets to draft** (project managers): projects with no budget and first drafts not yet submitted, marked overdue after the due date.
- **My Actions › Budgets overdue on your team** (Head of Delivery): other project managers' projects past their due date with no budget or only a draft, with **Remind** (once a day per project).
- The project search filters **Needs a budget** (no budget or a draft) and **Budget waiting for approval** (waiting or revision pending).

## Edge cases

- **Waiting counts as on its way.** A project whose budget is waiting for approval is not flagged, even after the due date.
- **Changing the setting** moves the due date of every project straight away, because it is worked out each time.

:::note[Good to know]
The *Delivery Budget overdue* to-do is sent only once per project. If the budget is later sent back with **Return for Changes**, or withdrawn, the project shows as overdue in My Actions but no new to-do is sent. The Head of Delivery can use **Remind** from My Actions instead.
:::
