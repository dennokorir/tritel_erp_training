When finance clicks **Close Project**, the system works out the final cost and the project's result, and freezes them. The Delivery KPIs in Accountability read these frozen results.

## The close figures

The **Close Project** window shows, in KES before VAT:

- **Spent**: everything posted to the project's cost account on expense accounts, the same as Spent in [budget tracking](/projects/calculations/tracking-figures/).
- **Open orders**: confirmed purchase order lines on the cost account not yet billed, the same as Committed.
- **Proposed final cost** = Spent + Open orders.
- **Adjustment**: optional, plus or minus, for example a known late bill or a cost that belongs to another project. **Why** is then required: *Explain the adjustment to the final cost.*
- **Final cost** = Proposed final cost + Adjustment.
- **Approved budget**: the **Budget total** of the latest approved baseline, contingency included.

## The result

The project is **scored** only if it has an approved baseline and a **Confirmed** handover. Then:

- deadline = **Approved completion date** + **Approved client delay (days)**;
- **Days late** = days from the deadline to the handover date, or 0 if handed over by the deadline;
- **On time** if Days late is not more than **Completion date grace (days)** (7 by default);
- **On budget** if the Final cost is not more than the Approved budget, to the cent. There is no tolerance.

| Result | When |
|---|---|
| **On time and on budget** | Both |
| **Late** | Over the grace, within budget |
| **Over budget** | On time, above the budget |
| **Late and over budget** | Neither |
| **Not scored** | No approved baseline, or no confirmed handover |

<Illustration name="projects/calc-close-result" caption="Handed over 5 days after the deadline, within the 7-day grace, and 30,000 under budget." />

The window previews the result before you confirm, for example *Handover 15 Apr 2027 against approved 31 Mar 2027 + 10 days client delay: 5 days late (grace 7).*

Closing stores everything with the project: the budget, baseline version, completion date, handover date, delay days and grace **at close**. Changing Delivery Settings later does not change a closed project's result. **Reopen** clears the figures (the old ones are posted in the chatter) and closing again works them out afresh.

## Costs after close

**Costs after close** = (Spent + Open orders now) − (Spent at close + Open orders at close). It shows on the **Handover & close** tab when the difference is 0.50 or more either way, for example a credit note gives a negative figure. The result stays frozen. Each night, finance gets a *Costs after close* to-do when the figure has changed since the last notice, until someone clicks **Mark reviewed** (it comes back only if the costs change again) or reopens the project.

## The Delivery KPIs

Accountability has three delivery metrics. Each one is worked out for an employee and a review window:

| Metric | Counts a project when |
|---|---|
| **Delivery success rate (on time and on budget)** | its result is **On time and on budget** |
| **Delivery on-time completion rate** | it was **On time** |
| **Delivery on-budget rate** | it was **On budget** |

1. Take the delivery projects where the employee is the **Project Manager** and whose **Confirmed** handover is dated inside the window.
2. Count only the ones that are closed and scored. Projects handed over but not yet closed, and closed projects that are **Not scored**, are listed but not counted.
3. If fewer than 3 are counted, add the projects handed over in the window of the same length just before.
4. Rate = projects meeting the rule ÷ projects counted × 100, to one decimal place.

If no project is counted, there is no result for that window. Automatic reviews wait 30 days after the window ends, so finance has time to close projects handed over at the end of it.

<Illustration name="projects/calc-delivery-kpi" caption="Only two of Employee A's projects are counted in Q2, so Q1 is added: 2 of 3 is 66.7%." />

See [How metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/) for how a metric becomes a target score.

:::note[Good to know]
**Days late** is recorded before the grace is applied. A project can show *5* in **Days late** and still be **On time**, because 5 is within the 7-day grace. Only more than 7 days late makes it **Late**.

The budget check uses the latest approved baseline at the time of closing, including any approved revision and the contingency.

A project counts in the KPI window of its handover date, not the date it was closed. A project handed over in June but closed in August counts in June's window once it is closed.
:::
