A revision is a new version of an approved Delivery Budget (budget v2, v3 and so on), started with **Request Revision**. The **Change** box on the revision shows how far it moves the budget and the completion date, and those figures decide who has to approve it.

## What the Change box shows

- **Budget vs previous** = this version's **Budget total** − the total of the version it was copied from.
- **Days vs previous** = days between the two **Completion date**s.
- **Budget vs original (%)** = (this total − the v1 total) ÷ the v1 total × 100.
- **Days vs original** = days between v1's completion date and this one.

The **Changes** tab lists each cost item as **Added**, **Changed** (with what changed, for example *qty 10.0 → 12.0* or *renamed from "Switches"*) or **Removed**, with the amount in both versions and the difference.

## Who must approve

| Approver | Needed when |
|---|---|
| Head of Delivery (**Approve Dates**) | Always: every original and every revision |
| Finance (**Approve Budget**) | The original, and a revision whose budget changed |
| MD (**Approve as MD**) | A revision whose **Budget vs original (%)** is above **MD approval above budget increase (%)** (10 by default), or whose **Days vs original** is above **MD approval above date extension (days)** (30 by default) |

"The budget changed" means a category total changed, or a cost item was added, removed or had its **What**, **Type**, **Qty**, **Unit cost**, **Currency**, **Incl. VAT**, **VAT %** or **% of other costs** changed. The approvals can come in any order. The revision becomes **Approved** when the last one needed is in.

## Edge cases

- **Exactly on the limit does not need the MD.** A revision at 1,155,000 against a v1 of 1,050,000 is exactly 10.0% above, and 30 days later is exactly 30: neither is *above* the limit.
- **Only increases count.** A smaller budget or an earlier completion date never needs the MD.
- **Measured from v1.** Three revisions of +5% each are 15.8% above the original by the third, which needs the MD.
- **No MD named.** Confirming stops with *This revision needs the MD, but no MD is set in Delivery Settings.*
- **No reason.** *Explain the reason for this revision.*
- **Nothing changed.** *This revision changes nothing compared with v2.* A change counts if the budget changed (as above), the completion date moved, or a milestone was added, removed, renamed or moved.
- **A date-only revision** needs the Head of Delivery but not finance (and the MD only if it goes more than 30 days past v1).

:::note[Good to know]
Changing only the **Pay from** or **Pay until** months, the **Basis**, the **Reference** or the quote file of items is not counted as a change. A revision that only re-phases the spend stops with *This revision changes nothing compared with v2.* To re-phase, the revision also has to change something that counts.

Renaming a cost item counts as a budget change, so it brings finance in even though no amount moved.
:::
