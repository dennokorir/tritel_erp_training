Once the Delivery Budget is approved, the project shows how much of each budget line is **committed** (ordered but not yet billed) and **spent** (posted costs), and how much is **left**. Check it before raising new orders and at every project review.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-track-budget" caption="Budget tracking on a delivery project. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › Delivery › Projects › My Delivery Projects**. It opens on your own projects; clear the filter to see every delivery project. The list shows each project's **Approved delivery budget**, **Committed**, **Spent**, **Left** and **Budget status** (**On track**, **Fully committed** or **Over plan**). Under **Filters**, **Over plan**, **Fully committed**, **On track** and **No approved budget** pick out projects by that status. Open the project.
   - Delivery Finance and the Head of Delivery use **Project › Delivery › Projects › Delivery Projects** instead. Delivery Finance also has **Accounting › Project Budgets › Tracking › Budget vs Actual**, which opens on the projects that are **Over plan** or **Fully committed**.
2. Read the smart buttons at the top: **Delivery budget** (the approved total), **Spent so far** (with the share of the budget, for example *Spent 43%*), **Committed**, and **Budget left**. **Budget left** is green when on track, amber when fully committed and red when over plan. Click it to see the table in a pop-up.
3. On the **Delivery** tab, read the **Budget tracking** table. Each **Budget line** shows:
   - **Planned**: the approved budget for the line.
   - **Expected by now**: the spend plan up to this month.
   - **Committed**: confirmed purchase orders not yet billed, before VAT.
   - **Spent**: costs posted on the project's cost account, before VAT.
   - **Left**: planned minus committed minus spent. Red when negative.
   - **Forecast at completion**: planned, or committed plus spent if that is higher.
   - **Status**: **On track**, **Fully committed** or **Over plan**.
4. Click **Refresh** next to **Figures as of** to bring the figures up to date. They also refresh every night and when a Delivery Budget is approved.
5. To see what is behind a figure, click **Spent so far** (the cost lines) or **Committed** (the open order lines). Buyers see the purchase orders themselves; finance sees the accounting entries.

If a line is **Over plan**, talk to the buyer before more orders go out, and [request a revision](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/request-a-revision/) if the plan has really changed.

## What happens next

- **When an order would take a line over plan**, the buyer sees on the purchase order *Over the delivery plan. You can still confirm; the project manager will be told.*, with the amounts, for example *Data Centre Upgrade · Direct project costs: this order is 100,000; 80,000 was left in the plan (over by 20,000).* If they confirm it, you get a message on the Delivery Budget headed *Order over the delivery plan: P00012*. The order is not blocked. The order's **Budget Available** smart button shows what is left on the project, in red in that case.
- **When a project is over plan**, it appears in **Project › My Actions** under **Projects over their approved budget** for its project manager and for the Head of Delivery.
- **Orders on the tender that don't carry the project's cost account** show on the purchase order as *Not tagged to the project.* They count as neither committed nor spent. The Head of Delivery or finance tags them with **Tag tender orders**. See [Tag orders to their project](/projects/how-to/approvers/tag-orders-to-a-project/).
- **Every order also has a Budget status** (for example **Within budget** or **Over budget**), checked against the project's accounting budget month by month. It can disagree with the delivery plan above. See [Check a purchase order against the budget](/projects/how-to/approvers/check-an-order-against-the-budget/).
- **To see every order line counting against the budget**, open **Project › Delivery › Projects › Budget Commitments**. See [Budget Commitments](/projects/reports/budget-commitments/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Only the Head of Delivery or finance can tag orders.* | You tried to tag tender orders without the right role. | Ask the Head of Delivery or Delivery Finance to tag them. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Rows are budget lines in the accounts, not budget categories. Categories that share the same ledger accounts share one row; their names are listed under the row's name. **Not split yet** collects costs on accounts outside every line.
- The contingency line never gets actual costs, because nobody posts to its reserve account. Its money stays in **Left**, so a project rarely shows **Fully committed** overall.
- The table on the **Delivery** tab is worked out when you open the project. The smart buttons and the list use the figures from the last refresh, shown in **Figures as of**. If they disagree, click **Refresh**.
- A purchase order counts only from the moment it is confirmed, and only for the lines that carry the project's cost account.
- Committed amounts are placed on a budget line by the product on the order line: the cost type whose product categories include that product decides the category. A service product with no match goes with subcontractors, any other product with equipment. If an order sits on an unexpected line, ask the Head of Delivery to check the product categories on the cost types.
:::
