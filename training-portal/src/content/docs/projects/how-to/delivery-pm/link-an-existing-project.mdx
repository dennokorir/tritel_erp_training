Some projects are created in Odoo before the tender is won, or outside the delivery flow. Until they are linked to a won tender they are not delivery projects: they have no baseline and the delivery KPIs don't measure them. Linking them gives them everything a newly opened delivery project gets.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-link-project" caption="Linking an existing project to its won tender. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Find the project.
   - Head of Delivery: open **Project › Delivery › Projects to Link**. It lists every project that is not linked to a tender. My Actions also shows the count under **Projects not linked to a won tender**.
   - Project managers: open **Project › Projects**, open **Filters** and choose **Not linked to a tender**. On the project's card, click **Open project**.
2. On the project, open the **Delivery** tab. The blue notice says *This project is not linked to a won tender, so it is not measured by the delivery KPIs.* Click **Link to Won Tender**.
3. In the **Link to Won Tender** window, **Existing project** is already filled in. Choose the **Won tender**. Only won tenders are listed. Check **Client** and **Award Date**.
4. Check the **Project manager** and **Start date**. They are taken from the project.
5. Check **Cost account**.
   - If the project already has one, it is kept: the line underneath says *The project already has this cost account; it is kept.*
   - If not, choose finance's existing account for the job, or leave it empty to create a new one.
6. Click **Open Delivery Project**.

## What happens next

- The project becomes a delivery project linked to the tender. The **Delivery** tab now shows the **Tender** details (**Tender**, **Award date**, **Contract value**, **Baseline due**) and the **Approved baseline** status.
- The client is filled in if the project had none. Missing delivery stages and standard milestones are added. Stages and milestones the project already had are kept.
- If the project has no baseline yet, **Baseline v1** is started in **Draft** with the starter items. Milestones the project already had are copied into it with their deadlines, and the project's end date becomes the baseline's **Completion date**.
- **Baseline due** counts from the tender's award date, so a project linked late may already be overdue. It then shows in your **Project › My Actions** under **Baselines to draft**.

Next: [build the baseline](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/build-a-baseline/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *This project is already linked to tender Customer B data centre.* | The project is linked to a different tender. | Check you have the right project and tender. A project belongs to one tender. |
| *Cost account PRJ Customer B data centre is already used by project PRJ Customer B phase 1.* | The account you chose belongs to another project. | Choose the right account with finance, or leave it empty. |
| *Only a won tender can open a delivery project.* | The tender is not **Won**. | Wait until the tender is marked **Won**. |
| *Only delivery project managers can open delivery projects.* | Your user is not a Delivery Project Manager. | Ask a delivery project manager or the Head of Delivery to link it. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Projects to Link** lists every project that is not linked, including internal projects that will never have a tender. Leave those alone.
- There is no button to undo a link. Check the tender before you click **Open Delivery Project**.
:::
