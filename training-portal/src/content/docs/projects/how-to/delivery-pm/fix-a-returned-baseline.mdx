An approver who is not happy with a baseline returns it with a note. It goes back to **Draft** so you can change it. Fix what they asked for and confirm it again.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-returned-baseline" caption="A returned baseline with the approver's note. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › My Actions**. The baseline is under **Baselines sent back to you**, with the note. Click it to open it. You can also open it from the *Delivery baseline returned* to-do.
2. Read the yellow banner. It starts **Returned:** and shows the approver's note.
3. Make the changes on the **Costs** and **Milestones** tabs, or change the **Completion date**. The baseline is in **Draft**, so everything can be edited again.
4. Click **Confirm for Approval**.

## What happens next

- The baseline is **Waiting for approval** again and the banner disappears.
- Your *Delivery baseline returned* to-do is marked done with *Confirmed again.*
- All the approvers get new to-dos. Approvals given before the return were cleared, so the Head of Delivery and finance both approve again, even if one of them had already approved.
- If the project no longer needs this baseline, click **Withdraw** instead. The to-do is marked done with *Baseline withdrawn.*

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Baseline v1 is Waiting for approval and can no longer be edited. Return it to draft or request a revision.* | You tried to edit a baseline that hasn't been returned. | Ask an approver to click **Return to Draft** with a note. |
| *"Freight, clearing and delivery to site" needs a quantity and a unit cost.* | A line you changed now has no price. | Price it or delete it. |
| *Milestones cannot fall after the completion date: Testing and commissioning complete.* | The date changes left a milestone after the completion date. | Move the milestone or the completion date. |

All the other checks from [Send a baseline for approval](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/send-for-approval/) run again when you confirm.

:::note[Good to know]
Only an approver who has not yet given their approval sees **Return to Draft**. If you spot a mistake in a baseline that is waiting, ask one of the approvers who still has to approve it to return it to you with a note.
:::
