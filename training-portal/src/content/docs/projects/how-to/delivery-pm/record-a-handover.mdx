The **handover** is the date the client took over the work. You record it and the Head of Delivery confirms it. The confirmed handover date is compared with the approved completion date to decide whether the project was delivered on time, and it lets finance close the project.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-handover" caption="A new handover, ready to submit. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the project (**Project › Delivery › Projects › My Delivery Projects**) and click **Record Handover** at the top. You can also click **Record handover** in **Project › My Actions**, where the project appears under **Handovers to record** once its approved completion date has passed.
2. Check **Project**, and set the **Handover date**: the date the client took over the work, which is the date on the certificate if there is one. It starts as today.
3. Attach the signed certificate in **Handover certificate**. It is optional, but it helps the Head of Delivery confirm it.
4. In **Note**, write what was handed over, who signed for the client and anything outstanding (the snag list).
5. Save, then click **Submit for Approval**.

## What happens next

- The handover is **Waiting for confirmation** and can't be edited. Every Head of Delivery gets a *Confirm project handover* to-do. It shows in your My Actions under **Waiting on others**, where you can **Remind** them.
- **If the Head of Delivery confirms it**, it becomes **Confirmed**. The project's **Handover** smart button shows **Confirmed** and the **Handover & close** tab shows the **Handover date**. Delivery Finance gets a *Close delivery project* to-do.
- **If the Head of Delivery returns it**, it becomes **Returned** with a yellow banner showing their note. You get a *Handover returned* to-do, and it is listed in My Actions under **Handovers sent back to you**. Make the changes and click **Submit for Approval** again; the to-do is marked done.
- At close, the handover counts as on time if it is no more than 7 days (the grace set in Delivery Settings) after the approved completion date plus any approved client delay.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Data Centre Upgrade already has a handover waiting or confirmed.* | Another handover for the project is already submitted or confirmed. | Open **Handover** on the project. Ask the Head of Delivery to return the existing one if it is wrong. |
| *This handover is waiting for confirmation; it can no longer be edited.* | You tried to change a submitted handover. | Ask the Head of Delivery to return it. |
| *Only a draft or returned handover can be submitted.* | It has already been submitted or confirmed. | Refresh the page and check the status bar. |
| *Only draft or returned handovers can be deleted.* | You tried to delete a submitted or confirmed handover. | Ask the Head of Delivery to return it first. |
| *Only delivery project managers can record a handover.* | Your user is not a Delivery Project Manager. | Ask the project manager to record it. |
| *Data Centre Upgrade is closed. Finance can reopen it first.* | The project has been closed. | Ask finance to reopen it if the handover must change. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Days late** is counted before the grace. A handover 5 days after the deadline is recorded as 5 days late but still **On time**. See [Close figures and the Delivery KPI](/projects/calculations/close-and-kpi/).
- The Head of Delivery can also return a handover that is already confirmed.
- **Record Handover** disappears once a handover is waiting or confirmed. It also appears on projects that are not delivery projects; those are closed as **Not scored**.
:::
