The Delivery Budget's dates are the **Completion date** and a **Planned date** for each milestone. The Head of Delivery approves them. Once approved, the completion date is what your handover is measured against for the on-time KPI.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-baseline-milestones" caption="The completion date and the Milestones tab. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. On the draft Delivery Budget, set the **Completion date**. It may already be filled in from the project's end date.
2. Open the **Milestones** tab. The project's milestones are listed, including the standard ones added when the project was opened. They start without dates.
3. Give every milestone a **Planned date**. Each one must be on or before the completion date.
4. Click **Add a line** to add a milestone the project needs. Delete milestones that don't apply. The list sorts itself by date when you save.

## What happens next

- When you submit the budget, the Head of Delivery approves the dates.
- On approval, the project's end date becomes the **Completion date** and is locked. The project's milestones get the planned dates, and any milestone you added is created on the project.
- After the completion date has passed with no handover recorded, the project shows in **Project › My Actions** under **Handovers to record**.
- Milestones past their date that are not marked reached show under **Overdue milestones**, with a **Mark reached** button.
- To change the dates after approval, [request a revision](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/request-a-revision/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Set the completion date.* | You submitted the budget without a **Completion date**. | Set it, then submit again. |
| *Give every milestone a date: Installation complete, Handover.* | Some milestones have no **Planned date**. | Date them, or delete those that don't apply. |
| *Milestones cannot fall after the completion date: Handover.* | A milestone is dated after the completion date. | Move the milestone, or move the completion date. |
| *The completion date of Data Centre Upgrade comes from its approved budget. Request a revision to change it.* | You tried to change the end date on the project itself. | Request a revision of the Delivery Budget instead. |
| *Budget v1 is Waiting for approval and can no longer be edited. Return it to draft or request a revision.* | The budget has been submitted. | Ask an approver to return it if the dates are wrong. |

:::note[Good to know]
Deleting a milestone from a Delivery Budget does not delete it from the project. It simply gets no new date. If it no longer applies, ask a project administrator to remove it from the project.
:::
