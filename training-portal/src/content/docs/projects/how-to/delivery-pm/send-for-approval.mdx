When the costs, payment months, completion date and milestones are ready, you **submit** the Delivery Budget. It is then locked and waits for its approvals. When all of them are in, it becomes the approved plan the project is measured against.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-send-for-approval" caption="A draft Delivery Budget ready to submit, with the Approvals tab open. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Check the budget one last time: **Completion date**, the **Costs** tab, the spend plan on **Summary by category**, and the dates on **Milestones**.
2. Click **Submit for Approval**. If something is missing, a message tells you what to fix (see below).
3. The status moves to **Waiting for approval**. The budget can no longer be edited.
4. Open the **Approvals** tab to follow progress. Under **Submitted**, **Confirmed By** and **Confirmed On** show your submission. **Dates approved by** and **Budget approved by** fill in as the approvers act.

## Follow your submissions

- **Project › Delivery › Projects › My Submissions** lists every budget and revision you submitted that is still waiting for approval. It opens on the filter **My submissions, waiting**. When nothing is waiting, it says *Nothing you submitted is waiting for approval.*
- **Project › My Actions** shows the budget under **Waiting on others**, with who it is waiting on. Click **Remind** to send them a message (once a day).

## What happens next

- **To-dos are sent.** Every Head of Delivery gets *Approve delivery budget: dates*. Every Delivery Finance user gets *Approve delivery budget: budget*. If the costs are over the expected revenue, the to-do says by how much.
- **Approvals come in any order.** The Head of Delivery clicks **Approve Dates**; finance clicks **Approve Budget**. When finance approves, the exchange rates of foreign-currency items are fixed.
- **When the last approval is in**, the budget becomes **Approved** and locked. The project's end date and milestones take its dates, the accounting budget is created on the project's cost account, and budget tracking starts. You get a message (not a to-do), and a summary is posted on the project.
- **If an approver returns it**, it goes back to **Draft** with a note for you. See [Fix a Delivery Budget that was sent back](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/fix-a-returned-baseline/).
- An open *Delivery Budget overdue* to-do on the project is marked done when you submit.
- To stop the approval altogether, click **Withdraw** and confirm *Withdraw this budget? It stays on record but no longer counts.*

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Set the completion date.* | **Completion date** is empty. | Set it. |
| *List the costs: what the project will pay for.* | No cost items. | Add the costs on the **Costs** tab. |
| *"Travel and site costs" needs a quantity and a unit cost.* | An item, often a starter item, has no price. | Price it or delete it. |
| *"48-port PoE switches" needs the month it will be paid.* | **Pay from** is empty. | Set the month. |
| *Give the contingency a percentage or an amount.* | The contingency line is blank. | Type a percentage or delete the line. |
| *The budget total must be above zero.* | Everything adds up to 0. | Check quantities and unit costs. |
| *Give every milestone a date: Handover.* | A milestone has no **Planned date**. | Date it or delete it. |
| *Milestones cannot fall after the completion date: Handover.* | A milestone is later than the completion date. | Move the milestone or the completion date. |
| *Contingency is 5.7% of the budget; the limit is 5.0%.* | Contingency is above the allowed share. | Lower it. |
| *These costs are paid after the completion date plus 180 days (27 Dec 2026): Retention release.* | A payment month is too late. | Bring it forward. |
| *Only a draft budget can be confirmed.* | It is already submitted or approved. | Refresh the page and check the status bar. |
| *Only delivery project managers can confirm a budget.* | You are not a Delivery Project Manager. | Ask the project manager to submit it. |
| *You already sent a reminder about this today.* | You clicked **Remind** twice in one day. | Try again tomorrow. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The **Approvals** tab shows **Budget approved by** only when finance's approval is needed. For an original budget it always is.
- A red *Costs are over the expected revenue* banner does not stop you submitting. The approvers decide.
- The messages still say "confirm" (for example *Only a draft budget can be confirmed.*): it means the same as **Submit for Approval**.
:::
