Delivery Budgets are keyed by hand from estimates and quotes. There is no button that pulls a whole purchase order or sales order into a budget. When a supplier has quoted for, or you have ordered, a whole package, key it as one cost item for each type of cost in the order.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-whole-order" caption="One supplier order keyed as two cost items, one per type. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the draft Delivery Budget (from **Project › Delivery › Projects › My Delivery Projects**, open the project and click the **Budget** smart button) and go to the **Costs** tab.
2. Click **Add a line** for the first part of the order:
   - **What**: the supplier and what the order covers, for example *Supplier A order: switches and optics*.
   - **Type**: the type that fits this part, for example **Equipment we supply**.
   - **Qty**: 1, and **Unit**: lot.
   - **Currency**: the order's currency.
   - **Unit cost**: the order amount for this part, before VAT. If you only have the VAT-inclusive amount, type that and tick **Incl. VAT**.
   - **Pay from** (and **Pay until** if it is paid in stages): when the supplier will be paid.
   - **Basis**: **Quoted**. **Reference**: the order or quote number. Attach the PDF in **Quote file**.
3. If the order mixes types (for example equipment and installation), add one line per type, so that each budget category gets its share.
4. Check **Total (before VAT)** on each line and the total under the table.
5. For a foreign-currency order, the amount is converted at today's rate until finance approves the budget.
6. On **Summary by category**, **Quote files attached** shows how many quoted lines have a file, for example *2 of 2 quoted lines*.

For a **sales order**, don't key the selling prices. Key what Tritel will buy or pay to deliver it: equipment, subcontractors, travel and so on.

## What happens next

- The items count in the budget like any other cost item, and the approvers see the reference and the quote.
- After approval, what the supplier's order actually commits is tracked separately: a confirmed purchase order counts as **Committed** only when its lines carry the project's cost account. Orders raised on the project's tender get it automatically when the tender has one delivery project. See [Track the budget](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/track-the-budget/). The order's own **Budget status** shows whether it fits the project's accounting budget: see [Check a purchase order against the budget](/projects/how-to/approvers/check-an-order-against-the-budget/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *"Supplier A order: installation" needs a quantity and a unit cost.* | The quantity or the amount is 0. | Use 1 lot and the order amount. |
| *"Pay until" is before "Pay from" on Supplier A order: installation.* | The months are the wrong way round. | Swap them, or clear **Pay until** if it is paid in one month. |
| *Budget v1 is Approved; its budget and milestones can no longer be edited.* | The budget is approved. | [Request a revision](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/request-a-revision/) to add the order. |

:::note[Good to know]
Delivery Budgets drafted in the first months of the module may contain items that were imported from tender orders at the time. They keep the order they came from as read-only history, and revisions copy them like any other item. New orders can't be imported; key them as shown above.
:::
