The **baseline** is the plan the project is measured against: what it will cost, when the money goes out, and when it will be finished. You build the cost part on the **Costs** tab, one **cost item** per thing the project pays for. The system works out the totals by budget category, so you never pick ledger accounts yourself.

<Illustration name="projects/pm-baseline-costs" caption="A draft baseline with the starter items and two new cost items. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the draft baseline. From **Project › Delivery › Delivery Projects**, open the project and click the **Delivery baseline** smart button (it shows **Draft v1**), or click **Open Baseline in Progress** on the **Delivery** tab. If the button shows **No baseline**, click **Draft Baseline** on the **Delivery** tab instead.
2. Deal with the starter items at the top of the **Costs** tab, if there are any. A baseline started by **Open Delivery Project** or **Link to Won Tender** comes with them. Type a **Unit cost** for each one that applies, and delete the ones that don't (the bin icon at the end of the row). **Contingency** is already set to 5 in **% of other costs**. A baseline started with **Draft Baseline** or **Start baseline** has no starter items: add a **Contingency** line yourself if the project needs one.
3. Click **Add a line** for each thing the project pays for, and fill in:
   - **What**: a plain description, for example *48-port PoE switches*.
   - **Type**: the kind of cost, for example **Equipment we supply** or **Work by a subcontractor**. The type decides the budget category.
   - **Qty** and **Unit** (units, days, lot, metres).
   - **Currency**: KES unless the quote is in another currency, for example USD.
   - **Unit cost**: the price of one unit in that currency. If the price includes VAT, tick **Incl. VAT** and the VAT is taken out.
   - **Pay from** and **Pay until**: the months the money goes out. See [Plan when the money goes out](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/phase-the-spend/).
   - **Basis**: **Quoted** when you have a supplier quote, **Estimate** when you don't.
   - **Reference**: the quote or order number. Attach the quote in **Quote file** if you have it.
4. Check **Total (before VAT)** on each line and the total under the table. The line under the table shows **Quoted** (the share of costs backed by a quote) and **Foreign currency**.
5. Check the figures at the top: **Budget total** (contingency included), **Contingency**, and under **Expected revenue**, **Margin left** and **Margin left (%)**.
6. Open **Summary by category** to see the totals per budget category and the spend plan by month. Then set the dates on the **Milestones** tab and confirm the baseline for approval.

Your changes are saved as you go when you click **Save** (the cloud icon). The baseline stays in **Draft** until you confirm it.

## What happens next

- The **Summary by category** tab is rebuilt every time you change an item. This is what finance approves and what the Odoo budget is made from.
- Foreign-currency items are converted at today's rate. The line under the table says *Foreign-currency amounts use today's rate until finance approves the budget.* The rate is fixed when the budget is approved.
- If the costs are above the tender's expected revenue, a red banner says *Costs are over the expected revenue. You can still confirm; the approvers will see this.*
- Nobody else is notified while you work. When the baseline is ready, set the [completion date and milestones](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/set-dates-and-milestones/) and [send it for approval](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/send-for-approval/).

## Common mistakes

Most of these appear when you click **Confirm for Approval**.

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *"Travel and site costs" needs a quantity and a unit cost.* | A starter item (or any item) still has a 0 quantity or unit cost. | Price it, or delete the line if the project doesn't need it. |
| *"48-port PoE switches" needs the month it will be paid.* | **Pay from** is empty. | Set **Pay from** on that line. |
| *Give the contingency a percentage or an amount.* | The contingency line has neither a **% of other costs** nor a **Unit cost**. | Type a percentage, or delete the line if there is no contingency. |
| *Contingency is 5.7% of the budget; the limit is 5.0%.* | Contingency is above the share allowed in Delivery Settings (5% of the budget total by default). | Lower the contingency percentage or amount. |
| *List the costs: what the project will pay for.* | The **Costs** tab is empty. | Add the cost items. |
| *The budget total must be above zero.* | Every item adds up to 0. | Check quantities and unit costs. |
| *Quantities, costs and percentages cannot be negative (48-port PoE switches).* | A negative number was typed. | Enter positive figures. To show a saving, lower the cost instead. |
| *Baseline v1 is Waiting for approval; its budget and milestones can no longer be edited.* | The baseline has already been confirmed. | Ask an approver to return it, or wait and request a revision once it is approved. |

:::note[Good to know]
- All amounts are before VAT and in the company currency (KES), whatever the item's currency.
- **Margin left** compares the budget with the tender's expected revenue. If other delivery projects share the same tender, their budgets are taken off too, and **Other projects on this tender** shows how much.
- A blue banner *Odoo budget mapping is not finished.* is for finance. You can carry on building and confirming the baseline.
- Delivery Finance users can also change the items on a draft baseline. If figures change that you didn't change, check the baseline's chatter.
:::
