A **delivery baseline** is the plan a project is measured against: a completion date, milestone dates and a budget. The Head of Delivery approves the **dates** of every baseline. Finance approves the budget separately, and the MD also approves a large revision.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-approve-dates" caption="A baseline waiting for your dates approval. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › Delivery › Waiting for My Approval**. The list shows only the baselines waiting for you. Click the baseline. You can also open it from **Project › My Actions**, under **Baselines to approve: dates**, or from the to-do.
2. Check the **Completion date**. On a revision, the **Change** box also shows **Days vs previous** and **Days vs original**, and **Reason for revision** says why it changed.
3. Open the **Milestones** tab.
4. Check each milestone's **Planned date**. They must make sense in order, and none can fall after the completion date. On a revision, the **Changes** tab lists the cost items that were added, changed or removed.
5. If the dates are right, click **Approve Dates**.
6. If something needs fixing, open the **Approvals** tab, type what needs to change in **Note for the project manager**, then click **Return to Draft**.

From **My Actions** you can do the same without opening the baseline: click **Approve dates**, or click **Return**, type the note in the box that opens, and click **Return** again.

## What happens next

- **After Approve Dates:** your name goes in **Dates approved by** on the **Approvals** tab and your to-do is removed. If finance or the MD still has to approve, the baseline stays **Waiting for approval**.
- **When the last approval is in,** the baseline becomes **Approved** and is locked:
  - the project's completion date and milestone dates are set from the baseline;
  - foreign-currency rates are fixed and the Odoo budget is created on the project's cost account;
  - on a revision, the previous approved version becomes **Superseded** and its Odoo budget is cancelled;
  - the project manager gets a message (not a to-do) with a summary.
- **After Return to Draft:** the baseline goes back to **Draft** and every approval already given is cleared, including finance's. All approvers' to-dos are removed. The project manager gets a *Delivery baseline returned* to-do with your note, and sees it in a yellow **Returned** banner. They fix it and [confirm it again](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/fix-a-returned-baseline/), and everyone approves again.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Write a note for the project manager before returning the baseline.* | You clicked **Return to Draft** with an empty **Note for the project manager**. | Type the note on the **Approvals** tab first. |
| *You cannot give this approval, or it has already been given.* | Another Head of Delivery approved it first, or you are not in the Head of Delivery group. | Refresh the page and check **Dates approved by**. |
| *This baseline is not waiting for approval.* | It was returned, withdrawn or approved while you had it open. | Refresh and check the status bar. |
| *Only a baseline waiting for approval can be returned.* | Same as above: it is no longer waiting. | Refresh the page. |
| *Baseline v1 is Waiting for approval and can no longer be edited. Return it to draft or request a revision.* | You tried to change a date or milestone yourself. | Return it with a note saying what to change. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Any one Head of Delivery can approve. When one does, the item leaves the other Heads' lists.
- **Return to Draft** shows only while your own approval is still pending. Once you have approved, you no longer see it; finance or the MD can still return the baseline while their approval is pending.
- Returning clears approvals that others have already given. Agree with finance before you return a baseline they have approved.
:::
