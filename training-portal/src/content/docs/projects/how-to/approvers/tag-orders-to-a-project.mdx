A project's budget tracking only counts order lines and bills that carry the project's **cost account**. Buyers sometimes raise a purchase order on the tender without it. Those lines count as neither committed nor spent until you **tag** them.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-tag-orders" caption="Tagging a tender's untagged order lines. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the project from **Project › Delivery › Projects › Delivery Projects** and click the amber **Untagged tender order lines** button (it shows **Untagged lines** and a count). Other ways in:
   - **Project › My Actions**: the line *Tender order lines not tagged to their project*, then **Tag orders**, which lists the projects that have some;
   - the project's **Delivery** tab, next to **Budget tracking**: **Tag tender orders**;
   - the won tender's **Untagged order lines** button.
2. The **Tag Tender Orders** window opens. It names the cost account the lines will get, and lists every line on the tender's purchase orders that has no cost account. All lines start ticked.
3. Check each line: **Order**, **Status**, **Supplier**, **What** and **Amount (before VAT)**. Switch off **Tag** on any line that does not belong to this project.
4. Click **Tag Ticked Lines**. Nothing changes until you do.

## What happens next

- The ticked lines get the project's cost account in full. Confirmed orders that are not yet billed now count as **Committed** on the project. Bills created from them afterwards normally carry the same cost account, so they count as **Spent**.
- The project's chatter records how many lines were tagged, with which cost account and by whom.
- Budget tracking is refreshed at once, so **Budget left** and the **Budget status** may change. If an order now takes a budget line over its plan, the project shows **Over plan**.
- The **Untagged tender order lines** button disappears when no lines are left.
- To fix one order rather than a whole tender (for example an order raised without a tender, or a line that should be split between two cost accounts), use **Assign Cost Accounts** on the purchase order itself. See [Set the cost account on order lines](/projects/how-to/approvers/assign-cost-accounts/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Tick the lines to tag.* | You switched off **Tag** on every line, then clicked **Tag Ticked Lines**. | Switch on the lines you want, or click **Cancel**. |
| *Only the Head of Delivery or finance can tag orders.* | You don't have the Head of Delivery or Delivery Finance role. | Ask one of them to tag the lines. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Bills already posted keep their own cost account. Only the order line changes, so **Already billed** lines may still not count as spent. Ask finance to correct the bill if it should.
- The list includes RFQs (not yet confirmed) as well as confirmed orders. Cancelled orders are left out.
- When a tender has more than one delivery project, the same untagged lines appear in each project's window. Tag each line from the project it belongs to, and switch off the others.
- When a tender has exactly one delivery project, new order lines on it get the project's cost account automatically. With several projects, buyers must pick the cost account on each line.
- Lines tagged to a different cost account are not listed here. The **Budget left** pop-up lists confirmed tender orders with lines not tagged to this project.
:::
