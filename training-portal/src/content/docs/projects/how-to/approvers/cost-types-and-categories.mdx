Project managers list costs in plain language: they pick a **cost type** such as *Equipment we supply*, and the type decides which **budget category** the amount is summarised in. Categories are what finance approves and what the accounting budget is built from. All four lists are under **Project › Delivery › Setup**. **Starter items** and **standard milestones** give every new project a head start.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-cost-types" caption="The Cost Types list. The numbers match steps 1 to 4." />

## Steps

### Cost types

1. Open **Project › Delivery › Setup › Cost Types** (Head of Delivery).
2. Click **New**. A blank row opens at the bottom of the list.
3. Fill in **Type** (the name project managers see), **Hint** (one line shown when they pick it), **Budget category**, and optionally **Product categories**. Click away from the row to save it. Drag the handle on the left to change the order.
4. To stop using a type, switch off the toggle at the end of its row. It stays on old Delivery Budgets but can't be picked on new cost items.

### Budget categories

5. Open **Project › Delivery › Setup › Budget Categories**. Delivery Finance can also use **Accounting › Configuration › Project Budgets › Budget Categories**. Each row has a **Name**, **What goes in**, a **Budgetary position** and a **Contingency** tick for the reserve category. Edit a row in place, or click **New**.
6. To stop using a category, switch off its toggle. Categories can't be deleted.

### Starter items

7. Open **Project › Delivery › Setup › Starter Items** (or, for Delivery Finance, **Accounting › Configuration › Project Budgets › Starter Items**). Set **What**, **Type**, **Qty**, **Unit** and **Unit cost** (company currency, before VAT; leave 0 for the project manager to price). For a contingency type, set **% of other costs** instead. **Note** becomes the item's reference.

### Standard milestones

8. Open **Project › Delivery › Setup › Standard Milestones** (Head of Delivery). Add, rename, reorder or switch off milestones by **Name**.

## What happens next

- **Cost types:** new and changed types are offered on cost items straight away. If you change a type's **Budget category**, draft budgets move the amount when they are next changed or submitted. Approved budgets keep their category totals.
- **Product categories:** in budget tracking, an order line's committed amount counts under the category of the cost type whose product categories match the ordered product. Without a match, services count as *Work by a subcontractor* and goods as *Equipment we supply*.
- **Budget categories:** a category's **Budgetary position** decides which accounting budget line it lands on when a Delivery Budget is approved. A category with no position goes on the **Direct project costs** position. **Apply mapping** in [Delivery Settings](/projects/how-to/approvers/delivery-settings/) sets the positions of the standard categories for you, and overwrites them each time it runs.
- **Starter items** are added to the first draft budget when a project manager opens a delivery project from a won tender, or links an existing project that has no Delivery Budget yet. Existing budgets and revisions are not changed.
- **Standard milestones** are copied onto a project when it is opened from a won tender or linked to one, unless the project already has a milestone with the same name. Existing projects are not changed.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Starter items cannot have negative values (Travel and site costs).* | **Qty**, **Unit cost** or **% of other costs** is below 0. | Enter 0 or more. |
| *Accounts 500000 are in both "Project travel and site" and the fallback "Direct project costs". Remove them from "Direct project costs" first, or the same cost would count twice.* | The **Budgetary position** you chose shares a ledger account with the direct project costs position. | Fix the accounts in [Delivery Settings](/projects/how-to/approvers/delivery-settings/), or choose another position. |

:::note[Good to know]
- What you see under **Project › Delivery › Setup** depends on your role: the Head of Delivery sees all four lists; Delivery Finance sees **Budget Categories** and **Starter Items** (plus **Delivery Settings** and **Cost Accounts to Sort Out**, see [Sort out cost accounts with no project](/projects/how-to/approvers/sort-out-cost-accounts/)). Project managers don't see **Setup**.
- The **Budget category** field on a cost type says *Set once by finance*, but only the Head of Delivery can change cost types. Agree the mapping with finance.
- The **Contingency** tick marks the reserve. Cost types in a contingency category can be priced as a **% of other costs**, and the total in contingency categories is limited by the **Contingency cap (%)** in Delivery Settings.
- A starter item left at 0 stops the project manager from submitting the Delivery Budget until they price it or delete it. Use 0 only for costs that most projects really have.
- Switch off rather than delete. A cost type that is used on a cost item can't be deleted.
:::
