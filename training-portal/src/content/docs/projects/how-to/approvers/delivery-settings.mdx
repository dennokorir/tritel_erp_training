**Delivery Settings** hold the rules every delivery project follows: when a handover still counts as on time, when a Delivery Budget is due, how much contingency is allowed, who the MD is, and how budgets reach Accounting Budgets.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-delivery-settings" caption="The Delivery Settings page. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › Delivery › Setup › Delivery Settings**. Delivery Finance can also use **Accounting › Configuration › Project Budgets › Delivery Settings**. The settings page opens directly.
2. Under **KPI rules**, set:
   - **Completion date grace (days)**: a handover up to this many days after the deadline still counts as on time (7 by default);
   - **Budget due after award (days)**: when a project without an approved Delivery Budget is flagged (14);
   - **Contingency cap (%)**: the most contingency a budget may carry, as a share of its total (5).
3. Under **MD approval of revisions**, choose the **MD** (one person) and the limits: **MD approval above budget increase (%)** (10) and **MD approval above date extension (days)** (30).
4. Under **Budgets and cost accounts**, set:
   - **New cost accounts**: **A new plan per project, named after it** or **One shared plan for all delivery projects**. If you choose the shared plan, also pick the **Shared plan for project cost accounts**;
   - **Budget runs past completion (days)**: how long the accounting budget runs after the completion date, so late bills still count (180);
   - **VAT rate (%)**: the VAT taken out of prices entered with **Incl. VAT** (16).
5. Under **Accounting budget mapping** (usually finance), choose the ledger **Accounts** for **Direct project costs**, **Project travel and site** and **Project bonds and insurance**, and the **Reserve account** for **Project contingency**. Each account may sit in one group only.
6. Click **Apply mapping**. Odoo saves the page and confirms *Budget mapping applied. New approvals create accounting budget lines per position and month.*

Changes to the other settings are saved when you leave the page, or when you click the save icon.

## What happens next

- **Completion date grace (days)** is used when finance closes a project, and is frozen on the project as **Grace at close (days)**. Projects already closed keep the grace they closed with.
- **Budget due after award (days)** moves every project's **Budget due** date at once.
- **Contingency cap (%)**, **VAT rate (%)** and **Budget runs past completion (days)** apply to Delivery Budgets submitted from now on. The VAT rate is the default for new cost items; existing items keep theirs.
- The **MD** and the MD limits apply at once, including to revisions already waiting (see Good to know).
- **New cost accounts** applies only when a delivery project is opened without an existing cost account.
- **Apply mapping** creates or updates the four budget positions (**Direct project costs**, **Project travel and site**, **Project bonds and insurance**, **Project contingency**) and links each budget category to one. Delivery Budgets approved from then on get accounting budget lines per position and month. Accounting budgets already created are not changed.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Delivery settings cannot be negative.* | A number is below 0. | Enter 0 or more. |
| *Choose at least one direct project cost account (usually 500000 Cost of sales).* | **Accounts** under **Direct project costs** is empty. | Add at least one account. |
| *Account 600000 is in both "Direct project costs" and "Travel and site". Each account may sit in one position only, or the same cost would count twice.* | The same ledger account is in two groups. | Remove it from one of them. |
| *Only finance or the Head of Delivery can change the budget mapping.* | You are not in either group (or an accounting adviser). | Ask finance or the Head of Delivery. |
| *Set the shared plan for project cost accounts in Delivery Settings first.* | A project manager opened a project while **One shared plan** is chosen and no plan is set. | Pick the **Shared plan for project cost accounts**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The Head of Delivery and Delivery Finance both see **Delivery Settings** under **Project › Delivery › Setup**, and both can change it. Project managers don't see **Setup**.
- The yellow box under **Accounting budget mapping** lists what is still to do, for example *Categories without a budgetary position: Logistics. Press Apply mapping.* Until the mapping is applied, those categories are budgeted together on the direct project costs position.
- The MD limits are read live. Lowering them can make a revision already waiting need the MD, but the MD gets no to-do for it, only an item on their My Actions. Raising them can mean the MD's approval is no longer needed, but their to-do stays, and if the MD's was the only approval missing, the revision stays **Waiting for approval** with nobody able to approve it. Change the limits when no revision is waiting, if you can.
- The contingency reserve account is one nobody posts to, so contingency shows as planned only and never fills up in budget tracking.
:::
