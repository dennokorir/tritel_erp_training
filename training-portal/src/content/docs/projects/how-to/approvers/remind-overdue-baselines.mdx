A delivery project can't be measured until its Delivery Budget is approved. When a project passes its **Budget due** date with no budget, or only a draft, it shows on your My Actions so you can chase the project manager.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-remind-overdue" caption="Overdue Delivery Budgets on your My Actions. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › My Actions**.
2. Find **Budgets overdue on your team**. Each item shows the project, the project manager, the client, whether there is **No budget** or a **Budget in draft**, the due date, and how many days it is overdue.
3. Click **Remind** on an item.
4. The item then shows *reminded today* and the **Remind** button disappears until tomorrow. Click **Open** to look at the project.

To see every project in the same state, open **Project › Delivery › Projects › Delivery Projects** and use the **Needs a budget** filter. Projects with no budget show in red.

## What happens next

- The project manager gets a message on the project: *Reminder from My Actions: Data Centre Upgrade, the delivery budget (due 15 Mar 2026).*
- The item stays on your list until the project manager submits the Delivery Budget. Once it is submitted it moves to the approvers, and you see it under **Budgets to approve: dates** (and in **Project › Delivery › To approve › Waiting for Me**).
- Separately, a daily check gives the project manager and every Head of Delivery a *Delivery Budget overdue* to-do the first time a project passes its due date. That to-do is marked done when a budget is submitted or approved. The check is the scheduled job *Project Delivery: flag projects without an approved budget*.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *You already sent a reminder about this today.* | You reminded on this project earlier today, perhaps in another tab. | Try again tomorrow. |
| *Nothing is waiting on anyone here.* | The budget was submitted after the page loaded. | Refresh My Actions. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The list shows projects managed by others. Your own overdue projects are under **Budgets to draft**.
- A project with no project manager shows without a **Remind** button. Set the project manager on the project first.
- The *Delivery Budget overdue* to-do is sent only once per project. After that, chasing is up to you through **Remind**.
- The due date counts from the tender's award date, or from the date the project was created if the tender has none.
:::
