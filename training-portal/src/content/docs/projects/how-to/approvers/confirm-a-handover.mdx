The **handover** is the date the client took over the work. Only a handover you have confirmed counts for the on-time part of the Delivery KPI, so check the date against the evidence before you confirm.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-confirm-handover" caption="A handover waiting for your confirmation. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › Delivery › Handovers**. The list opens on **Waiting for confirmation**. Click the handover. You can also use **Project › My Actions**, under **Handovers to confirm**, where each item says whether a certificate is attached.
2. Check the **Handover date**. It should be the date the client took over the work, the date on the certificate where there is one.
3. Open the **Handover certificate**, if there is one. It is optional.
4. Read the project manager's note: what was handed over, who signed for the client, and anything outstanding.
5. If it is right, click **Confirm Handover**.
6. If something needs to change, type it in **Note when returning** under **Head of Delivery**, then click **Return**.

From **My Actions** you can click **Confirm**, or **Return** and type the note in the box that opens.

## What happens next

- **After Confirm Handover:** the handover is **Confirmed** and the project's **Handover** button shows **Confirmed**. The project's chatter records the date. The *Confirm project handover* to-dos are removed from every Head of Delivery.
- Every Delivery Finance user gets a *Close delivery project* to-do, and the project appears under **Projects to close** on their My Actions. Finance then [closes the project](/projects/how-to/approvers/close-a-project/).
- **After Return:** the handover is **Returned**. Whoever submitted it (or the project manager) gets a *Handover returned* to-do with your note, and sees it in a yellow **Returned** banner. They fix it and submit it again.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Write a note explaining what needs to change, then return it.* | **Note when returning** is empty. | Type the note first, then click **Return**. |
| *Only a submitted handover can be confirmed.* | It was already confirmed or returned. | Refresh the page and check the status bar. |
| *Only a submitted or confirmed handover can be returned.* | It is still a draft, or already returned. | Leave it for the project manager. |
| *Only the Head of Delivery can confirm a handover.* | You don't have the Head of Delivery role. | Ask a Head of Delivery to confirm it. |
| *PRJ Customer A network upgrade is closed. Finance can reopen it first.* | The project is already closed. | Ask finance to reopen it if the handover must change. |

:::note[Good to know]
- You can also **Return** a handover you have already confirmed, as long as the project is not closed. The project then has no confirmed handover. Finance's *Close delivery project* to-do stays, but finance cannot close a project with an approved baseline until a handover is confirmed again.
- A project can have only one handover waiting or confirmed at a time.
:::
