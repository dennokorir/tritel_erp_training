After a project closes, its final cost and result stay frozen. If a late bill is posted or a new order is confirmed on its cost account, the difference shows as **costs after close**. You decide whether it should change the result.

<Illustration name="projects/appr-costs-after-close" caption="A closed project with costs after close. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Project › My Actions** and find **Costs after close**. Each item shows the amount since close, for example *KES 15,000 since close*. Click **Open**, or open the to-do.
2. On the project, open the **Handover & close** tab.
3. Read the **Costs after close** alert, and compare it with the frozen **Result** figures: **Final cost (before VAT)**, **Spent at close** and **Open orders at close**. Use the **Spent so far** and **Committed** buttons at the top to see the new lines.
4. If the costs should not change the result (for example, they were already in the final cost as an adjustment), click **Mark reviewed**.
5. If they should count, click **Reopen** at the top, give the reason, and then [close the project again](/projects/how-to/approvers/close-a-project/) with the new figures.

You can also click **Mark reviewed** or **Reopen** straight from the item on My Actions.

## What happens next

- **After Mark reviewed:** the project stays closed. The alert shows *Reviewed by* your name and the date. Your *Costs after close* to-do is marked done, the project's chatter records the amount reviewed, and the item leaves My Actions.
- If the costs change again later, the item comes back and the daily check sends a new to-do.
- **After Reopen:** the final cost and result are cleared, and the earlier figures go into the chatter. The project is open again until you close it.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Only finance can mark costs after close as reviewed.* | You don't have the Delivery Finance role. | Ask a finance user. |
| *Give the reason for reopening.* | You clicked **Reopen Project** with an empty reason. | Type why it is reopened. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The amount is the change since close: today's spent plus open orders, minus the spent and open orders frozen at close. A bill posted against an order that was already open at close does not add to it, because the cost simply moves from open orders to spent.
- The amount can be negative, for example when an open order is cancelled after close.
- Marking reviewed never changes the KPI result. Only reopening and closing again does.
:::
