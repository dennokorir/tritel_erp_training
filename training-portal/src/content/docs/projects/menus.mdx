Project Delivery has menus in three apps. Delivery project managers and Heads of Delivery work in **Project › Delivery**. Delivery Finance works mostly in **Accounting › Project Budgets**, which holds the same screens where finance already works. Purchase managers find the **Budget Commitments** report under **Purchase › Reporting**. What you see depends on your Project Delivery role.

| Role | How they get it |
|---|---|
| **Delivery Project Manager** | Automatic: anyone who is the **Project Manager** on an active delivery project has it (see below). |
| **Delivery Finance** | Given by hand, in the user's access rights under **Project Delivery**. |
| **Head of Delivery** | Given by hand, in the user's access rights under **Project Delivery**. A Head of Delivery also has everything a Delivery Project Manager has. |
| **Accounting manager** | The Accounting app's own administrator right. Accounting managers see one Project Budgets screen: **Cost Accounts to Sort Out**. |
| **Purchase manager** | The Purchase app's own administrator right. |

<Illustration name="projects/matrix-menus" caption="Which Project Delivery menus each role sees." />

## The Delivery Project Manager role is automatic

You don't ask for the **Delivery Project Manager** role. Odoo gives it to you as soon as you are the **Project Manager** on an active delivery project, and takes it away when you no longer manage one: when the project is handed to someone else, stops being a delivery project or is archived. It is checked each time a project's manager changes, and again every day by the scheduled job *Project Delivery: Project Manager role follows the projects*, which also undoes any change made to the role by hand. A Head of Delivery is never touched: their role already includes it.

:::note[Good to know]
Opening a delivery project from a won tender needs the Delivery Project Manager role. Someone who doesn't manage a delivery project yet doesn't have it, so they see *Only delivery project managers can open delivery projects.* Ask a Head of Delivery to open the project and name you as its **Project Manager**; the role follows. See [Open a delivery project](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/open-a-delivery-project/).
:::

## Project › Delivery

**Project › Delivery** shows to Delivery Project Managers, Delivery Finance and Heads of Delivery. It has three sections.

### To approve

Delivery Finance and Heads of Delivery see this section.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for Me** | Delivery budgets waiting for your approval, on the **Waiting for me** filter. See [Approve a budget](/projects/how-to/approvers/approve-budget/). |

### Projects

| Item | Who sees it | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **My Delivery Projects** | Delivery Project Managers, Heads of Delivery | Delivery projects, on the **My Projects** filter. Clear the filter to see all of them. |
| **Delivery Projects** | Delivery Finance, Heads of Delivery | Every delivery project. |
| **Projects Not in Delivery** | Heads of Delivery | Projects that aren't delivery projects, to link to a won tender. |
| **Delivery Budgets** | Everyone with a delivery role | Delivery budgets, on the **Waiting for approval** filter. |
| **Budget Commitments** | Everyone with a delivery role | Open purchase orders against accounting budgets, for the whole company. See [Budget Commitments](/projects/reports/budget-commitments/). |
| **My Submissions** | Delivery Project Managers, Heads of Delivery | The budgets you submitted that are still waiting, on the **My submissions, waiting** filter. |
| **Handovers** | Everyone with a delivery role | Handovers, on the **Waiting for confirmation** filter. |
| **Client Delays** | Everyone with a delivery role | Client delays, on the **Waiting for approval** filter. |

### Setup

Delivery Finance and Heads of Delivery see this section.

| Item | Who sees it | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Delivery Settings** | Delivery Finance, Heads of Delivery | KPI rules, the MD and the MD approval limits, cost accounts, the VAT rate and the accounting budget mapping. See [Change the Delivery settings](/projects/how-to/approvers/delivery-settings/). |
| **Cost Types** | Heads of Delivery | The plain-language types project managers pick for each cost. See [Maintain cost types, categories, starter items and standard milestones](/projects/how-to/approvers/cost-types-and-categories/). |
| **Budget Categories** | Delivery Finance, Heads of Delivery | The categories every delivery budget is split into. |
| **Starter Items** | Delivery Finance, Heads of Delivery | Costs added to every new draft budget. |
| **Standard Milestones** | Heads of Delivery | The milestones every new delivery project starts with. |
| **Cost Accounts to Sort Out** | Delivery Finance, Heads of Delivery | Cost accounts that belong to no project. See [Sort out cost accounts with no project](/projects/how-to/approvers/sort-out-cost-accounts/). |

## Accounting › Project Budgets

Delivery Finance sees all of this menu. Accounting managers see only **Tracking › Cost Accounts to Sort Out**.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **To approve › Budgets to Approve** | Delivery budgets, on the **Waiting for me** filter. When there are none: *No budget is waiting for your approval.* |
| **To approve › Costs After Close** | Closed delivery projects, on the **Costs after close to review** filter. When there are none: *No closed project has costs after close waiting for review.* See [Review costs after close](/projects/how-to/approvers/review-costs-after-close/). |
| **Tracking › Budget vs Actual** | Delivery projects with their tracking figures, on the **Over plan** and **Fully committed** filters. **On track** and **No approved budget** are in **Filters** too. |
| **Tracking › Delivery Budgets** | The same list as **Project › Delivery › Projects › Delivery Budgets**. |
| **Tracking › Budget Commitments** | The [Budget Commitments](/projects/reports/budget-commitments/) report. |
| **Tracking › Cost Accounts to Sort Out** | See [Sort out cost accounts with no project](/projects/how-to/approvers/sort-out-cost-accounts/). |

**Accounting › Configuration › Project Budgets** holds **Delivery Settings**, **Budget Categories** and **Starter Items** for Delivery Finance: the same screens as **Project › Delivery › Setup**.

:::note[Good to know]
The Accounting app shows only to people with an accounting right, and its **Configuration** menu only to accounting managers. A Delivery Finance user without those rights doesn't see **Accounting › Project Budgets** at all; they use **Project › Delivery**, which has the same lists.
:::

## Purchase › Reporting

| Item | Who sees it | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget Commitments** | Purchase managers | The [Budget Commitments](/projects/reports/budget-commitments/) report. |

## Renamed menus

If you knew the earlier Delivery menus, this is where each item went.

| Old name | Now |
|---|---|
| **Project › Delivery › Waiting for My Approval** | **Project › Delivery › To approve › Waiting for Me** |
| **Project › Delivery › Delivery Projects** (for project managers) | **Project › Delivery › Projects › My Delivery Projects**. **Delivery Projects** is now for Delivery Finance and Heads of Delivery only. |
| **Project › Delivery › All Baselines** | **Project › Delivery › Projects › Delivery Budgets** |
| **Project › Delivery › Projects to Link** | **Project › Delivery › Projects › Projects Not in Delivery** |
| **Project › Delivery › Handovers**, **Client Delays** | **Project › Delivery › Projects › Handovers**, **Client Delays** |
| **Project › Configuration › Delivery** | **Project › Delivery › Setup**, which Delivery Finance can open too |
