**Project contingency** is a reserve in the baseline for known risks: a supplier price rise, an extra site visit, a delay at customs. It is a cost item of type *Contingency*, in the **Contingency** budget category. Every new baseline starts with one, at 5% of the other costs.

You price contingency in one of two ways:

- **As a share:** fill in **% of other costs**. The amount follows the other items: if they go up, so does the contingency.
- **As an amount:** leave the percentage empty and give it a **Unit cost**.

If you give it neither, confirming stops with *Give the contingency a percentage or an amount.*

## The cap

Contingency may not be more than a set share of the **Budget total**. The share is **Contingency cap (%)** in **Delivery Settings**, 5% by default. The budget total includes the contingency itself. The check runs when you click **Confirm for Approval**.

## Where contingency shows

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| Baseline | **Contingency** next to the **Budget total**. The contingency line is greyed in the cost items. |
| Spend plan by month | Contingency has no pay months of its own. It is spread over the months in the same proportion as the other costs. If no other cost has a pay month yet, it sits in the completion month. |
| Odoo budget | Its own line, **Project contingency**, on the reserve account (500900 *Project Contingency Reserve*). Nobody posts to that account, so the line shows as planned only. |
| Budget tracking | Its own row, **Project contingency**, with nothing committed or spent. Its planned amount counts in **Left**. |

To use contingency in practice, you request a revision that moves the money from the contingency item to the cost that needs it. See [Request a revision](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/request-a-revision/).

:::note[Good to know]
- Because nothing is ever posted to the contingency row, it never fills up. A project with contingency will rarely show **Fully committed** overall, and **Left** includes the unused contingency.
- Until finance sets a contingency reserve account in **Delivery Settings**, contingency is budgeted together with the direct project costs.
- Contingency counts in the approved budget that the final cost is compared with at close. Unused contingency makes it easier to finish on budget.
:::
