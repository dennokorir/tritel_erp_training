A baseline is approved in parts. Each approver signs off the part they are responsible for, in any order. When every approval the baseline needs is in, it becomes **Approved** by itself.

| Approval | Button | Who | When it is needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dates** | **Approve Dates** | Head of Delivery | Always: the original and every revision. Covers the completion date and the milestone dates. |
| **Budget** | **Approve Budget** | Delivery Finance | The original, and any revision that changes the costs: a category total, or any cost item's name, type, quantity, price, currency, VAT or contingency %. |
| **MD** | **MD Approve** | The user named as **MD** in **Delivery Settings** | A revision whose **Budget vs original (%)** is above 10%, or whose **Days vs original** is above 30 days. |

Everyone with the Head of Delivery role, and everyone with the Delivery Finance role, gets the to-do. The first one to act gives the approval for their role. A revision that only moves dates needs the Head of Delivery alone; one that only moves money needs the Head of Delivery and Delivery Finance.

## The MD thresholds

The MD limits are measured **against version 1**, not against the previous version, so a large change can't be split into small revisions. Only increases count, and the change must be strictly more than the limit. Both limits are set in **Delivery Settings** under **MD approval of revisions**: **MD approval above budget increase (%)** (10 by default) and **MD approval above date extension (days)** (30 by default).

| Revision | Budget vs original (%) | Days vs original | MD needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| v2 | 10.0 | 15 | No: exactly 10% is not above 10% |
| v3 | 14.3 | 40 | Yes: both limits passed |
| v4 | -5.0 | 31 | Yes: the date limit is passed |

## Who can do what

<Illustration name="projects/concept-approvals-matrix" caption="Who approves what in Project Delivery." />

- **Return to Draft** sends a waiting baseline back to the project manager with a **Note for the project manager**. It clears every approval already given, so all the needed approvals start again when it is reconfirmed.
- **Handovers** and **client delays** are confirmed or approved by the Head of Delivery alone. See [Handover and close-out](/projects/concepts/handover-and-close-out/) and [Client delays](/projects/concepts/client-delays/).
- **Closing** a project and fixing its final cost is for Delivery Finance alone.

## Where approvers find their work

- A to-do: **Approve delivery baseline: dates**, **Approve delivery baseline: budget** or **Approve delivery baseline revision (MD)**. If the costs are over the tender's expected revenue, the to-do says so.
- **Project › Delivery › Waiting for My Approval**, which opens on the **Waiting for me** filter.
- **Project › My Actions**: **Baselines to approve: dates**, **Baselines to approve: budget** and **Revisions to approve (MD)**, each with an approve button and **Return**.

:::note[Good to know]
- The **Return to Draft** button shows only to approvers whose approval is still pending. Once you have approved your part, you no longer see it.
- The MD is a single user named in the settings, not a role. The **Delivery** menu shows only to delivery project managers and Delivery Finance, so an MD who is neither works from the to-do and **Project › My Actions**. If a revision needs the MD and nobody is named, confirming stops with *This revision needs the MD, but no MD is set in Delivery Settings.*
- Approving the budget fixes the exchange rates on foreign-currency items at once, even if the other approvals are still to come.
:::
