**Project › My Actions** lists what is waiting on you. The group **Projects over their approved budget** shows the delivery projects whose **Budget status** is **Over plan**: committed and spent are above the plan on at least one budget line.

<Illustration name="projects/report-over-budget" caption="Two projects over plan in My Actions. The numbers match the list below." />

## Reading the list

1. **Overdue tile.** Every project in this group is counted as overdue as well as to do.
2. **The group** and its hint: *Committed and spent are above the approved budget in at least one category.*
3. **Each project** shows *planned 850,000 · committed and spent 762,000*: the whole approved budget and everything ordered or spent. A Head of Delivery also sees the project manager's name.
4. **Buttons.** **Open tracking** and **Open** both open the project form; the budget tracking table is on its **Delivery** tab. **Request revision** starts a revision of the approved baseline. It shows only to the project's own manager, if they are a delivery project manager, and only while the baseline status is **Approved**.
5. **Who sees what.** The Head of Delivery sees every open delivery project that is over plan. Everyone else sees only the projects where they are the **Project Manager**.

A project leaves the list when its status is no longer **Over plan** at the next refresh, when it closes, or as soon as a revision is started. A project with a revision in draft or waiting is listed under **Revisions in progress** instead, marked *over its approved budget*.

## The budget tracking window

Click **Budget left** on the project (or **Margin left** on its approved baseline) to open **Budget tracking · Customer A network upgrade**.

<Illustration name="projects/report-tracking-popup" caption="The tracking window for the same project. Travel is the line over plan." />

1. **Status badge**: the project's **Budget status**.
2. **Figures as of**: when the figures were last refreshed.
3. **One row per budget line**, with **Planned**, **Expected by now**, **Committed**, **Spent**, **Left** (red below zero), **Forecast at completion** and **Status**. A dash means 0.
4. **Whole budget**: the totals.
5. **Confirmed tender orders with lines not tagged to this project**: confirmed orders on the tender with lines that carry no delivery project's cost account, with **Order**, **Supplier** and **Lines not tagged** (for example *2 of 5*). Those lines count nowhere until someone tags them.
6. **Close** shuts the window.

How each figure is worked out is on [Planned, expected, committed, spent, left and status](/projects/calculations/tracking-figures/).

## What to do about it

- Find the line marked **Over plan** and check its orders and bills from **Committed** and **Spent so far**.
- If a cost belongs to another project, ask finance to move it.
- If the plan was too low, **Request revision**. A revision above the limits in Delivery Settings also needs the MD. See [Request a revision](/projects/how-to/delivery-pm/request-a-revision/).

:::note[Good to know]
A project can be in this list while its whole-budget figures look fine, for example *planned 850,000 · committed and spent 762,000*. One line over its plan is enough; money left on other lines, including contingency, does not offset it.

The list reads the stored **Budget status**, which is refreshed every night and when someone clicks **Refresh**. A cost posted this morning may not show here until tomorrow.
:::
