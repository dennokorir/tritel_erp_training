Open a project from **Project › Delivery › Projects › My Delivery Projects** (or **Delivery Projects** if you are the Head of Delivery or in Delivery Finance). The project code, such as *P26-049*, sits above the project name. The buttons along the top of the form summarise the delivery plan, the money and the handover. Hover over a button for a short explanation. The money figures are in KES, before VAT, and come from the last tracking refresh (see **Figures as of** on the **Delivery** tab).

<Illustration name="projects/report-project-buttons" caption="A delivery project with an approved revision, a travel line over plan and a handover waiting. The numbers match the list below." />

## On the project

1. **Budget** (the **Delivery Budget** button): the version that counts, for example **Approved v3**. While a version waits for approval it shows **Waiting v4** in amber, or **Draft v1** before anything is approved. Underneath: the number of revisions, or *v4 in draft* / *v4 waiting* when a revision is in progress. Opens the version being worked on, else the approved one. With no budget at all it starts the first one (project managers only).
2. **Approved delivery budget**: the approved budget's **Budget total**, contingency included. This is the figure the budget KPI compares the final cost with. Opens the approved budget. Shown once a budget is approved.
3. **Spent so far**: costs posted to the project's cost account on expense accounts, with *Spent 54%* of the approved budget underneath. Finance staff with accounting access get the journal items behind it; everyone else gets a read-only list of cost lines with a pivot by budget line and month. Shown once something is spent.
4. **Committed**: confirmed purchase order lines on the cost account not yet billed. Buyers get the purchase orders; everyone else gets a read-only list of order lines filtered to **Still to bill**. Shown once something is committed.
5. **Budget left**: approved budget − committed − spent. Green when **On track**, amber when **Fully committed**, red when **Over plan** on at least one budget line, with the status underneath (*Left · over plan*). Opens the budget tracking window. See [Projects over their approved budget](/projects/reports/projects-over-budget/).
6. **Cost account**: the analytic account that order lines, bills and expenses must carry to count for this project, with its plan underneath. Finance with accounting access opens the account itself; everyone else gets a summary card: **Cost lines posted**, **Spent (expense accounts)**, **Automatic tagging rules** and **Also used by**.
7. **Won tender**: the tender the project was opened from, with its tender number. Opens the tender.
8. **Handover**: **Not recorded**, **Draft**, **Waiting**, **Confirmed**, **Returned** or **Closed**. Opens the project's handovers.
9. **Purchase orders** (*Orders*): orders with at least one line on the cost account, cancelled orders left out. Purchase users only.
10. **Vendor bills** (*Bills*): bills and refunds with a line on the cost account. Accounting access only.
11. **Accounting budget lines**: the number of lines in the accounting budget made from the approved budget. Accounting access only.
12. **Untagged tender order lines** (*Untagged lines*, amber): lines on this tender's purchase orders with no cost account. They count as neither committed nor spent until tagged. Head of Delivery and Delivery Finance only; opens the tagging tool.
13. **Automatic tagging rules**: rules that put this cost account on new lines automatically. Accounting access only.

Buttons 1 to 8 have no access restriction: anyone who can open the project sees them once they apply. Buttons 3 and 4 are hidden while their figure is 0, and button 12 while there is nothing to tag.

## On the budget, the tender and elsewhere

<Illustration name="projects/report-other-buttons" caption="The buttons on budget v2 and on its won tender. The numbers match the list below." />

1. **Accounting Budget** (budget): opens the accounting budget created on approval. Accounting access only.
2. **Project**: opens the delivery project.
3. **Tender**: opens the won tender.
4. **Budget Versions** (*Revisions*): every version of the budget with its status. Shown once there is a revision.
5. **Margin left**: expected revenue − this budget − the tender's other delivery projects, red when below zero. Opens the budget tracking window once a budget is approved, otherwise the project. See [margin](/projects/calculations/margin/).
6. **Projects** (on the tender, once called **Delivery projects**): the codes of every project linked to the tender, for example *P26-049, P26-050*: delivery projects, projects linked for reference only, and archived ones. Opens the project, or the list when there are several.
7. **Delivery budgets**: the total of each delivery project's approved budget, or its latest draft or waiting version. Opens the budget, or the list when there are several projects.
8. **Margin left** (tender): the tender's Expected Revenue at today's rate − Delivery budgets. Red below zero.
9. **Untagged order lines** (tender): Head of Delivery and Delivery Finance only.

Two more places carry a delivery button:

- **Budget Available** on a purchase order (once called **Left in delivery plan**): the project's **Budget left**, red when this order takes a budget line over its plan. Opens the project. The order may also show **Over the delivery plan.** and **Not tagged to the project.** Separately, the order's **Budget status** badge compares it with the accounting budget; see [Check a purchase order against the budget](/projects/how-to/approvers/check-an-order-against-the-budget/). See [tracking figures](/projects/calculations/tracking-figures/).
- **Delivery project** and **Delivery budget** on an analytic account used as a project's cost account.

:::note[Good to know]
The money buttons show the figures from the last refresh, which happens every night and when someone clicks **Refresh** on the **Delivery** tab. The **Budget tracking** table on that tab is worked out each time you open the project, so it can be a step ahead of the buttons.

Everyone who can open a delivery project sees its budget figures. There is no per-project restriction.
:::
