Most records have a **chatter**: a panel at the side or bottom of the form.

<Illustration name="general/chatter" caption="The chatter on a tender." />

1. **Send message** emails the record's followers. Use it for messages that should leave the building or reach everyone following the record.
2. **Log note** adds an internal note. Only staff see it, and nobody is emailed unless you @mention them.
3. **Activities** schedules a to-do for someone, with a due date.
4. **Planned activities** lists to-dos still open on this record. The systems create many of these for you, for example "review this baseline".
5. **History** shows every message, note and tracked change, newest first.

## Where your to-dos show up

- The clock icon at the top right of the back office lists every activity assigned to you.
- Each system also has a **My Actions** list that gathers the work waiting for you. See [Your My Actions lists](/getting-started/my-actions/).
- Many steps also send an email, so you can act from your inbox.
