## Lists and boards

A **list** shows records as rows. A **board** (Odoo calls it *kanban*) shows them as cards in columns, usually one column per status. Switch between them with the view buttons at the top right.

- **Search:** type a word and pick what to search in (for example *Employee* or *Customer*).
- **Filters:** ready-made filters such as *My Sessions* or *Needs My Response*. Several filters together narrow the list further.
- **Group By:** groups rows, for example by status or by person. Click a group to open it.
- **Favorites:** save the current search to use again.

Many lists open with a filter already applied. If a list looks empty, check the grey filter tags in the search bar and remove them with ×.

## Forms

A **form** shows one record.

<Illustration name="general/form-parts" caption="The parts of a form." />

1. **Action buttons** do the next step of the work, for example **Record Handover**. You only see the buttons that apply to the record's current status and your role.
2. **The status bar** shows where the record is in its workflow. The highlighted step is the current one.
3. **Smart buttons** show related figures and open related records, for example the project's baseline or its spend.
4. **Fields.** Click into a field to change it.
5. **Tabs** hold more fields and lists.

## Saving

Odoo 16 saves a form when you leave it, or when you click the cloud icon beside the breadcrumb. To throw away changes, click the **×** (Discard) next to it. If a save is refused, a red message explains why; the guides list the common ones under **Common mistakes**.
