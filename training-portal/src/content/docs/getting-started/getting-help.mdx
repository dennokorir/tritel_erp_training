1. **Search this site.** Press Ctrl + K and type what you're trying to do, in your own words.
2. **Read the red message.** If the system refuses to save, the message says why. Task pages list the common ones under **Common mistakes**.
3. **Check your role.** If a menu or button is missing, compare the **Who can do this** box on the task page with your role.
4. **Ask your line manager or HR** about access, targets and reviews; **ask the Head of Delivery** about projects; **ask the Head of Commercial** about tenders.
5. **Report a problem to IT** with the screen's breadcrumb, what you clicked, and a screenshot of any message.
