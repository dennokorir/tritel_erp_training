1. **The app switcher.** The grid icon at the top left shows every app you can use, for example **Accountability**, **Tender Manager** and **Project**. Click one to open it.
2. **The top menu.** Each app has its own menu across the top. In these guides a menu path is written like this: **Accountability › My Team › Review › Team Reviews**. When a menu groups its items under small headings, as **My Team** does (**Set targets**, **Review**, **Approve**), the heading is part of the path.
3. **The breadcrumb.** Shows where you are. Click any earlier part to go back to it. Use it instead of the browser's back button, which can lose unsaved changes.
4. **The search bar.** Type to search, and use **Filters**, **Group By** and **Favorites** to narrow the list. See [Lists, boards and forms](/getting-started/lists-boards-forms/).
5. **The records.** Click a row to open it.

## You only see what your role allows

Menus appear only if your role uses them. In Accountability, a line manager sees **My Team**, HR sees **People**, and someone without reportees sees neither. [Accountability menus](/accountability/menus/) lists what each role sees. If a guide mentions a menu you can't see, check the **Who can do this** box at the top of the page, then ask HR if you think your access is wrong.
