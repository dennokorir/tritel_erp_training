Everyone sees the **Accountability** menus in the same order: **Company Overview**, **My Performance**, **My Team**, **People**, **My Actions**, **Organisation**, **Configuration**. Which of them you see depends on your role:

| Role | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Everyone** | **Company Overview**, **My Performance** and **My Actions**. |
| **Line manager** | Adds **My Team** and **Organisation** (**Objectives** and **Review Coverage**). |
| **HR** | Adds **People** (HR's company-wide lists) and **Organisation** (**Review Coverage**, **Compliance** and **Targets Library**). |
| **Accountability administrator** | Everything a line manager sees, plus **Compliance**, **Targets Library** and **Board Reviews** under **Organisation**, and **Configuration** as the last menu. |

To learn how each role is given, see [Who can do what](/accountability/who-can-do-what/).

## Company Overview

**Accountability › Company Overview** is where the app opens: where the company stands today, with each person's score in the organogram. Everyone sees it. See [Company Overview](/accountability/reports/performance-overview/) and [See my performance](/accountability/how-to/everyone/see-my-performance/).

## My Performance

Everyone sees these.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **My Targets** | Your own targets, scores and milestones. See [Check my targets](/accountability/how-to/everyone/my-targets/). |
| **My Reviews** | Your reviews, to read, acknowledge or question. See [Respond to my review](/accountability/how-to/everyone/respond-to-my-review/). |
| **My Score History** | Your frozen snapshots: your scores as they stood at month ends, period closes and when someone froze them. See [Snapshots and Compare](/accountability/reports/snapshots-and-compare/). |
| **Progress Briefs** | The progress briefs sent to you. Shown only while **Send progress briefs** is switched on. See [Read my progress briefs and snapshots](/accountability/how-to/everyone/read-briefs-and-snapshots/). |

## My Team

Line managers and accountability administrators see this menu. Its items sit under three headings.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Set targets › Team Targets** | Your team's targets, to create, activate and change. See [Set targets for my team](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/set-team-targets/). |
| **Set targets › Cascades** | Following the targets you pushed down to your team, and the shares your team asked for. See [Push a target down, or approve a pull](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/push-and-pull-targets/). |
| **Set targets › Milestones** | Your team's milestone stages on a timeline. See [Set milestone dates and confirm milestones](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/milestone-dates-and-confirmations/). |
| **Set targets › Next Year's Targets** | Next year's targets proposed from this year's, ready to turn into drafts. See [Propose next year's targets](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/propose-next-years-targets/). |
| **Review › Review Sessions** | Review sessions, opening on **My Sessions**. See [Plan a review session](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/plan-a-review-session/). |
| **Review › Team Reviews** | Your team's reviews, grouped by whose move it is. See [Track reviews on the Team Reviews board](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/team-reviews-board/). |
| **Review › Team Scores** | Own, team and overall scores for everyone in your team. See [See my team's scores](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/team-scores/). |
| **Approve › Scorecard Changes** | Changes to your team's scorecards, opening on **Waiting for approval** and **Proposed by employees**. Clear the filters for the full history. See [Request a scorecard change](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/request-a-scorecard-change/). |
| **Approve › Priority Changes** | Changes to your team's priorities and whether they were acknowledged. See [Priority change workflow](/accountability/workflows/priority-change/). |

## People

Only HR sees this menu: the same screens as **My Team**, for the whole company.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Review Sessions** | Every review session, with no **My Sessions** filter. See [Review session workflow](/accountability/workflows/review-session/). |
| **All Reviews** | Every review except your own, on the same board and with the same filters as **Team Reviews**. See [Track reviews on the Team Reviews board](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/team-reviews-board/). |
| **Scores** | The **Team Scores** screen, titled **Scores**, with the **Everyone** scope. See [See my team's scores](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/team-scores/). |
| **Scorecard Changes** | The same list as **My Team › Approve › Scorecard Changes**. See [Decide scorecard changes and escalations](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/decide-changes-and-escalations/). |
| **Priority Changes** | Every priority change. See [Priority change workflow](/accountability/workflows/priority-change/). |
| **Next Year's Targets** | The **Next Year's Targets** screen. See [Propose next year's targets](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/propose-next-years-targets/). |

## My Actions

**Accountability › My Actions** lists everything waiting for you to act on, such as a review to acknowledge or new priorities to accept. Everyone sees it. See [Acknowledge new priorities](/accountability/how-to/everyone/acknowledge-new-priorities/) and [Respond to my review](/accountability/how-to/everyone/respond-to-my-review/).

## Organisation

Line managers, HR and accountability administrators see this menu, each with different items.

| Item | What it opens | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Objectives** | The company's objectives by period. See [Set up objectives](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/objectives/). | Line managers, administrators |
| **Review Coverage** | Whether every manager is running their review sessions. See [Review Coverage](/accountability/reports/session-coverage/). | Line managers, HR, administrators |
| **Compliance** | Which cascaded targets have been adopted, objective by objective. See [Compliance](/accountability/reports/compliance/). | HR, administrators |
| **Targets Library** | Standard targets for each job position, with the **Seed from this year** button. See [Maintain the targets library](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/targets-library/). | HR, administrators |
| **Board Reviews** | The Managing Director's quarterly board reviews. | Administrators |

## Configuration

Only accountability administrators see this menu.

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Periods** | Performance periods, to open, close and reset. See [Open and close a period](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/open-and-close-periods/). |
| **Metrics** | The metrics that measure targets automatically. See [Set up and test a metric](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/metrics/). |
| **Settings** | The company-wide settings: how managers' scores combine, the rating scale, progress briefs, review deadlines and access rights. See [Change the Accountability settings](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/settings/) and [Fix someone's access](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/access-rights/). |

## Renamed, moved and removed

If you knew the old menus, this is where each item went.

| Old | Now |
|---|---|
| **My Performance › Performance Overview** | **Company Overview**, its own menu |
| **My Performance › Performance Snapshots** | **My Performance › My Score History** |
| **My Team › Propose Targets** | **My Team › Set targets › Next Year's Targets** (HR: **People › Next Year's Targets**) |
| **My Team › Cascade Management** | **My Team › Set targets › Cascades** |
| **My Team › Milestone Timeline** | **My Team › Set targets › Milestones** |
| **My Team › Scorecard Change Requests** | **My Team › Approve › Scorecard Changes** (HR: **People › Scorecard Changes**) |
| **Organisation › Session Coverage** | **Organisation › Review Coverage** |
| **Organisation › Periods**, **Metrics**, **Settings** | **Configuration › Periods**, **Metrics**, **Settings** |
| **My Performance › Board Reviews** | **Organisation › Board Reviews**, administrators only |
| HR's **My Team** menu | **People** |
| **Organisation › Scorecard Changes** (the full log) | Removed. Use **Scorecard Changes** under **My Team › Approve** or **People** and clear the filters. |
| **Organisation › Seed Targets Library** | Removed. Use the **Seed from this year** button on **Organisation › Targets Library**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Progress Briefs** appears under **My Performance** only while **Send progress briefs** is switched on in the settings (of any company). Switch it off and the menu disappears for everyone.
- HR staff who also manage people see both **My Team** (their own team, first) and **People** (the whole company).
- HR staff without a team don't see **Organisation › Objectives**.
- **Seed Targets Library** is now the **Seed from this year** button on the **Targets Library** list. It asks you to confirm first and leaves job positions that already have a role set alone. Only HR and accountability administrators can seed.
- Accountability administrators who are not HR reach scorecard changes through **My Team › Approve › Scorecard Changes**. The list has no pivot view any more.
:::
