Each target counts for a share of your score. When a target is added after the period has started, withdrawn, closed, fully passed down to your team, or given a new weight, those shares change. The system collects the changes into one **priority change** and asks you to read and acknowledge it.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-priority-change" caption="A priority change waiting for you. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Actions**. Under **Priorities to acknowledge**, click the period (for example **FY 2026**) to open it. The line shows what changed and the date to acknowledge by.
2. Read the yellow banner. It gives the **Acknowledge By** date.
3. Under **What Changed**, read each change: the date, the target and the reason, what changed (for example *Weight changed: weight 1 → 2*), the **Share of score** before and after, and who made it.
4. Under **Your Score Now**, check how your score is made up today: a coloured bar, then each target's **Weight now**, **Counts as**, **Share of score** and **Share from today**.
5. Click **Acknowledge New Priorities**.

If you are happy with the change without reading the detail, you can also click **Acknowledge** straight from the line in **My Actions**.

## What happens next

- The status moves to **Acknowledged** and your to-do is closed. Your manager can see this under **My Team › Priority Changes**.
- If you do nothing, the daily job accepts the change on the day after the **Acknowledge By** date. The status becomes **Accepted automatically**.
- If you disagree with a change, talk to your manager. Acknowledging only confirms you have seen it; it does not change any score.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *These priorities were already acknowledged.* | You (or the daily job) already acknowledged them. | Nothing to do. Refresh the page. |
| *Only Employee A can acknowledge their priorities.* | Someone other than the employee clicked **Acknowledge New Priorities**. Managers can't acknowledge for you. | The employee acknowledges it. Managers can click **Remind** in **My Actions**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Your past reviews never change. A new weight counts from the day it applies.
- If more changes arrive before you acknowledge, they join the same priority change and the **Acknowledge By** date starts again.
- **Share of score** is for the whole period, so it moves slowly. **Share from today** is how much each target counts from now on.
:::
