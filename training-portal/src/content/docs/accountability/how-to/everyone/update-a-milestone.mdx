A milestone target is split into dated **stages**. When you finish a stage, you **mark it done**. Your manager then **confirms** it, and only then does it count towards your score.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-mark-stage-done" caption="Marking the third stage of a milestone target done. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Performance › My Targets** and find the milestone target. It has a **Mile.** badge.
2. Click the **Stages** line under the target (for example **Stages 1/3 confirmed**) to open the list of stages. Each stage shows its dates, its timing (such as *Due in 14 day(s)* or *Overdue by 2 day(s)*) and its status.
3. On the finished stage, click **Mark Done**. A window called **Mark Stage Done** opens.
4. Check **Completed On**. It defaults to today; change it to the day you actually finished. It can't be in the future.
5. Click **Save**.

If you marked the wrong stage, click **Withdraw** on it before your manager confirms it. The stage goes back to **Open**.

You can do the same from the target's form (the stages list has the same **Mark Done** button), or on the portal: open **/my/performance**, click the target, pick the date next to the stage and click **Mark Done**.

## What happens next

- The stage moves to **Awaiting Confirmation**. Your manager gets a *Confirm Milestone Stage* to-do. If you have no manager with a user account, accountability administrators get it.
- Your manager either:
  - clicks **Confirm**: the stage becomes **Confirmed**, you get a notice, and its weight counts towards the target's score straight away; or
  - clicks **Send Back** with a reason: the stage returns to **Open** and shows *Sent back:* followed by the reason. Fix what's needed, then mark it done again.
- Reminders: you are reminded 7, 3 and 1 days before an open stage is due. Once it is overdue, you and your manager are reminded every week.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *The completed date can't be in the future.* | **Completed On** is after today. | Use the date you actually finished. Mark the stage done once it is finished. |
| *This stage can't be updated: its target isn't live, or the period is closed.* | The target is still **Draft**, is closed, or the period has closed. | Ask your manager to activate the target. Closed periods can't be changed. |
| *Only the target's owner can do this.* | Someone other than the target holder tried to mark the stage done. | The person who holds the target marks it done. |
| *This stage isn't open.* | The stage is already awaiting confirmation or confirmed. | Refresh the page and check its status. |
| *Once a target is live, only the manager or an Accountability Administrator can change its stages.* | You tried to rename, re-date or add a stage on a live target. | Ask your manager to change the stage. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Only **Confirmed** stages count. A stage that is **Awaiting Confirmation** adds nothing to your score yet.
- On a target scored "done or not done", you score 100% only when every stage is confirmed.
- A warning such as *Starts before System configured (#2)* means a stage starts before one listed above it. It is a warning only and does not change your score.
- Once a stage is confirmed you can't undo it. Only an accountability administrator can reopen it.
:::
