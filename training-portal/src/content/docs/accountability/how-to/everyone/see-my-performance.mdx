The **Performance Overview** is the first screen you see when you open **Accountability**. It shows your score for the period, your rating, and how many of your targets have been reviewed. Use it whenever you want a quick answer to "how am I doing?".

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-performance-overview" caption="The Performance Overview with your own row selected. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the **Accountability** app. It opens on **My Performance › Performance Overview**. You can also reach it from the menu at any time.
2. In the dark header, check the period. The active period has a dot after its name (**FY 2026 ●**). To look back, pick another period, or pick a month end in the **As at** list instead of **As at today (live)**.
3. Read the tiles across the top: **Org Composite**, **Employees**, **Targets**, **Reviewed**, and one tile per rating level. For staff without a team these describe only you.
4. Click your name in the list (or switch to **Organogram** or **Focus**). A panel opens on the right.
5. In the panel, read your scores:
   - **Own**: the score from your own targets.
   - **Team**: shown only if you manage people. It is the average of your team's scores.
   - **Composite**: your overall score. The line under the tiles says how it is made up, for example *Composite = 100% Own Score*.
6. Scroll the panel to **Targets**. The count in brackets says how many have been reviewed, for example **(2/4 reviewed)**. Each target shows its cadence, actual against target, and score.

To act on a target (see its stages, request a review), open **My Performance › My Targets**. See [Check my targets](/accountability/how-to/everyone/my-targets/).

## What happens next

- Scores are recalculated every night, and straight away when a review is signed off or a stage is confirmed.
- **As at** a past month end rebuilds the picture from history. Click **Open snapshot** in the panel to see that date's detail; if a frozen copy exists the button reads **❄ Compare with frozen**. See [Read my progress briefs and snapshots](/accountability/how-to/everyone/read-briefs-and-snapshots/).
- Your line manager, their managers, HR and accountability administrators can see the same figures for you.

:::note[Good to know]
- Staff without a team see only themselves here. Managers see themselves and everyone below them. HR and administrators see the whole organisation.
- A target counts towards your score only once it has a result (a signed-off review, an actual entered, or a confirmed stage). Until then your score can look low early in the year.
- The line under the score tiles always shows the default split (*40% Own + 60% Team Avg*) for managers, even if HR has changed the split in the settings. The score itself uses the settings.
:::
