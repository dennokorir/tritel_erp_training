A **review session** is one sitting in which you review all your direct reports for one finished window, for example **Q3 2026**. Each person gets one **review** in the session, holding a line for every target that is due. You plan the session here, then score it (see [Conduct a review](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/conduct-a-review/)).

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-review-session" caption="A new review session for a quarter, before its targets are loaded. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Team › Review Sessions**. The board opens on **My Sessions**. Switch to the list view and click **New**. (Shortcut: on **Organisation › Session Coverage**, click a **✕ Not run** cell in your row to start a session already filled in.)
2. Choose the **Review Cadence**. A quarterly session also picks up monthly and weekly targets; a monthly session picks up monthly and weekly ones.
3. Check the **Review Cutoff**: the last day of the window you are reviewing. It defaults to the latest finished window, and **Covers** shows the start and end. Each target is reviewed for its own last window ending by the cutoff (for example September for a monthly target in a Q3 session).
4. Leave **Scope** on **All my direct reports**, or choose **Selected direct reports** and pick the people under **Employees**.
5. Save. The session is named from the window, for example *Q3 2026 — Quarterly Review*. The **Employees** tab is empty for now.
6. Click **Populate & Start**. Your reports' due targets are loaded, one review per person appears on the **Employees** tab, and the session moves to **In Progress**.

**Launch Session** starts the session without loading targets. Use **Refresh Targets** later to load them.

## What happens next

- The session stays **In Progress** while you score. **Refresh Targets** adds any targets added since, and drops lines for targets that were withdrawn or fully cascaded.
- The session moves to **Done** by itself once every person's review has been submitted.
- A session that is not **Done** more than 7 days after the cutoff is marked **Late** (the card shows, for example, *Late · 10 days*). Managers above you and HR see this on **Session Coverage**.
- While nothing has been submitted you can still use **Back to Draft** to change the cadence, cutoff or scope. Changing the cutoff or cadence clears the figures already entered.
- People exempt from accountability on the cutoff date are left out of **All my direct reports**. If you pick one under **Selected direct reports** you get the warning *Exempt from accountability*: only their development targets are reviewed, and they never count.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *The cutoff must end a finished quarterly window. Q4 2026 has not ended yet; the latest you can review is Q3 2026 (cutoff 2026-09-30).* | The cutoff is in a window that is still running. | Pick the latest finished window shown in the message. |
| *Q3 2026 — Quarterly Review already exists for Employee B (In Progress). Open that session instead.* | You already have a session for this cadence and cutoff. | Open the existing session from the board. |
| *No accountability period contains the cutoff 2026-09-30. Create the period first or choose another cutoff.* | No period covers that date. | Choose another cutoff, or ask your administrator. |
| *Employee D is not a direct report of Employee B. Sessions follow the org chart.* | You picked someone who doesn't report to you. | Remove them. If HR has the wrong manager on record, ask HR to fix it. |
| *You can only run review sessions for your own direct reports. Ask an accountability administrator to set up a session for another reviewer.* | You tried to set someone else as **Reviewer**. | Run sessions as yourself. |
| *2 review(s) from this session have already been created (…). A session can only be moved back while nothing has been finalised.* | You clicked **Back to Draft** or **Reopen** after submitting reviews. | Work on the submitted reviews instead. |

:::note[Good to know]
**Mark Done** closes the session straight away without any checks. Reviews still in draft stay in draft and those employees are not notified. To finish a session properly, submit the reviews (or use **Submit All Reviews**): the session then closes by itself.
:::
