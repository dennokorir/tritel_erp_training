When a target no longer applies, for example after a change of role or territory, you **withdraw** it. It stops counting from the date you choose. Any result it earned still counts for the days it was live, so nothing is lost.

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-withdraw" caption="Withdrawing a live target. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Team › Team Targets**, click the person, then click the target. In the **Assignment** section, click **Withdraw** next to **Weight**. The **Withdraw** window opens.
2. Set **From**: the day the target stops counting. It defaults to today and must fall inside the period.
3. Write why in the reason box. The employee sees it.
4. Read the effect on the person's **Overall rating score**.
5. Click **Submit**.

## What happens next

- **Before the target-setting deadline**, or if the period has none: the target is withdrawn at once. **After the deadline**: it waits for your manager or HR to approve it.
- **Once applied**: the target shows *Withdrawn from* the date, and *any result counts for the time it was live*. Its share of the score shrinks to the days it counted, and the other targets' shares grow.
- Shares of this target still waiting to be adopted are removed, and the people they were offered to are told. A review request on the target is set back, and it leaves **My Actions**.
- The employee gets a **Priority Change** to acknowledge.
- On **Team Targets**, withdrawn and closed targets are hidden. Tick **Show withdrawn and closed** to see them.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Give a reason for the change.* | The reason box was empty. | Write the reason. |
| *Choose a date inside FY 2026.* | **From** is outside the period, or on its first day. | Pick a date within the period. |
| *'Customer visits per month' was already withdrawn.* | It has been withdrawn before. | Nothing more to do. |
| *'Customer visits per month' already has an open change request (Change what it is measured against · Customer visits per month, waiting for approval). Decide or cancel it first.* | Another request is open on the target. | Wait for it, or cancel it. |
| *Only the employee's manager, the manager who cascaded it, HR or an accountability administrator can withdraw 'Customer visits per month'.* | You are not the person's manager. | Ask their manager. |
| *FY 2026 is closed: withdrawing a target is no longer possible for 'Customer visits per month'.* | The period has closed. | Nothing can change in a closed period. |
| *Only draft targets without reviews can be deleted. Withdraw 'Customer visits per month' instead, so its result keeps counting for the time it was live.* | You tried to delete a live target. | Withdraw it instead. |

:::note[Good to know]
The **Withdraw** link only appears on live targets, and only for the person's manager, the manager who cascaded the target or an accountability administrator. A development target (not scored) can't be withdrawn, because it never counts.
:::
