A milestone target is a piece of work delivered in **stages**, each with a due date. With weighted scoring, each confirmed stage adds its **Weight (%)** to the score. Your team member marks a stage done; it only counts once **you confirm it**.

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-milestones" caption="A milestone target with one stage awaiting confirmation, and the Send Stage Back window. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

### Set the dates before activating

Do this while the target is still a **Draft**: open it from **Team Targets** (click the target).

1. Under **Milestone / Task Target**, choose the **Scoring Mode** and set the target's **Start** and **End**. Both must fall inside the period.
2. In the stage list, add each stage with its **Stage** name, optional **Start**, **Due** date and **Weight (%)**. With **Weighted milestones (partial credit)** the weights must total 100. Every stage needs a due date between the target's start and end. Save, then activate the target as usual (see [Set targets for my team](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/set-team-targets/)).

### Confirm or send back a stage

3. When your team member marks a stage done, you get a **Confirm Milestone Stage** to-do. Open the target (from **My Actions** or **Team Targets**). The stage shows **Awaiting Confirmation**. On Team Targets, open **Stages** under the target to see the same list.
4. Check the work, then:
   - click **Confirm** if it is done. The stage becomes **Confirmed** and counts towards the score; or
   - click **Send Back**, write the **Reason** (what still needs doing) and click **Save**. The stage goes back to **Open** and the owner sees *Sent back:* with your reason.
5. Click **Timeline** on the target to see its stages on a chart. **My Team › Milestone Timeline** shows the stages of everyone in your reporting line.

## What happens next

- **Confirmed:** the stage's weight is added to the target's score at once, and the owner is told.
- **Sent back:** the owner is told, finishes the work and marks it done again.
- **Reminders:** the owner is reminded 7, 3 and 1 days before a stage is due. Once a stage is overdue, the owner and you are reminded every week.
- **On a live target** you can still change the target's dates and the stages' names and dates. The **Timing** column shows *Due in 14 day(s)*, *Overdue by 2 day(s)* and so on. A **Sequence check** warning such as *Starts before Build and test (#2)* is only a warning.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *'Launch the partner portal' isn't ready: - Add the target's start and end dates. - Stage 'Go live' needs a due date. - Stage weights must total 100% (they total 90%).* | Dates or stages are incomplete when activating. | Fix each item listed, save, then activate. |
| *Stage weights on a live target are locked: they decide its score.* | You changed a stage weight after activation. | Ask your accountability administrator to correct it. |
| *'Launch the partner portal' is live: its stages and their weights are part of what it is measured against. Ask an administrator to correct them.* | You added a stage to a live weighted target. | Ask your accountability administrator. |
| *Please say why the stage is being sent back.* | The **Reason** box was empty. | Write what still needs doing. |
| *This stage isn't waiting for confirmation.* | The owner withdrew the mark, or it was already confirmed. | Refresh the page. |
| *Only the owner's manager can confirm or send back this stage.* | You are not the owner's manager. | Their manager confirms it. |
| *This stage can't be updated: its target isn't live, or the period is closed.* | The target is a draft or closed, or the period has ended. | Nothing to confirm on this target. |
| *Only an Accountability Administrator can reopen a confirmed stage.* | You want to undo a confirmation. | Ask your accountability administrator. |

:::note[Good to know]
You never confirm your own stages, even if you are an administrator: the owner's manager does. If you confirmed a stage by mistake, only an accountability administrator can **Reopen** it.
:::
