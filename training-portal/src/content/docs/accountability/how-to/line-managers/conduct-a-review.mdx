In a review session each team member has one **review**. You record the result for every target line, add an overall comment and submit it. The employee then acknowledges it or raises a concern, which you answer.

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-review-pack" caption="One employee's review while you score it. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

### Score and submit

Open the session from **Accountability › My Team › Review › Review Sessions**. On the **Employees** tab, click **Review** on the person's row. Their review opens.

1. Work down the **Targets** tab. Each line shows the target, its **Cadence**, **Target (yours)** (what this person is measured on) and the window it covers.
2. Type the result in **Actual**. The **Score (%)** fills in and the line becomes **Done**. Click **ƒx** to see how the line's value and score are reached.
3. For the lines still **Pending**:
   - **Fetch** reads the value from the system for targets that have a metric. If there is nothing to measure in the window, the line becomes **Not Applicable** and is left out of the score.
   - **✓** accepts the value as it is, for example a genuine 0.
   - Milestone lines take their value from the confirmed stages and can't be typed.
   - Until you deal with it, a pending line counts as 0% in the average.
4. Click **Skip** for a target you won't review this time. Skipped lines are left out of the average. **Reopen** undoes Done, Skip or Not Applicable while the review is a draft.
5. Write your overall feedback in **Comment for the employee**. It appears at the top of their review.
6. Check **Avg Score (%)** and **Rating**. The line under them (and **How the average is reached**) explains which lines were counted.
7. Click **Submit Review** and confirm. Back on the session you can instead click **Submit All Reviews** to submit every review that has no pending line.

If a figure may include your team members' shares, an ⓘ or ⚠ icon appears on the line: the result must be this person's own result only.

### Answer a concern

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-answer-concern" caption="Answering a concern on one review. The numbers match the steps below." />

When the employee raises a concern you get an **Answer review concern** to-do, and their card on **Team Reviews** shows **Answer**.

1. Open the review (status **Concern Raised**) and click **Answer the Concern**.
2. Read the **Questioned targets** and what **The employee asked**.
3. Under **Your answer**, choose **Revise the scores** or **Keep the scores**.
4. Write your **Reply to the employee**.
5. Click **Send Answer**.
6. If you chose to revise, the review opens again on **Being Revised**. Only the questioned lines (marked **Questioned**) are open. Enter the corrected **Actual** values, then click **Resubmit Review** and confirm.

### Review a single target

When a team member clicks **Request Review** on one target, it moves to **Pending Review** and is listed in **My Actions** under **Targets waiting for review**. Open the target and click **Conduct Review**. Check **Covers From** and **Covers To** (**Previous window** steps back), type the **Actual Value** or click **Fetch value**, add **Notes** and a **Next Review Date**, then click **Sign Off**. The employee is asked to acknowledge it.

## What happens next

- **After Submit Review:** the review is **Awaiting Acknowledgment**. The employee gets one message with a link and an **Acknowledge your review** to-do, due on the **Acknowledge By** date (5 days by default). Each line's weight is fixed as submitted.
- **Reminders:** the employee is reminded 2 days before the deadline. You can also click **Send Reminder** on the review, or **Remind** on the session or the Team Reviews card.
- **If they acknowledge, or the deadline passes,** the review closes. The results are written to the targets and the scores are recalculated.
- **After Keep the scores:** the employee gets a new deadline to acknowledge, or to **Escalate** the review to your own manager (or HR) to decide.
- **After Resubmit Review:** the employee is told the review was revised and gets a new acknowledgment window.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Employee A: 1 target(s) are still pending. Enter a value (or press Done to accept 0), skip them or mark them not applicable before submitting: • Pipeline coverage* | A line is still **Pending**. | Type an actual, click **✓**, **Fetch** or **Skip** on the lines listed. |
| *Some reviews were not submitted* — *Still pending targets for: Employee C. Finish their lines, then submit again.* | **Submit All Reviews** skipped reviews with pending lines. | Finish those reviews, then submit again. |
| *'Customer visits per month' can't be changed: Employee A's review is awaiting acknowledgment.* | You tried to edit a submitted review. | Wait for the employee. If they raise a concern, revise it then. |
| *This figure comes from the confirmed stages and can't be typed over.* | You typed an actual on a milestone line. | Confirm the stages on the target instead. |
| *'Pipeline coverage' has no finished window by the session cutoff.* | The target's own window hasn't ended by the cutoff. | **Skip** the line this time. |
| *Only the reviewer (Employee B) can answer this review.* | Someone other than the reviewer clicked **Answer the Concern**. | The reviewer answers it. |
| *Reviews cover finished periods: the review period can't end in the current quarterly window. Use Previous window.* | In **Conduct Review**, the dates fall in a window that hasn't ended. | Click **Previous window**. |
| *Only Employee B (their direct manager) or an accountability administrator can review 'Customer visits per month'.* | You are not the person's direct manager. | Their direct manager reviews it. |

:::note[Good to know]
Two buttons are labelled **Sign Off** and they behave differently. In the **Conduct Review** window, **Sign Off** sends the review to the employee to acknowledge. On a review's own form (for example a draft review the system created from a metric, listed under **Reviews to confirm** in My Actions), **Sign Off** closes it straight away, without asking the employee. Check the figures before you use the second one.
:::
