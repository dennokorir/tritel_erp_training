**Team Scores** lists everyone in your team with their **Overall** score and rating. For managers in your team it also shows their **Own** and **Team** scores, and how the team score is put together.

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-team-scores" caption="Team Scores for a manager with three direct reports. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Team › Team Scores** and check the period. To see the team as it stood at a past month end, choose it in the **As at** list (for example *As at 30 Sep 2026 · quarter end*).
2. Choose whose scores to show: **Direct reports** or **Whole reporting line**. HR also sees **Everyone**.
3. Read the summary: **Team average (overall)**, **Where the team stands** (how many people sit in each rating) and **Biggest moves** compared with the previous closed period.
4. Click a rating chip (for example **Needs Improvement**) to list only those people. **All** shows everyone again. Use **Sort** to order by **Lowest rating first**, **Highest overall**, **Biggest change** or **Name**.
5. Read each row: **Overall**, **Own**, **Team** (managers only), **Status** (the rating), **vs last period** and **Targets scored**. Click **Scorecard** to open that person's targets.
6. For a manager, click **Team ▾**. **How the team score is reached** lists each team member's score and share of the period, and the **Overall score** line shows the own and team split.
7. To keep a record of today's figures, click **Freeze team snapshot**. Everyone listed gets a frozen snapshot that never changes.

**Export / list** opens the same scores as a normal list you can filter, group, pivot or export.

## What happens next

- Scores update by themselves when reviews are signed off, and again every night.
- Frozen snapshots appear under **My Performance › Performance Snapshots** and can be compared with today later. See [Snapshots and Compare](/accountability/reports/snapshots-and-compare/).
- A ❄ next to the status means a frozen snapshot exists for that date.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *You cannot freeze snapshots for: Employee B.* | You can freeze snapshots for people below you, never for yourself. | Ask your own manager or HR to freeze yours. |
| *No team scores* — *Nobody in this view has a score for FY 2026 yet.* | Nobody in the chosen view has a scored target in the period. | Choose another period, or wait for the first reviews. |

:::note[Good to know]
**Overall** is the score the rating is based on. While the period is open, running-total targets are counted *on pace* (against what should have been reached by their last review), so the figure can be higher than the score against the full-year target. When the two differ, the full-year figure is shown underneath as *full period*. A person with no scored target yet shows **No data yet**.
:::
