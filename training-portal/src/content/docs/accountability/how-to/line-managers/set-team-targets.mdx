A target only counts once it is **active** on the person's scorecard. New targets start as **Draft**. You, as the manager, check each draft and add it to the scorecard with **Activate**. The system records your reason and shows the effect on the person's overall score.

<Illustration name="accountability/mgr-team-targets" caption="Team Targets with a draft target and the Add to the scorecard window. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Team › Team Targets**. Check the period at the top (for example **FY 2026**). The chips beside it show how many people and targets are listed and the team's average.
2. In the **My Team** list on the left, click the person.
3. Find the target marked **Draft**. Click the target to open it and check the **Objective**, **Target Value**, **Unit**, **Review Cadence** and **Weight**. Change anything that is wrong while it is still a draft, then go back.
4. Click **Activate** on the target's row. The **Add to the scorecard** window opens.
5. Write why the target is being added in the reason box. The employee, the approver and HR all see it.
6. Read the effect line: the person's **Overall rating score** before and after. A new target counts once it has a result, so the score usually does not move yet.
7. Click **Submit**.

## What happens next

- **Before the period's target-setting deadline** (or when the period has no deadline): the change is applied at once. The target becomes **Active**, and your **Activate Target** to-do is closed.
- **After the deadline:** the request waits for approval. Your own manager and HR get an **Approve scorecard change** to-do, and either of them may approve it. The target stays a draft until then. A target activated after the deadline is also flagged as late-set on its history.
- **If the period has already started**, the new target changes how much the other targets count. The employee gets a **Priority Change** to acknowledge (*Your priorities changed: please acknowledge.*). If they don't, it is accepted automatically after the acknowledgment window.
- Every request is listed under **My Team › Scorecard Change Requests**, and HR receives a monthly summary.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Give a reason for the change.* | The reason box was empty. | Write a short reason and click **Submit** again. |
| *Only Employee B, the manager who cascaded it, HR or an accountability administrator can add 'Customer visits per month' to the scorecard.* | You are not this person's manager. | Ask their manager to activate it. |
| *'Customer visits per month' already has an open change request (Add to the scorecard · Customer visits per month, waiting for approval). Decide or cancel it first.* | A request for this target is already waiting. | Open it from **Scorecard Change Requests** and wait for the decision, or cancel it. |
| *'Launch the partner portal' isn't ready: - Add the target's start and end dates. …* | A milestone target is missing dates or stages, or its stage weights don't total 100%. | Fix the items listed. See [Set milestone dates and confirm milestones](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/milestone-dates-and-confirmations/). |
| *FY 2026 is closed: adding a target is no longer possible for 'Customer visits per month'.* | The period has been closed. | Targets can only be added in an open period. |
| *'Grow qualified pipeline' is live: change what it is measured against with 'Change target', which records the reason and its effect on the score.* | You tried to edit the value, type or objective of an active target. | Use [Request a scorecard change](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/request-a-scorecard-change/). |

:::note[Good to know]
Team Targets has no "new target" button. To create a target for someone, use **Propose Targets** (next year's targets), push a share with **Cascade ↓** from your own target, or open the objective under **Organisation › Objectives** and add a line on its **Targets** tab with the employee's name. Your team members can also add their own drafts from **My Targets**; you then get an **Activate Target** to-do.
:::
