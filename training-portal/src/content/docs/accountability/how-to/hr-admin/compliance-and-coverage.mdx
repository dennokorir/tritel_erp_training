Two screens show whether the process is being followed. **Session Coverage** answers "has every manager reviewed their team for each month or quarter?". **Compliance** answers "have cascaded targets been adopted?".

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-session-coverage" caption="Session Coverage. The numbers match steps 1 to 5." />

## Steps

### Session coverage

1. Open **Accountability › Organisation › Session Coverage**.
2. Pick the period and the cadence: **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Semi-annual** or **Annual**.
3. Read the grid: one row per manager, with their **Team** size, and one column per review window.
   - **✓ Done** with the session's average.
   - **◔ 75% scored**: in progress. **✎ Draft**: created but not started. Either may say *late 10 days*.
   - **✕ Not run**: the window has finished and there is no session.
   - **not yet due**, or **—** when no team targets were due in that window.
4. Check **Run on time**, for example **2 / 3**: finished windows whose session was done within 7 days of the cutoff, out of all finished windows. Green is 80% or more; red is under half.
5. Click a cell to open its session. On a **✕ Not run** cell, a manager (for their own row) or an administrator can click to start the missing session, already filled in.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-compliance" caption="Cascade Compliance. The numbers match steps 6 to 9." />

### Cascade compliance (administrators)

6. Open **Accountability › Organisation › Compliance** and pick the period.
7. Read the tiles: **Total Cascades**, **Adopted ✓**, **Pending ⏳**, **Declined ✗** and **Adoption Rate**.
8. Click an objective to open it. Under **Cascade Recipients**, each recipient's objective and targets are listed with their status: **Adopted**, **Pending** or **Declined**.
9. On a **Pending** target, click **Remind** to nudge the recipient.

## What happens next

- A session started from a **✕ Not run** cell opens as a draft for that manager, cadence and cutoff. The manager then runs it as normal. See [Plan a review session](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/plan-a-review-session/).
- **Remind** sends the recipient a reminder to adopt or decline the share. Unadopted cascades are also reminded automatically (see the cascade reminder interval in the settings).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *You can only run review sessions for your own direct reports. Ask an accountability administrator to set up a session for another reviewer.* | A manager started a missing session for someone else. | Only that manager or an administrator can start it. |
| *Sep 2026 — Monthly Review already exists for Employee B (Draft). Open that session instead.* | A session for that manager, cadence and cutoff already exists. | Open the existing session. |

:::note[Good to know]
- A window counts as expected when any direct report had a live target at that cadence or finer due in it. Exempt staff and development targets are left out.
- A session closed with **Mark Done** shows as done even if some reviews were never submitted. Open the session to check.
- HR managers can open sessions here but can't start or edit them.
:::
