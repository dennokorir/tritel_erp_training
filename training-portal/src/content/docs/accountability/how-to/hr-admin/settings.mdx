There is one settings page per company. It controls how scores are combined and rated, when people are reminded, and who decides escalated reviews.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-settings-form" caption="The Accountability settings. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › Organisation › Settings**. The company's settings open directly.
2. **Composite Score Weights**: a manager's overall score is **Own Performance Weight (%)** of their own score plus **Team Average Weight (%)** of their team's (40 and 60 by default). **Min. Headcount for Team Weighting** is how many direct reports a manager needs before the team part applies (2 by default).
3. **Performance Rating Scale**: set where each rating starts: **Needs Improvement from**, **Meets Expectations from**, **Exceeds Expectations from**, **Outstanding from**. Below the first is Unsatisfactory. Under **What each level means**, edit the description shown for each level.
4. **Progress Briefs**: tick **Send progress briefs** and choose **Brief checkpoints** (**Monthly** or **Quarterly**). **Send briefs now** sends the latest checkpoint's briefs at once, skipping anyone who already has one.
5. **Review Workflow**: **Acknowledgment Window (days)** is how long staff have to acknowledge a review before it is signed off automatically (5 by default). The same number of days applies to acknowledging new priorities.
6. **Automatic Metric Reviews**: **Metric Settle Period (days)** is how long to wait after a window closes before the automatic review is created, so late entries count. **Metric Review Confirmation Deadline (days)** is when the manager's *Confirm Metric Review* to-do falls due.
7. **Cascade Notifications** and **Notifications**:
   - **Cascade Action Deadline (days)**: due date for cascade to-dos. **Cascade Reminder Interval (days)**: how often to remind about unadopted cascades (0 turns reminders off).
   - **Review Escalation Contacts**: who decides an escalated review when the reviewer has no manager of their own. Leave empty to use HR.
   - **Notify on Standalone Employees**: who is told when someone has no manager and no team.
8. Save. Every change is logged at the bottom of the page.

## What happens next

- A new rating boundary re-rates every target and every score at once.
- New weights and the headcount apply at the next nightly recalculation.
- New deadlines apply to reviews and changes submitted from now on; existing deadlines are not moved.
- **Resync Access Rights** is covered in [Fix someone's access](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/access-rights/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *The rating boundaries must rise from left to right, between 0 and 100: Needs Improvement &lt; Meets &lt; Exceeds &lt; Outstanding.* | A boundary is lower than the one before it, or above 100. | Make each boundary higher than the last. |
| *Only Accountability Administrators can open the settings.* | Someone without the Administrator role opened Settings from a link. | Ask an administrator. |
| *This company already has accountability settings. Open them from the Settings menu.* | Someone tried to create a second settings page. | Use **Organisation › Settings**. |
| *Nobody is set up to handle escalations. Ask an administrator to set Review escalation contacts in the accountability settings.* | An employee escalated a review, but the reviewer has no manager and there are no contacts or HR users. | Add **Review escalation contacts**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The two weights are not checked to add up to 100. Keep them at a total of 100 yourself.
- Briefs are sent only for checkpoints reached in the last 7 days, and the 2-day reminder before a review deadline and the 7-day "late session" rule can't be changed here.
- The Performance Overview always describes managers' scores as *40% Own + 60% Team Avg*, even after you change the weights.
:::
