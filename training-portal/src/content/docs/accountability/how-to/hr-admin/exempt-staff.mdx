Some people take no part in accountability: interns, temporary staff, or someone on a long absence. Marking them **exempt** means no targets are expected of them, they get no score, and they are left out of their manager's team figures, cascades and review sessions for the exempt days.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-employee-exempt" caption="Exempting an intern. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the **Employees** app and open the person's record.
2. Open the **Performance** tab.
3. Under **Accountability exemption**, tick **Exempt from Accountability**.
4. Fill in the **Exemption Reason** (required), for example *Internship, May–Aug 2026*. If it is for a set time, fill in **Exempt From** and **Exempt Until**. Leave **Exempt From** empty for "since they joined" and **Exempt Until** empty for "until further notice".
5. Save. While the exemption applies, a grey banner at the top of the tab says the person is exempt.

To end an exemption early, set **Exempt Until** to their last exempt day. To remove it entirely, untick **Exempt from Accountability**; the dates are cleared.

## What happens next

- Scores for the person and everyone above them in the active period are recalculated straight away.
- Their manager's team score leaves out the exempt days. Team screens hide exempt staff unless the viewer ticks **Show exempt staff**.
- Review sessions set to **All my direct reports** leave out people exempt on the cutoff date. A manager who picks them by hand sees a warning.
- New targets created for them while exempt are marked **Development (not scored)**: they can be tracked and reviewed, but never count towards a score or objective.
- In the employee list, the **Exempt** column and the **Exempt from Accountability** filter show who is exempt today.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Give a reason for exempting Employee F from accountability.* | **Exemption Reason** is empty. | Write the reason. |
| *The exemption for Employee F ends before it starts.* | **Exempt Until** is before **Exempt From**. | Correct the dates. |
| *Only HR managers and accountability administrators can change exemptions.* | Someone else tried to change the exemption. | Ask HR. |
| *Only HR managers and accountability administrators can change whether a target is scored.* | Someone else ticked or unticked **Development (not scored)** on a target. | Ask HR. |
| *'Customer visits per month' is a development target (not scored), so rebalancing weights doesn't apply to it.* | A manager tried to rebalance or withdraw a development target. | Nothing to do: it doesn't count anyway. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Exempting someone does not change targets they already hold. If those should stop counting, tick **Development (not scored)** on each target, or withdraw them.
- Signed-off reviews and closed periods never change.
- The person's real reporting line is untouched; they still appear under their manager in the org chart.
:::
