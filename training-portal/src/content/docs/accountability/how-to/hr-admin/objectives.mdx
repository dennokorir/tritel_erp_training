**Objectives** are what the company wants to achieve in a period. They form a tree: a few top-level objectives, each with sub-objectives owned by departments or managers. Every target belongs to one objective, so objectives must exist before targets can be set.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-period-browser" caption="The Objectives screen. The numbers match steps 1 to 3." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › Organisation › Objectives**. The left panel lists periods; the active period is selected.
2. Click the period you want. The right panel lists its objectives with their level (**L0** is top level), owner, number of targets, **Achievement** and score.
3. Click **New Objective**. The objective form opens for the selected period.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-objective-form" caption="A new sub-objective. The numbers match steps 4 to 9." />

4. Type the objective's name.
5. Check the **Period**. For a sub-objective, choose its **Parent Objective**; leave it empty for a top-level one.
6. Set **Default Weight for New Targets**. Targets added under this objective start at this weight. Also check the **Achievement Threshold (%)** (70 by default): the average score at which the objective counts as **Achieved**.
7. Choose the **Owner**. The **Department** fills in from the owner; change it if needed.
8. Save, then click **Activate**.
9. To add sub-objectives, open the **Sub-objectives** tab and add lines. The **Targets** tab lists the targets set against this objective; the **Cascade** and **Reviews** tabs appear once there are cascades or reviews.

To copy last year's objectives instead of typing them, an administrator or HR clicks **Copy Last Year's Objectives** on the period. See [Open and close a period](/accountability/how-to/hr-admin/open-and-close-periods/).

## What happens next

- Active objectives can be chosen on targets. Managers then [set targets for their team](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/set-team-targets/).
- As targets are reviewed, the Objectives screen shows each objective's score. **Your Level** is the average for you and your reporting line; **Org** is the whole organisation.
- When you click **Close** on the objective, **Achievement** shows **Achieved**, **Partial Achievement** (at least three quarters of the threshold), **Not Achieved**, or **Not Measured** if no target has a result. Until then it reads **Not Set**.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *The default weight for new targets must be greater than zero.* | **Default Weight for New Targets** is 0 or negative. | Enter a positive number, such as 1. |
| *No objective 'Grow revenue' in FY 2027 yet. Copy last year's objectives first.* | A manager proposing next year's targets found no matching objective. | Copy or create the objective in the new period. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The default weight is a starting point only. A target's own weight decides its share of the score; the objective's weight is never used in a target score.
- Any line manager can create, activate and close objectives, in any department. Agree who maintains the tree, usually HR or the administrator, before the year starts.
- Closing a period does not close its objectives. Close each objective at year end, before the period is closed, so its achievement is worked out and kept.
:::
