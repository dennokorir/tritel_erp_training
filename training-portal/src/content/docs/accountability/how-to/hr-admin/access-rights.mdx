Nobody is given Accountability access by hand, except administrators. Access follows the employee record and the org chart:

- **User** (everyone): given automatically to anyone whose user account is linked to an active employee record.
- **Supervisor** (line managers): given automatically when that employee is the **Manager** of at least one active employee. It is taken away again when they no longer manage anyone.
- **Administrator**: given by hand. The automatic updates never add or remove it.

So when someone can't see something, the fix is almost always on their employee record, not in the access settings.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-access-employee" caption="The two fields that decide access. The numbers match steps 1 and 2." />

## Steps

1. Open the **Employees** app and find the person. Check they appear (an archived employee loses access) and check the **Manager** field. To make someone a line manager in Accountability, they must be the **Manager** on at least one active employee's record.
2. Open the **HR Settings** tab and check **Related User**. It must be the person's own user account. Without it, the **Accountability** app doesn't appear for them at all. Save any change; access updates straight away.

<Illustration name="accountability/howto-access-resync" caption="Resyncing access rights. The numbers match steps 3 and 4." />

3. If access still looks wrong (for example after employees were imported in bulk), open **Accountability › Organisation › Settings** and click **Resync Access Rights**.
4. A message confirms it: *Access rights synced* and how many users were updated *from the HR org chart*.
5. To give someone the **Administrator** role, open **Settings › Users & Companies › Users**, open their user, and on the **Access Rights** tab set **Accountability Framework** to **Administrator**. To take it away, set it back to **User**, save, then click **Resync Access Rights** so the automatic role is worked out again.
6. Ask the person to reload the page in their browser.

### When someone can't see a menu

| They can't see | Who sees it | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| The **Accountability** app | Everyone with a linked employee record | **Related User** on their employee record; the employee is not archived |
| **My Team** (Team Targets, Cascade Management, Milestone Timeline) | Line managers | They are the **Manager** of at least one active employee |
| **My Team** (Review Sessions, Team Reviews, Team Scores, Propose Targets, Priority Changes, Scorecard Change Requests) | Line managers and HR | As above, or they have HR rights in **Employees** |
| **Organisation › Objectives** | Line managers | As above |
| **Organisation › Session Coverage**, **Targets Library**, **Scorecard Changes** | HR (Session Coverage also line managers) | HR rights in **Employees**, or the Administrator role |
| **Organisation › Periods**, **Compliance**, **Metrics**, **Settings** | Administrators only | The **Administrator** role (step 5) |

## What happens next

- Changing **Related User**, **Manager** or archiving an employee updates access for the people involved at once, including the old and new manager.
- Someone who is newly a **Manager** gets the line-manager menus; someone who no longer manages anyone loses them.
- Administrators keep everything a line manager has, whatever the org chart says.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Only Accountability Administrators can resync access rights.* | Someone without the Administrator role clicked **Resync Access Rights**. | Ask an administrator. |
| *Only Accountability Administrators can open the settings.* | Someone without the Administrator role opened the settings from a link. | Ask an administrator. |

:::note[Good to know]
- HR staff who have no employee record of their own don't see the **Accountability** app, even with HR rights. Link them to an employee record.
- Portal-only users (such as board members) are never given Accountability access by the automatic updates.
- Seeing someone's scores also follows the org chart: you see your own, your whole reporting line's, and HR and administrators see everyone's.
:::
