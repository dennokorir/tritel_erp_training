A **Milestone / Task** target is scored from its stages. The **Scoring Mode** on the target decides how.

## The rule

| Scoring Mode | Score |
|---|---|
| **Weighted milestones (partial credit)** | The sum of the **Weight (%)** of every stage with status **Confirmed**. Stage weights must add up to 100. |
| **Binary — done / not done** | 100% when every stage is **Confirmed**, otherwise 0%. |
| **Supervisor assessment (0–100)** | The **Supervisor Score (0–100)** your manager types. Anything outside 0–100 is treated as 0 or 100. |

Only **Confirmed** stages count. When you click **Mark Done**, the stage moves to **Awaiting Confirmation**. It adds to the score only once your manager clicks **Confirm**.

## Edge cases

- **No confirmed stage yet.** A weighted or binary target with no confirmed stage has no result, so it doesn't count in your own score yet.
- **Sent back.** If your manager clicks **Send Back**, the stage returns to **Open** with the reason in **Sent Back Because**. It adds nothing until it is marked done and confirmed again.
- **Reopened.** An accountability administrator can **Reopen** a confirmed stage (*Undo this confirmation? The stage stops counting towards the score.*). The score drops by that stage's weight.
- **Late stages still count.** Finishing a stage after its **Due** date doesn't reduce the score. The **Timing** column shows it, for example "Completed 4 day(s) late".
- **Stage weights are locked once the target is live.** *Stage weights on a live target are locked: they decide its score.* To change them, ask an administrator.
- **Reviews of a milestone target** record the completion % from the confirmed stages. It can't be typed over: *This figure comes from the confirmed stages and can't be typed over.*

:::note[Good to know]
In **Supervisor assessment** mode there are no stages to tick. The score is whatever the manager types in **Supervisor Score (0–100)**, and it counts once a score above 0 has been entered.
:::
