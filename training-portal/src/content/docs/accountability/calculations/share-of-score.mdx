The **Share of Score** section on a target shows how much that target counts in your score. There are two figures, because weights can change during the year.

## The rule

- **Share of Score (%)** (the whole period) = this target's **Counts As** ÷ the total **Counts As** of all your live and finished targets in the period × 100. See [time-weighted weights](/accountability/calculations/weights-over-time/).
- **Share From Today (%)** = this target's weight today ÷ the total weight of your targets that are live today × 100.

The label next to **Share of Score** shows the share from today, for example *40% of the score from today (weight 2 of 5)*, and the **Rank**, for example *#1 of 3*. The text below the bar gives the whole-period share.

<Illustration name="accountability/calc-share-of-score" caption="Three targets for Employee A. Over the year the revenue target is 53.2% of the score; from today it is 40%." />

## The hint under the bar

The hint tells you what a better result is worth: *A 10-point gain on this target adds about 4.0 points to the FY 2026 score.* That is the whole-period share ÷ 10.

For a line manager, the hint adds the own-score part: *Own score is 40% of the overall score, so about 1.6 points overall.* That is share ÷ 10 × the **Own Performance Weight (%)**.

## Edge cases

- **Drafts** show *Draft: counts once activated* and have no share.
- **Live targets with no result yet** are included in the shares, but they don't count in your score until a value is in. The bar segment is hatched and the hint adds *Not scored yet: it carries its share once a value is in.*
- **Finished targets** (withdrawn, closed or fully cascaded) show *No longer counts from 30 Sep 2026* and keep their whole-period share if they had a result.
- **Targets that ended with no result** get a share of 0: *It ended with no result, so it is not part of the score.*
- **Shares from today always add up to 100%.** In **Rebalance Weights** each target needs at least 1%: *Every target needs at least 1%: Customer visits.*
- **Any change to the shares** (a new target, a weight change, a withdrawal or a close) asks you to acknowledge your new priorities.

:::note[Good to know]
The share shown includes live targets that have no result yet, but your own score is worked out only from targets that have one. Early in the year, a target with a large share may not affect your score at all until its first review.
:::
