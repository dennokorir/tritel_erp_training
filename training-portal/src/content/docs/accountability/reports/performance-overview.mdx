**Accountability › My Performance › Performance Overview** is the first screen of the Accountability app. Its title is **Org Performance Cascade**. It shows people as a tree that follows the reporting lines, each with their scores, and a side panel with the details for one person.

What you see depends on who you are. Accountability administrators and HR see the whole organisation. Everyone else sees themselves and the people below them, so a manager sees their branch and a team member sees only their own card.

<Illustration name="accountability/report-performance-overview" caption="The Performance Overview as at a quarter end, with one manager's panel open. The numbers match the list below." />

## Reading the screen

1. **Period.** Choose the period at the top; the active one is marked ●.
2. **As at.** *As at today (live)* shows today's figures. Pick a month end (for example *As at 30 Sep 2026 · quarter end*) to see the organisation as it stood then. Every figure is rebuilt from the dated history, and the tree follows the reporting lines of that date. A banner says so.
3. **Views.** **List**, **Organogram** and **Focus** show the same people in different layouts. **+ Expand All** / **− Collapse All** opens or closes every branch. Each card shows the person's composite score, **T** (targets reviewed / targets) and **O** (objectives).
4. **KPI strip** (for everyone shown):
   - **Org Composite**: the simple average of the composite scores of everyone with a scored target.
   - **Employees**, **Targets** and **Reviewed** (targets that are Reviewed or Closed).
   - One tile per rating, with its range, counting how many people sit in it.
   - **Standalone**: people with no manager and no team, flagged for HR.
5. **A person.** Click a card to open the side panel.
6. **The side panel** shows the person's role and scoring type (**Top Level**, **Manager**, **Individual Contributor** or **Standalone**), their **Own**, **Team** (managers only) and **Composite** scores, and then **Team Summary**, **Objectives**, **Targets** (with how many are reviewed) and **Direct Reports** (click one to move to them). **Open snapshot** (or **❄ Compare with frozen** when one exists) opens the person's snapshot for the chosen date.
7. **❄ Freeze organisation snapshot** (managers, HR and administrators) saves a frozen snapshot, as at the chosen date, for everyone shown that you are allowed to freeze. Add an optional note and click **Freeze**. People you can't freeze are skipped and counted in the message.

Tick **Trend** to add a line with the organisation average at each month end. Tick **Show exempt staff** to show people who are exempt; they are never counted in the figures.

## What the scores mean

- **Own**: the weighted average of the person's scored targets.
- **Team**: for managers, the average of their team members' results, weighted by how much of the period each one reported to them.
- **Composite**: for managers, own and team combined using the split in the Accountability settings. For everyone else it equals the own score.

The full rules are in [Own, Team and Overall score](/accountability/calculations/own-team-overall/).

:::note[Good to know]
The line under the scores in the side panel always reads *Composite = 40% Own + 60% Team Avg* for managers. That is the default split. If your administrator has changed the split in the settings, the score itself uses the new split; only that line of text stays the same. **Team Scores** shows the split actually used.
:::
