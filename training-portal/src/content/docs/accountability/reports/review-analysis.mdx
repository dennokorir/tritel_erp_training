Two menus have analysis views for reviews. **My Team › Team Reviews** analyses individual reviews: how they closed, how long employees took and how often they raised concerns. **My Team › Review Sessions** analyses sessions: average scores, how much was scored and how soon after the cutoff they were done. Both show only what you are allowed to see: your team and the teams below you, or everything for HR.

<Illustration name="accountability/report-review-analysis" caption="Team Reviews in pivot view: turnaround by reviewer and by how the review closed. The numbers match the list below." />

## Team Reviews pivot

1. **Filters.** The default filter **Open + Closed in Last 30 Days** applies here too. Remove it, or pick **Closed**, to analyse a longer stretch.
2. **Measures.** **Turnaround (days)**, **Concerns** and **Avg Score (%)** are shown. Use **Measures** to add or remove one.
3. **Pivot view.** Switch to the pivot view with the view buttons at the top right.
4. **The table.** One row per **Reviewer**, one column per **Closed By**: **Acknowledged by Employee**, **Signed Off Automatically** or **Closed after Escalation**.

What the measures mean:

- **Turnaround (days)**: from the moment the review was submitted to the moment it closed. For example, submitted 1 Oct at 09:00 and acknowledged 3 Oct at 21:00 is 2.5 days. Each cell shows the average.
- **Concerns**: how many concerns were raised on the reviews.
- **Avg Score (%)**: each review's weighted average score.

## Review Sessions pivot and graph

Open **My Team › Review Sessions** and switch view:

- **Pivot**: one row per reviewer, one column per cutoff month, with **Avg Score (%)**, **Scored (%)** and **Days from Cutoff to Done**.
- **Graph**: **Average Session Score** by cutoff month.
- **Calendar**: sessions on their cutoff dates.
- **Gantt** (**Review Cycle Timeline**): each session as a bar from the start of its window to its cutoff, with the share scored.

What the measures mean:

- **Avg Score (%)**: the simple average of the employees' review averages. Each employee counts once.
- **Scored (%)**: lines no longer pending ÷ all lines. Skipped and Not Applicable lines count as scored.
- **Days from Cutoff to Done**: for example, cutoff 30 Sep and done on 9 Oct is 9.0 days.

:::note[Good to know]
In the Team Reviews pivot, **Avg Score (%)** is added up across the reviews in a cell rather than averaged, so a cell with several reviews can show more than 100. Read scores per review, or use the Review Sessions pivot, where **Avg Score (%)** is averaged. Reviews that are still open have a turnaround of 0, so pick the **Closed** filter before reading turnaround.

The same review behaviour can be measured on managers' own scorecards. Metrics such as *Team review coverage (direct reports)*, *Review acknowledgment rate*, *Review acknowledgment turnaround* and *Review concern response time* read these figures. See [How metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).
:::
