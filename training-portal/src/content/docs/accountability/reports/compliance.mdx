**Accountability › Organisation › Compliance** opens the **Cascade Compliance** screen. It shows whether the targets managers pushed down have been taken up by their teams. HR and accountability administrators have this menu. Line managers follow their own cascades on **My Team › Set targets › Cascades**.

<Illustration name="accountability/report-compliance" caption="Cascade Compliance for one period with one objective opened. The numbers match the list below." />

## Reading the screen

1. **Period.** Choose the period at the top right.
2. **Tiles.** **Total Cascades** counts every target cascaded from another one in the period, at every level of the chain. **Adopted ✓**, **Pending ⏳** (pushed and waiting to be adopted) and **Declined ✗** split it up, and **Adoption Rate** is adopted ÷ total. Pull requests not yet approved count in the total but in none of the three tiles.
3. **Objectives.** One row per top-level objective, with *adopted/total adopted* and a small bar: amber while anything is pending, green when it isn't. Click the row to open it.
4. **The chains.** Inside an objective, each source target is shown with its owner and value, and below it every person it was cascaded to, with their value and status. On a **Pending** line, click **Send Reminder** to remind that person to adopt or decline.
5. **Declined** lines stay listed so you can follow them up with the manager who pushed the target.

:::note[Good to know]
When a period has no cascades the screen says *Use Cascade ↓ from My Targets to start cascading.* The **Cascade ↓** button is on the target itself: open the target from **My Targets** first. See [Push a target down, or approve a pull](/accountability/how-to/line-managers/push-and-pull-targets/).

Only the first level below each source target is listed. If a recipient cascades their share further, those targets are counted in the tiles but not shown in the list.
:::
