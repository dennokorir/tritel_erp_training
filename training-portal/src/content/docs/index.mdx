## The systems

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Accountability Framework" icon="star">
    Targets, scores and reviews for every member of staff. Everyone uses it for their own targets; line managers set targets and run reviews; HR runs the periods.

    [Open the Accountability guide →](/accountability/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Project Delivery" icon="rocket">
    Turns a won tender into a delivery project with an approved budget, then tracks spend, handover and close-out. Used by Delivery and Finance & Accounts.

    [Open the Projects guide →](/projects/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tenders and Channel" icon="document">
    Every bid from opportunity to result, the bid desk, project summaries, customer activities, and OEM bookings. Used by Commercial and Channel.

    [Open the Tenders guide →](/tenders/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Board portal" icon="seti:pipeline">
    What board members see: performance, financial position, payroll, profitability, cheques to sign, and the MD's quarterly review.

    [Open the Board guide →](/board/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time Off" icon="sun">
    Leave balances, requests and approvals, who is in with Team Presence, and allocations for HR. Everyone uses it for their own leave; line managers approve; HR keeps the allocations and settings.

    [Open the Time Off guide →](/leave/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How the pages work

Every task page starts with **who can do it**, **where to find it** and **what you need first**. The picture of the screen has numbered markers that match the numbered steps below it.

<Illustration name="general/form-parts" caption="Numbers on a picture match the numbered steps in the text." />

Can't find something? Use **Search** at the top (Ctrl + K), the [A–Z index](/reference/index/) or the [glossary](/reference/glossary/).
