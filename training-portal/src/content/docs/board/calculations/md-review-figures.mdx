Each target in the MD's quarterly review is one card. The card has a window (the dates it covers), a result proposed by the system or the MD, and a score. The board member accepts or changes the result and signs off.

## The window

The review covers the calendar quarter that has just ended, for example 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 for Q3 2026. Each card's window is that quarter, trimmed to the accountability period. Two exceptions:

- A target measured **to date** (a running total, such as revenue for the year so far) covers the period start to the quarter end, for example 1 Jan to 30 Sep 2026.
- A target that was not live during the quarter is left out of the review.

## The proposed result

- **Calculated by the system**: the target has a metric. The system works it out over the window when the review opens, the day after the quarter ends, and shows **How It Was Calculated** to the board. The MD can't change it.
- **Entered by** the MD: the MD types the result and a comment.
- **Nothing to measure**: the metric found no data for the window. The card shows the reason and has no result to sign.
- If a metric fails, the card falls back to a result the MD enters.

## The score

The score uses the same rules as any review:

| Target type | Score |
|---|---|
| Higher is better | Result ÷ target × 100, at most 100% |
| Lower is better | Target ÷ result × 100, at most 100%. A result of 0 scores 100% |
| Milestone | The result is read as % complete, kept between 0 and 100 |
| Threshold (SLA) | **At or above floor**: 0 below the floor, 100% at or above the target, in proportion in between. **At or below ceiling** works the same way downwards. **Within range**: 100% inside the range, 0 outside |
| Nothing to measure | Not scored |

<Illustration name="board/b2-calc-md-review" caption="A revenue target: the board raises the result, so the score rises and a comment is required." />

The portal shows the proposal next to the box: *Result (proposed 90,000,000.00, scores 75.0%)*.

More examples, all with dummy figures:

- Lower is better, target 5 incidents, result 8: 5 ÷ 8 × 100 = **62.5%**.
- Milestone, result 130: **100%**. Result 75: **75%**.
- Threshold with floor 80 and target 95, result 90: (90 − 80) ÷ (95 − 80) × 100 = **66.7%**.

## When the board must comment

When the board member's result differs from the proposed one (by more than half a cent), a comment is required. Without one, **Sign Off** stops with *Say why you changed the result for Grow revenue.* Commas in the result are fine: *96,000,000* is read as 96000000. An empty box or text stops it with *Enter a result for Grow revenue.*

## What the sign-off writes

For every card with a result, the sign-off creates a signed-off review of the MD's target for the window, with the board result as the actual and both comments as notes. From then on the target's score, history, snapshots and rating work as they do for anyone. See [target scores](/accountability/calculations/target-scores/). A *Nothing to measure* card becomes a review marked not applicable.

## Edge cases

- **The score is a preview.** The review card's score uses the formulas above. The target's own score after sign-off follows the target's review rules, for example averaging several reviews on a running total.
- **Shared targets.** If the MD passed part of a target down to the team, the card still shows the full **Target**, but the score is worked out against the part the MD keeps.
- **Late postings.** A metric result is fixed when the review opens. Invoices posted after that are not picked up; the board can change the result with a comment.
- **A result of 0.** The MD must write a comment on every entered card, but a result of 0 is accepted. Check such cards before signing.
