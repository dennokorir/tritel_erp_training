The Payroll tab adds up payslip lines. It uses payslips of the company that are **done** or **paid**, are not refunds, and whose pay period lies inside the year you picked: 1 January to 31 December, or to today for the current year.

## Which payslip lines

| Figure | Payslip line (salary rule code) |
|---|---|
| Gross pay | P030 |
| Net pay | P120 |
| PAYE | P105 |
| Employee NSSF | P055 |
| Employer NSSF | P065 |
| NHIF/SHA | P106 |
| Employee Housing Levy | P110 |
| Employer Housing Levy | P118 |

## The figures

- **YTD Gross Payroll** = gross pay on all those payslips.
- **Net Pay to Employees** = net pay on them.
- **Total Employer Cost** (and **Total Cost to Company**) = gross pay + employer NSSF + employer Housing Levy.
- **Active Headcount** = the number of different employees with at least one of those payslips.
- **vs prior year** = (gross − last year's gross) ÷ last year's gross × 100, to one decimal. Last year runs from 1 January to the same day and month. With no payslips last year: *No prior-year data*.
- **Pay Composition** = net pay, PAYE, employee NSSF, NHIF/SHA and employee Housing Levy, each as a share of those five added together.
- **Payroll by Department** = gross pay by the department on each payslip, with **% of Total** = the department's gross ÷ **YTD Gross Payroll**. Payslips with no department appear as *Unassigned*.

Amounts on the tiles are shown in millions to two decimals (10.35M) or, below a million, in thousands (350K).

## Edge cases

- **The current month**: a payslip counts only once its pay period has ended, so mid-month the current month's payslips are missing from every figure, and from last year's comparison too.
- **Draft or refund payslips** never count.
- **Employer pension or other benefits** outside these lines are not in **Total Employer Cost**.
- **Pay Composition** won't add up to gross pay when there are other deductions, such as loans or pension, which are not shown.
- **Headcount** counts anyone paid during the year, including staff who have left.
