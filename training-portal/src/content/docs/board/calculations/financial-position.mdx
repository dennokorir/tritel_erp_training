The Financial Position is a snapshot of today. It adds up what is still open, whenever it started, and converts everything to KES.

## The totals

- **Receivables** = what is unpaid on posted customer invoices (not paid or partly paid) + what confirmed sales orders have not yet invoiced.
- **Payables** = the same for posted vendor bills and confirmed purchase orders.
- **Net Position** = Receivables − Payables. **Favourable** when 0 or more, **Adverse** below 0.

## One order: backlog and exposure

For each confirmed order that is still waiting to be invoiced (or billed), or waiting for delivery (or receipt):

- **Total** = the order total including tax, converted to KES at the rate of the order date.
- **Invoiced** (or **Billed**) = posted invoices on the order minus posted credit notes, converted at each invoice's date.
- **To Invoice** (or **Unbilled**) = Total − Invoiced, never below 0. This is the order's backlog, and the only part added to Receivables or Payables. Orders with nothing left to invoice are not listed.
- **Residual** = what is unpaid on the order's invoices.
- **Total Exposure** = To Invoice + Residual: everything still to come from (or go to) that party on the order.

## Aging

| Band | Invoices | Orders |
|---|---|---|
| **Current (0–30 d)** | Up to 30 days past the due date, including not yet due | Up to 30 days since the order date |
| **31–60 days** | 31 to 60 days past due | 31 to 60 days old |
| **61–90 days** | 61 to 90 days past due | 61 to 90 days old |
| **Over 90 days** | More than 90 days past due | More than 90 days old |

An invoice with no due date is aged from its invoice date.

## Orders left out

| Flag on the order | Order backlog in the totals | Listed | Its invoices |
|---|---|---|---|
| None | Yes | In the order list | Counted |
| **Exclude from Board Position** | No | In **Excluded from position report**, with the reason | Counted |
| **Hide from Board Portal** | No | Nowhere on the Financial Position | Counted |

## Edge cases

- **Credit notes on an order** reduce what counts as invoiced, so the backlog can grow back.
- **Over-invoiced orders** have a backlog of 0, never a negative one.
- **Foreign currency**: orders are converted at the order date's rate and invoices at the invoice date's rate, so on a part-invoiced foreign-currency order the backlog in KES can differ a little from the order's own figures.
- **Tax**: order and invoice amounts here include tax.
- **The Performance tab's dates** don't apply, except in the invoice lists opened from **Sales →** or **Purchases →**, which keep only invoices dated in that period.
