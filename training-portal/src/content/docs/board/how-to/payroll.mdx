The **Payroll** tab shows the year's staff costs from confirmed payslips: no names, only totals by month and by department.

<Illustration name="board/b2-payroll" caption="The Payroll tab for 2026 to date. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Click **Payroll**, then a year under **Year**. The current year runs from 1 January to today; a past year runs to 31 December. The dates are under the **Payroll** heading.
2. Read the four tiles:
   - **YTD Gross Payroll**: gross pay on the year's payslips, with the change against the same months of last year (for example *+11.1% vs prior year*), or *No prior-year data*.
   - **Net Pay to Employees**: what staff took home, *After all statutory deductions*.
   - **Total Employer Cost**: *Gross + ER NSSF + Housing Levy (ER)*.
   - **Active Headcount**: the number of different employees *With confirmed payslips this period*.
3. **Employer add-ons** breaks the employer's extra cost into **Employer NSSF** and **Housing Levy (ER)**, and repeats the **Total Cost to Company**.
4. **Monthly Gross vs Net Pay** shows a pair of bars for each month: **Gross Pay** and **Net Pay**.
5. **Pay Composition** splits pay into **Net Pay**, **PAYE**, **NSSF**, **NHIF/SHA** and **Housing**, each with its share. The figure in the middle is gross pay.
6. **Payroll by Department** lists each department's **Headcount**, **YTD Gross** and **% of Total** gross, largest first.

## What happens next

Nothing changes in Odoo. See [Payroll employer cost](/board/calculations/payroll-cost/) for how each figure is worked out.

:::note[Good to know]
- Only payslips that are done or paid count. Draft payslips and refund payslips don't.
- A payslip counts once its whole pay period has ended. In the middle of November, the November payslips are not in the figures yet, even if they are confirmed.
- If there are no confirmed payslips for the year, the tiles show 0 and **Payroll by Department** says *No payroll data for this period.*
- **Pay Composition** shares are of the five parts added together, not of gross pay, so other deductions (such as loans) are not shown.
:::
