The **Performance** tab is the board's summary of the company: invoiced revenue and spend, tenders, the organisation's accountability results and the biggest expense accounts. You open it at the start of a board meeting or when you prepare the board pack.

<Illustration name="board/b2-performance" caption="The Performance tab for Q3 2026. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Under **Period**, click a year. The page shows 1 January to 31 December of that year, and the badge at the top right shows the year.
2. To narrow it to a quarter, click **Q1**, **Q2**, **Q3** or **Q4** under **Quarter**. The dates under **E-Board** change to that quarter.
3. Read the five headline tiles:
   - **Revenue**: the total of posted customer invoices dated in the period, tax included. The caption counts the invoices and the sales orders confirmed in the period.
   - **Total Spend**: the same for posted vendor bills, with the purchase orders confirmed in the period.
   - **Net Position**: **Revenue** minus **Total Spend** (*Revenue minus spend*). Green when 0 or more, red below.
   - **Won Tenders**: the expected revenue of won tenders, with *won of total* underneath.
   - **Win Rate**: won tenders as a share of decided tenders. Green from 50%, amber from 30%, red below.
4. In the **Accountability** block, the **As at** list starts on **Today**. Pick a past month end to see the figures as they stood then. The page says *Figures as they stood on 30 Sep 2026, rebuilt from the reviews covering up to that date.*
5. Read the four accountability tiles: **Organisation Score**, **Objectives Meeting Expectations**, **Targets Reviewed** and **Staff with Targets**. See [Organisation score and rating](/board/calculations/organisation-score/).
6. **Strategic Objectives** lists each top-level objective with its **Score**, **%**, **Reviewed** (targets with a signed review, *x of y*) and **Status**. The weakest objectives come first.
7. Scroll down for **Commercial Performance — Tenders & Pipeline** (win rate, pipeline value and tenders by stage), **Revenue — Sales Invoices** and **Procurement — Purchase Invoices** (**Total Invoiced**, **Collected** or **Paid**, **Outstanding**, and the top five customers or vendors) and **Expenses by Account** (the eight biggest expense accounts on vendor bills). Click **Profitability →**, **Sales →** or **Purchases →** to open the detail.

## What happens next

- Nothing changes in Odoo: this page only reads figures. Each visit is recorded in the [access log](/board/how-to/access-log/).
- **Sales →** and **Purchases →** open the outstanding invoices dated in the period you picked. See [Read the Financial Position](/board/how-to/financial-position/).
- **Profitability →** opens the won tenders of the matching tender period. See [Read Profitability](/board/how-to/profitability/).

:::note[Good to know]
- The accountability figures build up over the year, so the quarter buttons don't change them. The page says so: *Scores build up over the year, so the quarter buttons don't change them.* To look back, use **As at** instead.
- If no accountability period covers your dates, the block shows *No accountability period covers the selected dates.*
- The **Organisation Score** and the rating word under it can tell different stories, for example 57% with *Meets Expectations*. The word judges running totals such as revenue against what is expected by now. See [Organisation score and rating](/board/calculations/organisation-score/).
- Tender figures follow the tender periods that overlap your dates. If tender periods run for a whole year, picking a quarter still shows that year's tenders.
- **Revenue** and **Total Spend** include tax and count invoices and bills only; credit notes are not taken off. The order counts and values use amounts before tax.
- An order hidden from the board portal is left out of the Financial Position lists only. Its invoices still count in **Revenue** and **Total Spend** here.
:::
