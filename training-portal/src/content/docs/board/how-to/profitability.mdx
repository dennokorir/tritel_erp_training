The **Profitability** tab lists every won tender with its estimated gross profit (from the business case) and its actual gross profit (from confirmed sales and purchase orders). On a tender's page you can also add a commission or facilitation cost that only you can change.

<Illustration name="board/b2-profitability" caption="The Profitability list. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Read the list

1. Click **Profitability**. **All Periods** shows every won tender; click a tender period to narrow the list.
2. To find a tender, type part of its reference, name or customer in **Search Ref No, Tender, Customer…** and click **Search**. **✕ Clear** removes the search.
3. Use **All**, **Ready for Analysis** (no missing data) or **⚠ Analysis Gaps** (something missing) to filter the rows. The tiles above add up the rows shown: **Won Tenders**, **Estimated GP**, **Actual GP** and **Analysis Gaps**.
4. Each row shows **Revenue**, **Est. GP**, **Est. Margin**, **Actual GP**, **Actual Margin**, **Variance** (actual minus estimated GP) and **BC Status** (**Draft**, **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **No BC**).
5. A warning sign next to the reference means data is missing. Click it to see what, for example *No business case*, *Business case is in Draft — costs may be incomplete*, *No confirmed Sales Orders* or *No confirmed Purchase Orders*. Click **Understood** to close it. Click the reference itself to open the tender.

## Add, edit or delete a commission line

<Illustration name="board/b2-profitability-detail" caption="A won tender's page with your own commission line. The numbers match the steps below." />

1. On the tender's page, the tiles show the business case's **Revenue**, **Total Cost**, **Gross Profit** and **Margin**.
2. **Cost Breakdown** lists every cost line. Commission lines show *— Confidential —* instead of a description, for everyone. Your own lines have **Edit** and **Delete** links.
3. Scroll to **Add Commission / Facilitation Line**.
4. Type a **Description**, which is required (for example *Agency facilitation fee*), and choose the **Currency** (KES by default).
5. Enter the **Quantity** and **Unit Amount**, then click **Add Line**. The page reloads with the new line and the new totals.
6. **Actual vs Estimated** (won tenders only) compares **Actual Revenue**, **Actual Cost**, **Actual GP** and **Actual Margin** with the estimate, and shows the **GP Variance**.

To change your line, click **Edit** on it: the form becomes **Edit Commission Line**. Change the fields and click **Save Changes**, or **Cancel**. To remove it, click **Delete** and confirm *Delete this commission line?*

## What happens next

- The line is added to the tender's business case under **Commissions & Facilitation**. It raises **Total Cost** and lowers **Gross Profit**, **Margin** and **Estimated GP** straight away, so the **Variance** moves too. **Actual GP** doesn't change: it comes from the orders.
- A line in another currency is converted to KES at the day's rate when you save it.
- In Odoo the description stays masked for staff; only tender administrators can read it.
- Adding, editing and deleting are recorded in the [access log](/board/how-to/access-log/).

## Common mistakes

| What you see | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The page reloads and no line was added. | The **Description** was empty. | Type a description and click **Add Line** again. |
| *No business case has been created for this tender yet.* and no form | The tender has no business case. | Ask the bid team to create one. See [business cases](/tenders/concepts/business-cases/). |
| No **Edit** or **Delete** on a commission line | Another board member added it. | Ask them to change it. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Actual GP** uses confirmed order totals including tax, while the estimate uses the business case lines. A difference can come from tax alone. See [business case margin](/tenders/calculations/business-case-margin/).
- A negative quantity or amount is saved as 0.
- Your own line also shows *— Confidential —* in the table. Click **Edit** to see what you wrote.
:::
