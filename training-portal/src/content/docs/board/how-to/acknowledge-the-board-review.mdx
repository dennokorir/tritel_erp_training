When the Board Reviewer signs off your quarter, their results are written to your targets straight away. You then read what they decided and acknowledge it, with a comment for them if you wish.

<Illustration name="board/b1-acknowledge" caption="A signed-off board review. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Actions**. Under **Board reviews**, click the review whose status reads **Signed off**. Or click the **Acknowledge Board Review** to-do.
2. Check **Signed By** and **Signed On**.
3. In the **Targets** list, compare your **Result** and **Score (%)** with the **Board Result** and **Board Score (%)**. Where the board changed a result, the reason is in **Board Comment**. Optionally, write a reply in **Your Comment**.
4. Click **Acknowledge**.

## What happens next

- The review moves to **Acknowledged** and your to-do is marked done. **Your Comment** can no longer be changed.
- The board member who signed off gets an email *Board review acknowledged: Board review · Employee A · Q3 2026* with your comment, and sees the comment on the review in the portal.
- Your scores do not change when you acknowledge: they were updated at sign-off. Each target's reviews show the board member under **Signed by the Board**.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *You can comment once the board has signed off.* | You tried to write **Your Comment** before the sign-off, or after acknowledging. | Comment while the review is **Signed off**. |
| *There is no board sign-off to acknowledge.* | The review is not **Signed off**: it is still with the board, or already acknowledged. | Refresh the page and check the status bar. |
| *Only Employee A can do this.* | Someone other than the MD clicked **Acknowledge**. | Only the MD can acknowledge their own review. |

:::note[Good to know]
- There is no concern or escalation step for a board review, and nothing is acknowledged automatically. If you disagree with the board's results, say so in **Your Comment** and take it up with the board directly.
- You reach the review through **My Actions** or the to-do. The email from the board says to *Open Board Reviews*, but that menu is for Accountability administrators.
:::
