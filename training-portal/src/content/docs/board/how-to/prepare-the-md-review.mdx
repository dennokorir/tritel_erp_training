After each quarter the system opens a review of your targets for the board. Results that come from a metric are already filled in. You add the rest, explain them, attach evidence and submit the review to the Board Reviewer.

<Illustration name="board/b1-prepare-review" caption="Your board review while it is being prepared. The numbers match steps 1 to 4." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › My Actions**. Under **Board reviews**, click the review, for example *Board review · Employee A · Q3 2026* (its status reads **Being prepared**). Or click the **Prepare Board Review** to-do in your activities.
2. Check **Quarter From** and **Quarter To**.
3. Look down the **Targets** list. Lines with **Metric** ticked were calculated by the system: you can't change their **Result**, but you may add a comment and evidence. Every other line needs your result and a comment. Each line's **Window From** and **Window To** show the dates it covers.
4. Click a line that needs your input. The window shown below opens.

<Illustration name="board/b1-prepare-line" caption="One target, with the Result, Comment and Evidence boxes. The numbers match steps 5 to 8." />

5. Type the **Result**. For a milestone target, type how far it is complete, as a percentage (for example 75). The **Score (%)** updates.
6. Write a **Comment** that explains the result to the board.
7. Attach supporting files under **Evidence**. The Board Reviewer can download them from the portal.
8. Click **Save**. Repeat steps 4 to 8 for each line.
9. Click **Submit to the Board**, then **Ok** when asked *Submit this quarter to the board? You can't change it afterwards.*

## What happens next

- The review moves to **With the board** and can no longer be changed. Your to-do is marked done.
- The Board Reviewer gets an email with a link to the review on the portal. They accept or change each result and sign off.
- If they have not signed off after 7 days they are reminded. After 14 days the Backup Board Reviewer, if one is named, can sign off too.
- When the board signs off, you get an **Acknowledge Board Review** to-do. See [Acknowledge the board's sign-off](/board/how-to/acknowledge-the-board-review/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Add your result and a comment for: Open Branch A* | A line you have to fill in has no **Comment**. | Open each line named and write a comment, then submit again. |
| *'Grow revenue' comes from a metric. The board member can adjust it.* | You tried to change the **Result** of a line calculated by the system. | Leave it. Explain in the **Comment** if you think it is wrong; the Board Reviewer can change it. |
| *This review has been submitted, so it can no longer be changed.* | The review is already **With the board**. | Nothing more can be added. Contact the Board Reviewer if something is missing. |
| *No board reviewer is named in the accountability configuration.* | The Board Reviewer was removed from Accountability Settings. | Ask an Accountability administrator to [name the Board Reviewer](/board/how-to/name-the-board-reviewer/). |
| *Only Employee A can do this.* | Someone other than the MD clicked **Submit to the Board**. | Only the MD can submit their own review. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The MD has no **Board Reviews** menu. You reach the review through **My Actions** (group **Board reviews**: *Prepare the quarter for the board, or acknowledge its sign-off.*) or through the to-do.
- Submitting checks only that each line you fill in has a comment. A line left with a **Result** of 0 but a comment is accepted, so check every result before you submit.
- Metric results were worked out when the review opened. If something was posted later, say so in the **Comment**; the Board Reviewer can adjust the result.
:::
