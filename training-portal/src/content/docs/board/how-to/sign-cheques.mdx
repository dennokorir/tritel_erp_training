Finance writes post-dated cheques to pay suppliers. Each one needs a set number of board signatures (2 unless finance sets another number). The **PDC Cheques** tab lists the cheques still to be cleared so that you can check and sign them.

<Illustration name="board/b2-cheques" caption="Cheques grouped by the vendor bill they pay. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Click **PDC Cheques**. The tiles count **Awaiting My Signature** (*Cheques you have not yet signed* and not yet fully signed), **Fully Authorised** (*All required signatures obtained*) and **Overdue / Critical** (*Due within 3 days or already overdue*).
2. Under **Show:**, click **Needs My Signature** to see only the cheques waiting for you. **Fully Authorised** shows the finished ones; **All** shows everything.
3. Choose how to list them: **By document** (the default) groups the cheques under the **Vendor Bill** or **Purchase Order** they pay; **Flat list** shows one row per cheque with **Linked To** and **Tender** columns.
4. In **By document**, check the heading above the cheques: the **Bill Amount** or **PO Amount**, the **Total Cheques**, and whether the bill is **Fully covered**, **Partially covered** or **Over-covered** by the cheques.
5. Check the cheque row: **Cheque Ref**, **Cheque No.**, **Bank**, **Due Date**, **Urgency** and **Amount (KES)**. Then click **Sign**.
6. The row now shows **✓ Signed**. **Authorisation** shows how many of the required signatures are in (for example **2/2**) and **✓ Authorised** once all are.

## What happens next

- Your name and the time are added to the cheque's **Signatures** tab in Odoo, where finance sees them.
- When the number of signatures reaches the number required, the cheque counts as fully signed and moves to **Fully Authorised**. Its status in Odoo doesn't change: finance still issues, deposits and clears it.
- Your signature is recorded in the [access log](/board/how-to/access-log/) with the cheque reference and amount.

:::note[Good to know]
- You can sign a cheque once. Clicking **Sign** again does nothing.
- A signature can't be withdrawn from the portal. If you signed the wrong cheque, tell finance.
- A cheque that pays two bills appears under both. Cheques linked to no bill or order are listed under *Not linked to a document*.
- **Urgency** reads **Normal**, **Soon** (7 days or less), **Critical** (3 days or less) or **Overdue**, from the date on the cheque.
- If nothing is waiting, the page says *No cheques are awaiting your signature.*
:::
