Every visit to a board portal page and every action there (signing a cheque, adding, editing or deleting a commission line, searching, signing off the MD review) is written to the access log. Check it when you need to show who saw or did what.

<Illustration name="board/b2-access-log" caption="The access log filtered to cheque signatures. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the board portal (**/my/board**) and click **Access Log**. The tiles show today's **Page Views Today**, **Active Users Today** (*of 5 board members*) and **Actions Taken Today** (with how many cheques were signed and commissions added). They always count today, whatever filter you use.
2. Under **Filter**, choose a **User**, a **Page** and an **Action** (**View**, **Sign**, **Add**, **Edit**, **Delete** or **Search**), and a **From** and **To** date. Click **Apply**. **Clear** removes the filters.
3. **Portal Activity** lists the entries, newest first: **Timestamp**, **User**, **Page**, **Action**, **Detail** and **IP Address**. **Detail** gives the context, for example the period viewed, the search typed, or the cheque reference and amount signed.
4. The log shows 20 entries a page. Use **‹ Prev** and **Next ›** to move between pages.

## What happens next

Nothing: the log is read-only. Entries are written automatically and can't be changed from the portal.

:::note[Good to know]
- The **Page** names are those the log records: **Board Overview** (the Performance tab), **Financial Position**, **Payroll**, **Profitability**, **Tender Detail**, **Cheques** and **MD Review**.
- The **User** list offers board members only. Visits by administrators are recorded too; choose **All Users** to see them.
- **From** and **To** both include the whole day.
- There is no menu for the log in Odoo; it is read here on the portal.
:::
