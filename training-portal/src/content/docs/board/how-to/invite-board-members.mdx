Board members use the Board portal as portal users. You invite them from their contact: the system emails them and gives them the **Board Viewer** role, which opens every board page. You can invite several board members at once.

<Illustration name="board/b1-invite" caption="Inviting a board member from the Contacts list. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the **Contacts** app and find the board member's contact. Create one first if needed, with their name and email.
2. Tick the contact in the list (tick several to invite them together), or open the contact. Then click **Action** and choose **Invite as Board Viewer**.
3. The **Invite as Board Viewer** window opens with the contacts under **Board Members**. Add or remove contacts there; only contacts with an email address can be chosen.
4. Optionally, type a **Personal Message (optional)**. It is shown in the invitation email.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

## What happens next

- **A board member with no portal account** gets an email *You're invited to Tritel Executive Board Portal*, with your message and a **Set up my account →** button. They choose a password; when the account is created, the **Board Viewer** role is added. The email says the link is valid for 24 hours.
- **A board member who already has a portal account** gets the **Board Viewer** role at once and an email *Your board portal access is ready — Tritel* with an **Open Board Portal →** button.
- When they sign in and open **/my**, they land on the Board portal's **Performance** tab.
- To make them the Board Reviewer or backup, see [Name the Board Reviewer and backup](/board/how-to/name-the-board-reviewer/).

:::note[Good to know]
- If the set-up link no longer works, send the invitation again from the same contact.
- Invite board members as portal users from their own contacts. The Board Reviewer and backup must be board portal users who are not employees.
:::
