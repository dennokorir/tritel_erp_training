The Board Reviewer is the board member who checks and signs off the Managing Director's results each quarter. Naming one switches the MD's targets to board review: from then on, only the board's sign-off gives them results.

<Illustration name="board/b1-name-reviewer" caption="Accountability Settings, with the board review section. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Accountability › Organisation › Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Board Review of the Top-Level Employee**, below **Notifications**.
3. In **Board Reviewer**, choose the board member. Only board portal users are offered.
4. Optionally, in **Backup Board Reviewer**, choose a different board member. The backup can sign off once a review has waited 14 days.
5. Save the settings.

## What happens next

- A note is added to the settings' history, for example *The board reviewers of the top-level employee were changed by Employee C. Reviewer: Board member A. Backup: Board member B.* Everyone named before or after gets an email *Board reviewers changed*.
- The MD's targets can no longer be reviewed in the usual way: **Request Review** is hidden and **Actual Value** can't be typed in.
- On the next daily run, a review opens for the most recent finished quarter if there isn't one yet, and the MD is asked to prepare it. After that, one opens after each quarter.
- A review that is **With the board** can be signed off by whoever is named now.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Board member A cannot review the top-level employee: board reviewers must be board portal users who are not employees.* | The person is a staff user, isn't a board portal user, or is linked to an employee. | [Invite them as a board member](/board/how-to/invite-board-members/) from a contact that is not an employee. |
| *The backup must be a different person from the board reviewer.* | You chose the same person twice. | Choose someone else as backup, or leave it empty. |
| *Name the board reviewer before the backup.* | **Backup Board Reviewer** is filled in but **Board Reviewer** is empty. | Fill in **Board Reviewer** first. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The MD, to the system, is the employee with no manager who has people reporting to them. If the MD has a manager set in Employees, or has no direct reports, no board review opens and their targets are reviewed as usual. Check the org chart.
- If you remove the Board Reviewer, no new reviews open and the MD's targets go back to the usual reviews. A review that was already submitted can't then be signed off until a reviewer is named again.
- Without a backup, the reviewer gets a single reminder after 7 days and nothing more.
:::
