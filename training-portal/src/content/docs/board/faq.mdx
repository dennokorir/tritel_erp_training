## How do I get to the board portal?

Sign in with the account from your invitation. Board members land on **E-Board** straight away; you can also go to **/my/board**. See [Invite a board member](/board/how-to/invite-board-members/).

## Why didn't the accountability figures change when I picked a quarter?

They build up over the year, so the quarter buttons only choose which period to show. To look back, use the **As at** list. See [Read the Performance tab](/board/how-to/read-performance/).

## The Organisation Score is 57% but the label says Meets Expectations. Which is right?

Both. The score is the plain result so far. The label judges running totals, such as revenue, against what is expected by now. See [Organisation score and rating](/board/calculations/organisation-score/).

## Does Revenue include VAT?

Yes. **Revenue** and **Total Spend** add up posted invoices and bills including tax. The order counts and values under them use amounts before tax.

## Why is Net Position on the Performance tab different from the Financial Position?

They measure different things. On **Performance** it is invoiced revenue minus invoiced spend for the dates you picked. On **Financial Position** it is everything owed to the company minus everything it owes, today. See [Receivables, payables, backlog and exposure](/board/calculations/financial-position/).

## Why does an order appear under "Excluded from position report"?

Finance ticked **Exclude from Board Position** on it and gave a reason, shown in **Reason for Exclusion**. Its unbilled amount is left out of the totals. See [Exclude or hide an order](/board/how-to/exclude-or-hide-orders/).

## An order is hidden from the board. Why does its invoice still count?

**Hide from Board Portal** removes the order from the Financial Position lists only. Its invoices are ordinary invoices, so they still count in **Revenue**, **Total Spend** and the unpaid invoice totals.

## Why do the aging cells add up to more than the invoice list behind them?

The cells include the unbilled order balances, aged from the order date. The lists behind the cells show invoices only.

## Why is this month missing from the Payroll tab?

A payslip counts once its pay period has ended. The current month's payslips appear after the month end. See [Payroll employer cost](/board/calculations/payroll-cost/).

## What does Total Employer Cost include?

Gross pay plus employer NSSF plus the employer's Housing Levy. Employee deductions are already inside gross pay.

## Who can see the commission line I added?

Nobody sees its description on the portal: everyone, you included, sees *— Confidential —*. The amount counts in the tender's costs. In Odoo only tender administrators can read the description. See [Read Profitability and add a commission line](/board/how-to/profitability/).

## Can I change another board member's commission line?

No. Only the person who added a line sees **Edit** and **Delete** on it.

## How many signatures does a cheque need?

Usually 2. Finance can set another number per cheque or per bank account. **Authorisation** shows how many are in, for example **1/2**.

## I signed the wrong cheque. Can I undo it?

Not from the portal. Tell finance, who manage the cheque in Odoo. See [Sign post-dated cheques](/board/how-to/sign-cheques/).

## Does a fully signed cheque get paid automatically?

No. Full signatures move it to **Fully Authorised**; finance still issues, deposits and clears it.

## I am the Board Reviewer. Why is there no MD Review tab?

The tab appears only when a review is waiting for you or you have signed one. A review opens the day after a quarter ends and reaches you when the MD submits it. You get an email, *Board review to sign off*, with a link.

## Why does Sign Off ask me to say why I changed a result?

Any result different from the one proposed needs a comment. It is saved with the review, so the MD can see the reason. The message is *Say why you changed the result for Grow revenue.* See [How the MD review figures are worked out](/board/calculations/md-review-figures/).

## What happens if the Board Reviewer is away?

After 7 days the reviewer gets a reminder. After 14 days the review opens to the Backup Board Reviewer too, if one is named, and whoever signs first closes it. See [Review and sign off the MD's quarter](/board/how-to/review-the-md/).

## As the MD, can I change a result after submitting?

No. Once submitted, the review can't be changed. The board member can change any result when signing off, with a comment. You can reply in **Your Comment** when you acknowledge. See [Acknowledge the board's sign-off](/board/how-to/acknowledge-the-board-review/).

## Who can see who visited the board portal?

System administrators, on the **Access Log** tab. See [Check the board portal access log](/board/how-to/access-log/).
