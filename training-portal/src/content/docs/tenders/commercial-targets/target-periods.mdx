A **target period** is a named stretch of time, such as *Q2 2026*, that the commercial team plans and reports in. Periods can't overlap. The system files records into the period whose dates cover them:

| Record | Filed by |
|---|---|
| Tender | **Award Date**; if there is none, the submission deadline; if there is none, the date it was created. |
| CRM opportunity | **Expected Closing**; if there is none, the date it was created. |
| Achievement line | The tender's **Award Date**; if no period covers it, the person's individual target's period. |

A record whose date falls outside every period has no period.

## Where periods show

- **Tender board and CRM pipeline:** the kanban views have a **Period** bar with **All** and the latest periods. They open on the period whose status is **Active**. Picking a period shows the tenders or opportunities filed in it, plus those with no period whose submission deadline, expected closing or creation date falls inside it.
- **Commercial portal:** the dashboards use the period that covers today, whatever its status.
- **Target Periods list:** **Total Target**, **Total Achieved** and **Achievement %** add up the role targets filed in each period. Role targets are [being replaced](/tenders/commercial-targets/), so expect these to stay at zero.

## Create and activate a period

1. Open **Tender Manager › Configuration › Target Periods** and click **New**.
2. Type the **Period Name**, for example *Q2 2026*.
3. Set **Date From** and **Date To**. Save.
4. When the period starts, click **Set Active**. Then open the period that has just ended and click **Close Period**.
5. The status bar shows **Draft**, **Active** or **Closed**. A closed period can go back with **Reset to Draft**, or straight to **Active** with **Set Active**.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Period 'Q2 2026' overlaps with 'Q1 2026'. Target periods must not overlap.* | The dates touch another period's dates, whatever its status. | Start the new period the day after the other ends. |
| *End date must be on or after start date.* | **Date To** is before **Date From**. | Correct the dates. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The status only decides which period the tender board and CRM pipeline open on. Keep one period **Active** at a time; if several are, the one starting latest wins.
- **Closed** locks nothing. Tenders, opportunities and achievements in a closed period can still be changed, and they still move between periods when their dates change.
- Any internal user can create, change and activate periods, but no one can delete one. Correct a wrong period's name or dates instead.
- Create next quarter's period before it starts. Tenders awarded on a date no period covers have no period, and may show only under **All**.
:::
