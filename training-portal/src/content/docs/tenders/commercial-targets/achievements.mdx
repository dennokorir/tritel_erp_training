When a tender is won, the heads credit the people who won it. Each person gets an **achievement line** on the tender's **Target Achievements** tab. The line works out how much of the tender's value that person achieved. The steps are in [Attribute a won tender](/tenders/how-to/bids/attribute-a-won-tender/); this page explains the arithmetic.

## The rule

1. **Gross Value** = the tender's **Expected Revenue** in KES (converted at the rate on the award date), divided by the number of lines **with the same role** on that tender. Two Account Managers share the value; the Bid Liaison and Technical Presales each get the whole value.
2. **% Achievement** = **Pipelining** + **Bid Submission Support** + **Post-Submission Influence**. Each stage has a maximum that depends on the line's **Role**; the maximums always add up to 100.
3. **Achieved Value** = **Gross Value** × **% Achievement** ÷ 100.

| Role | Pipelining | Bid Submission Support | Post-Submission Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account Manager | up to 40 | up to 20 | up to 40 |
| Bid Liaison | none | up to 60 | up to 40 |
| Technical Presales | up to 60 | up to 40 | none |

## Stage points from activities

The heads can type the points, or click **Compute Related Activities** on the achievement line. That button:

1. links the customer activities on the tender, and on its opportunity, whose **Responsible** is this person;
2. counts the valid ones in each stage;
3. sets each stage to (valid activities ÷ 3, at most 1) × the stage maximum, to one decimal.

An activity is valid, and gets its stage, by when it was logged in the system (not by its **Date**):

- on the **opportunity**, logged on or after the opportunity was created: **Pipelining**;
- on the **tender**, logged on or after the tender was created and not after the award date: **Bid Submission Support** if logged on or before the submission deadline, otherwise **Post-Submission Influence**;
- linked to neither: not valid.

## Name, dates and period

Picking the person's individual target on a new line fills **Name** with the award quarter (for example *Q2 2026*) and sets **Date From** and **Date To** to that quarter. The line's **Period** is the target period covering the award date.

## Who can see and change the lines

| Who | Can |
|---|---|
| The person credited | Read their own lines |
| Their managers (anyone above them in the employee reporting line) | Read the lines of everyone below them |
| Head of Commercial, Head of Channel, Tender administrators | Read, add, change and delete every line |
| Everyone else | Nothing: other people's lines don't show |

Before November 2026 every internal user could read and change every line.

## How Accountability counts it

Accountability's **Achieved revenue (attributed)** metrics (one each for Account Manager, Bid Liaison and Technical Presales) add up the **Achieved Value** of a person's lines for that role on tenders with the status **Won**, by award date in the review window. **Gross awarded value**, **Awarded tenders** and **Attribution rate** do the same for Account Managers. Lines still at 0% count as nothing and are flagged as *award(s) awaiting attribution*. See [Metric families](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).

## Edge cases

- **Totals across roles are more than the tender.** Each role is credited with the full value, so a fully attributed tender can count up to three times its value across the three roles. That is intended: each role is measured on its own.
- **A second line in the same role.** A second Account Manager line halves both Account Managers' gross value, even before points are entered.
- **Expected Revenue changes.** Every line on the tender is worked out again.
- **No award date.** Picking the target or tender stops with *The 'Award Date' field is missing. Please provide a value before executing this action.* Set the **Award Date** on the tender first.
- **Too many points.** *'Bid Submission Support' cannot exceed 20 for role 'Account Manager'.* For a Bid Liaison, any Pipelining points give *'Pipelining' cannot exceed 0 for role 'Bid Liaison'.* Negative points give *'Pipelining' cannot be negative.*
- **Prequalified tenders** show the **Target Achievements** tab too, but are never flagged for attribution, and Accountability counts only **Won** tenders.
- **Colours.** On the line, **Excellent** means 90 or more, **Meets Expectation** 50 to under 90, and **Below Target** under 50. These bands are not the target statuses (**Exceeded**, **On Track**, **At Risk**, **Behind**).

:::note[Good to know]
- **Compute Related Activities** replaces any points typed by hand. Run it again after more activities are logged; nothing reruns it for you.
- Only activities where the person is **Responsible** count. Activities logged for them by someone else, or not linked to the tender or its opportunity, don't.
- An activity linked to both the opportunity and the tender counts as **Pipelining**.
- The **Role** on a line is chosen by hand and isn't checked against the person's role on the tender. Check it: it sets the maximums and which share of the value they get.
- Account Manager lines also feed the account manager's **Awarded value growth vs same quarter last year** KPI, and the **Awarded** figures on a customer's form and account plan. See [From target account to the account manager's KPI](/end-to-end/account-plan-to-am-kpi/).
:::
