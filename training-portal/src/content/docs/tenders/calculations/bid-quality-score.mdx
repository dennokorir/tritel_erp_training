Two figures carry the name "quality". The **Quality Score (%)** sits on the tender's **Bid Quality** tab: the Bid Liaison's manager gives it once the bid is submitted. The **Bid Quality Score** is an Accountability metric: it averages a Bid Liaison's scored bids over a quarter, counts must-win bids three times and counts confirmed errors at 0%.

## The Quality Score on a tender

When a tender first moves to **Evaluation**, its **Quality Check** becomes **To Score** and is due 10 days later. The scorer clicks **Score Bid** and marks each guideline **Met**, **Not met** or **Not applicable** in the **Bid Quality Check** window.

- **Quality Score (%)** = guidelines **Met** ÷ guidelines that apply × 100, rounded to one decimal.
- A guideline marked **Not applicable** is left out of both sides.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-quality-score" caption="6 guidelines met out of the 7 that apply gives 85.7%, not 6 out of 8." />

The guideline list comes from **Tender Manager › Configuration › Bid Quality Guidelines** (8 to start with, from *All required forms are completed and signed* to *Submitted before the deadline at the correct location*). Each scored tender keeps the wording it was scored against, so changing the list later doesn't change past scores. **Change Score** reopens the same rows.

**Who scores:** the Bid Liaison's manager in the Employees app. If the Bid Liaison has no usable manager, the Head of Commercial users score; failing that, Tender administrators. The Bid Liaison can never score their own bid.

## The Bid Quality Score in Accountability

The **Bid Quality Score (Bid Liaison)** metric looks at the tenders where the person is the **Bid Liaison**:

| Tender | Counts as | Weight | Which quarter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disqualification confirmed, **Caused By** **Bid Liaison** | 0% | 3 if must-win, else 1 | The quarter it was confirmed |
| Submitted and scored, no confirmed error by the Bid Liaison | Its **Quality Score (%)** | 3 if must-win, else 1 | The quarter of its **Actual Submission** |
| Submitted, not yet scored | Left out of the average and listed | – | – |

**Bid Quality Score** = sum of (score × weight) ÷ sum of weights, rounded to one decimal.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-bid-quality-score" caption="A must-win bid at 90% counts three times. The confirmed disqualification counts at 0%. The unscored bid is left out." />

## Bid Compliance for Technical Presales

The **Bid Compliance (Technical Presales)** metric works the same way for the tenders where the person is **Technical Presales**, but ignores the manager's quality check: every bid counts at 100%, except a disqualification confirmed as caused by **Technical Presales**, which counts at 0%.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-bid-compliance" caption="Two compliant bids, one of them must-win, and one confirmed error give 80.0%." />

Both metrics wait 14 days after the quarter ends before an automatic review, so late scores and confirmations are picked up. See [how metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).

## Edge cases

- **No bids in the quarter**: the review is **Not Applicable** and doesn't affect the score.
- **Every bid unscored**: the same. The worksheet lists the bids waiting to be scored.
- **A disqualification caused by someone else** (Account Manager, OEM or supplier, Client, Other) doesn't count against the Bid Liaison. The bid counts at its quality score if it has one.
- **A disqualification still To Confirm** doesn't count as an error yet. The bid counts at its quality score and is flagged *awaiting confirmation*.
- **Cancelled tenders** count only if they were scored.
- **Must-win** is the flag on the tender when the metric runs. Flagging or removing it later changes the weight.
- **All guidelines Not applicable**: the score can't be saved: *At least one guideline must apply to this bid.*

:::note[Good to know]
A disqualification caused by the Bid Liaison counts in the quarter it was **confirmed**, not the quarter the bid went in. A bid submitted in March and confirmed as a Bid Liaison error in April counts at 0% in Q2, and is not counted in Q1 at all. It also counts when the bid was dropped and never submitted.

The scorer can correct the **Actual Submission** in the **Bid Quality Check** window. That date decides which quarter a scored bid counts in.

Must-win can currently be flagged at any status, even after submission. Flagging a bid after it was scored makes it count three times.
:::
