The business case opens from the **Business case** button on a tender. Its four cards show **Revenue**, **Total Cost**, **Gross Profit** and **Margin**: the estimate (**Est.**) always, and the actual figures (**Act.**) once the tender is **Won** or **Prequalified**. All figures are in the company currency (KES).

## The estimate

- **Expected Revenue**: the tender's **Expected Revenue**, converted to KES at the rate on the **Award Date**, or else on the **Submission Deadline** date, or else today.
- Each cost line's **Subtotal (line CCY)** = **Qty** × **Unit Price**, in the line's **Currency**. **Subtotal (CC)** is that amount in KES at today's rate when the line is saved.
- **Total Cost** = sum of the lines' **Subtotal (CC)**.
- **Gross Profit** = **Expected Revenue** − **Total Cost**.
- **Gross Margin (%)** = **Gross Profit** ÷ **Expected Revenue** × 100, rounded to one decimal; 0 when there is no revenue.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-business-case-estimate" caption="KES 7,000,000 of costs against KES 10,000,000 of revenue is a 30.0% margin." />

The **Margin** card's coloured stripe is green from 20%, amber from 10% to under 20%, and red below 10%. The **Gross Profit** stripe is red when profit is below zero.

## The actual figures

Once the tender is **Won** or **Prequalified**, the **Actual vs Estimated** tab and the **Act.** lines appear:

- **Actual Revenue**: the totals of the tender's confirmed sales orders, each converted to KES at the rate on its order date.
- **Actual Cost**: the totals of the tender's confirmed purchase orders, converted the same way.
- **Actual Gross Profit** = **Actual Revenue** − **Actual Cost**.
- **Actual Margin %** = **Actual Gross Profit** ÷ **Actual Revenue** × 100, one decimal.
- **GP Variance (Actual − Estimated)** = **Actual Gross Profit** − **Gross Profit**. The cards also show the revenue and cost variances (actual − estimated) and the margin variance in percentage points (pp): **Actual Margin %** − **Gross Margin (%)**.

On the cards, a green ↑ or red ↓ badge shows each variance. For cost the colours are reversed: spending more than estimated is red.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-business-case-actual" caption="VAT on the orders lifts the actual gross profit by 480,000 while the margin stays at 30.0%." />

Orders count only when they carry the tender in their **Tender** field and are confirmed (sales order or purchase order, including locked). Quotations, requests for quotation and cancelled orders don't count.

## Edge cases

- **No sales order yet**: **Actual Revenue** is 0, and so is **Actual Margin %**. The margin variance then shows the whole estimated margin as a drop.
- **Foreign-currency cost lines** keep the rate of the day they were last changed. They are not revalued every day.
- **The tender's value changes** (or its award date): **Expected Revenue**, **Gross Profit** and **Gross Margin** follow straight away.
- **Commissions & Facilitation** lines count in the total like any other line. Their **Description** shows as dots to everyone except the Head of Commercial (for commercial commissions), the Head of Channel (for channel commissions) and Tender administrators.
- **One business case per tender**: *A business case already exists for this tender.*

:::note[Good to know]
The actual figures use the orders' totals including taxes, while cost lines are usually entered before tax. With 16% VAT on both sides, the actual gross profit looks 16% bigger than the same deal estimated without VAT, and **GP Variance (Actual − Estimated)** shows a gain that is only tax. Compare the margins (the pp badge on the **Margin** card) rather than the amounts.

This business case is the bid-stage view. The delivery budget and its margin live on the delivery project: see [expected revenue and margin left](/projects/calculations/margin/).
:::
