Every account plan and every quarterly review has a date it should be approved by. The dates come from the **Account Plans** settings (see [Tender Manager configuration](/tenders/configuration/#account-plans-settings)); the defaults are used below.

## The plan's due date

A plan should be approved by **31 January** of its year (the setting **Plan approved by (MM-DD)**, default *01-31*). The **Due date** is set when the plan is created. The Head of Commercial can change it for one plan; nobody else can (*Only the Head of Commercial can change a plan's due date.*).

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-account-plan-due" caption="A 2026 plan approved on 3 February is late." />

| Plan **Timeliness** | When |
|---|---|
| **Not due yet** | Not approved, and the due date hasn't passed. |
| **Approved on time** | Approved on or before the **Due date**. |
| **Approved late** | Approved after the **Due date**. |
| **Overdue** | Not approved (**Draft**, **Submitted** or **Returned**) and the **Due date** has passed. |

## A review's due date and Counts until

A quarterly review looks back at one quarter:

- **Due date** = the last day of the quarter + **14 days** (setting **Review due, days after quarter end**).
- **Counts until** = the **Due date** + **5 days** (setting **Grace days for the KPI**). These are calendar days.

| Quarter | Ends | Due date | Counts until |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 31 Mar | 14 Apr | 19 Apr |
| Q2 | 30 Jun | 14 Jul | 19 Jul |
| Q3 | 30 Sep | 14 Oct | 19 Oct |
| Q4 | 31 Dec | 14 Jan (next year) | 19 Jan (next year) |

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-account-plan-review-dates" caption="The Q1 2026 review approved on 17 April: late, but it still counts." />

| Review **Timeliness** | When | Counts for the KPI? |
|---|---|---|
| **Not due yet** | Not approved, and the **Due date** hasn't passed. | Not yet |
| **Approved on time** | Approved on or before the **Due date**. | Yes |
| **Approved late** | Approved after the **Due date** but on or before **Counts until**. | Yes |
| **Overdue** | Not approved, past the **Due date**, not yet past **Counts until**. | Not yet |
| **Missed** | Past **Counts until** and not approved, **or approved after Counts until**. | No |

Only the approval date matters. A review submitted on time but approved after **Counts until** is **Missed**, so supervisors should approve promptly. How the KPI counts reviews is in [Account manager KPIs](/accountability/calculations/account-manager-kpi/).

## Which quarter Quarterly Review opens

**Quarterly Review** on an approved plan opens:

- **the quarter that has just ended**, until its **Counts until** date (with the defaults, until the 19th day of the next quarter), as long as that review isn't approved yet and the quarter is in the plan's year;
- otherwise **the current quarter**.

So on 10 April 2026 it opens Q1 2026; on 20 April 2026, or once Q1 is approved, it opens Q2 2026. In January, the 2026 plan opens Q1 2026; to review Q4 2025, open the **2025** plan and use **Quarterly Review** there. If the quarter isn't in the plan's year at all, you see *This plan is for 2025; open the plan for 2026 to review this quarter.*

## Working days

**Waiting (working days)**, the reminder gaps and the escalation limits count working days: Monday to Friday, starting the day after. Public holidays are not skipped. For example, a review submitted on Thursday 9 April 2026 has waited 1 working day on Friday 10 April and 2 on Monday 13 April.

## Reminders and escalation

The scheduled job **Account Plans: reminders and escalation** is switched off when the system is installed. The schedule below applies once your administrator turns it on (see [Tender Manager configuration](/tenders/configuration/#account-plans-settings)). It runs at 05:00 on weekdays only.

It chases the account manager of every **Key account** and **Major growth** target account. Something is outstanding when the customer has no plan for this year, the plan is **Draft** or **Returned**, or the plan is approved but the review for the quarter being reviewed (the one **Quarterly Review** would open) hasn't been submitted.

| Email | To | When (default settings) |
|---|---|---|
| *Account plans due* | The account manager | From **14** days before the due date (**First reminder, days before due**), then every **3** working days (**Then every (working days)**), until it is submitted. Each line says *due 14 Apr 2026* or *3 days late*. |
| *2 account plan item(s) waiting for your approval* | The supervisor (**Approver**) | Once a plan or review has waited **2** working days (**Remind the supervisor after (working days)**), then every 2 working days, until it is approved or returned. |
| *Account plans escalated* | The Head of Commercial (or the person in **Escalations go to**) | Once per plan or review: when it is **5** working days past its due date (**Escalate overdue after (working days)**), or has waited **5** working days for approval (**Escalate waiting approvals after (working days)**). The plan then shows **Head of Commercial informed**. |
| *Account plans this week* | The Head of Commercial | Every Monday, if **Weekly summary to the Head of Commercial** is on: one line per account manager, for example *Employee A: 1 overdue · 0 waiting approval · 3 of 5 reviewed for Q1 2026*. |

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-account-plan-reminders" caption="The Q1 2026 review, from the first reminder to the escalation." />

For a 2026 plan due on Saturday 31 January, the first reminder goes out on Monday 19 January (14 days before falls on a weekend), and if there is still no submitted plan, the escalation goes out on Friday 6 February, 5 working days after the due date.

## Edge cases

- **Changing the settings** affects plans created and reviews started afterwards. Existing plans keep their **Due date**, and existing reviews keep their **Due date** and **Counts until**. The **Plan Review Board** works out its cells from the current settings.
- **The quarter's review moves on** after **Counts until**: from then on, reminders and **My Actions** are about the next quarter, and the missed review stays **Missed**.
- **Customers in Maintain, or with no tier, or with no account manager** are never chased, though you can still write a plan for them.
- **No email address**: someone without an email address on their user gets no reminder.

:::note[Good to know]
- With the default settings an **overdue review** is rarely escalated: 5 working days after the due date usually falls after **Counts until**, by when the reminders have moved on to the next quarter. Overdue **plans**, and plans or reviews **waiting for approval**, are escalated as described.
- In January, the reminders and **My Actions** don't mention the Q4 review of last year's plan; they move straight to this year's plan. Account managers need to remember to open last year's plan and use **Quarterly Review** by 19 January.
- The **Head of Commercial informed** date shows on the plan form only, not on a review.
:::
