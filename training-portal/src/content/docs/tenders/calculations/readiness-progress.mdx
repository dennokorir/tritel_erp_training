The **Bid Readiness** tab shows **Progress** as a count, such as *5 of 7 ready*. It is not a percentage. It counts the checklist items that are **Ready** against the items that are needed.

## Each item's Status

Each row of the checklist gets a **Status**, checked in this order:

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| **Not Needed** | **Not Needed** is ticked on the row |
| **Missing** | No file in **Document Files**, or the document has an expiry date and **Valid Until** is empty |
| **Expired** | The document has an expiry date and **Valid Until** is before the day of the **Submission Deadline** (Nairobi time) |
| **Ready** | Anything else |

Only documents set up with an expiry date (for example a tax compliance certificate) can be **Expired**. **Valid Until** can be filled in only on those rows.

## Progress

**Progress** = number of **Ready** items "of" the number of items not marked **Not Needed**.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-readiness-progress" caption="One item is Not Needed, so 7 items count. 5 of them are Ready." />

**Confirm Ready** checks the list again. If anything is **Missing** or **Expired**, it stops with a list, for example *This bid isn't ready yet: - Tax compliance certificate: expired*. When it succeeds, **Readiness** becomes **Confirmed Ready** with **Confirmed Ready On** and **Confirmed By**.

## In Accountability

**Ready at 72 Hours** (for the Bid Liaison and for Technical Presales): bids submitted in the window that were confirmed ready at least 72 hours before the deadline ÷ bids submitted with a checklist × 100. A bid never confirmed ready counts as not met. Tenders created less than 3 days before their deadline, cancelled tenders and tenders from before the bid desk started are left out. See [how metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).

The daily reminder emails the Bid Liaison and Technical Presales 5 days and 3 days before the deadline while the bid isn't confirmed ready.

## Edge cases

- **No checklist yet**: **Progress** is empty. The checklist is built when the tender moves to **Bidding**, or with **Create Checklist**.
- **Everything Not Needed**: **Progress** shows *0 of 0 ready* and **Confirm Ready** is allowed.
- **Valid Until on the deadline day**: the document is **Ready**. Only a date before the deadline day is **Expired**.
- **The deadline moves**: the statuses are worked out again, so a document can turn **Expired** after the deadline is pushed back.
- **Bid bond**: the *Bid bond* item is added only when **Bid Bond Required** is ticked on the tender. **Confirm Ready** adds it if the tick was set after the checklist was made.
- **Changing a confirmed checklist**: refused with *This checklist is confirmed. Reopen it to make changes.* **Reopen** puts it back to **In Progress**, and the earlier confirmation time is cleared.

:::note[Good to know]
Reopening a confirmed checklist clears **Confirmed Ready On**. If you confirm again later than 72 hours before the deadline, the bid no longer counts as ready at 72 hours. Only reopen when something really has to change.
:::
