A tender has two times that matter at submission: the **Submission Deadline** on the **Tender Info** tab, and the **Actual Submission** on the **Bid Quality** tab. Comparing them gives **Submitted Late**. Separately, **Submission Recorded** says whether someone attached proof that the bid went in.

## Actual Submission

**Actual Submission** is set the first time the tender moves to **Evaluation**:

1. **Mark Submitted** asks **Actually Submitted** (it starts at the current time). Whatever you enter becomes the **Actual Submission**. It can't be in the future: *The submission time can't be in the future.*
2. **Add Receipt** on the **Bid Quality** tab asks the time again and replaces it.
3. The manager who scores the bid can correct it in the **Bid Quality Check** window.

## Submitted Late

**Submitted Late** is ticked when **Actual Submission** is later than the **Submission Deadline**, comparing date and time.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-late-submission" caption="Thirty minutes after the deadline is late. Exactly on the deadline is not." />

The flag is worked out each time, so it changes if either time is changed later. It is shown on the **Bid Quality** tab only; it doesn't change any score by itself.

## Submission Recorded and the receipt

**Submission Recorded** is ticked when the Bid Liaison (or their manager, or a Tender administrator) attaches a **Submission Receipt**, either in **Mark Submitted** or later with **Add Receipt**. Without a file: *Please attach the submission receipt.* **Recorded By** shows who recorded it.

Once recorded, the Bid Liaison can't record it again. Only their manager or a Tender administrator can add another receipt or change the time.

## Submission Buffer in Accountability

**Submission Buffer (Bid Liaison)** = bids recorded as submitted at least 24 hours before the deadline ÷ bids submitted in the window × 100. A bid counts as met only if **Submission Recorded** is ticked and **Actual Submission** is at least 24 hours before the **Submission Deadline**.

<Illustration name="tenders/calc-submission-buffer" caption="One bid had no receipt recorded, so 3 of 4 bids met the buffer." />

Left out: tenders created less than 3 days before their deadline, cancelled tenders, and tenders from before the bid desk started. See [how metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).

## Edge cases

- **No deadline on the tender**: **Submitted Late** stays off and the bid is left out of Submission Buffer.
- **Moving the deadline after submission** changes **Submitted Late** and the buffer, because both use the current deadline.
- **Reopening a tender** to **Bidding** keeps the old **Actual Submission**. Marking it submitted again replaces it.
- **Times are shown in your own time zone**, but compared as exact moments, so the result is the same for everyone.

:::note[Good to know]
**Submitted Late** and the lost or drop reason *Disqualified: late submission* are not linked. Choosing that reason doesn't tick the flag, and a late flag doesn't record a disqualification.

Change the deadline on the **Tender Info** tab. The **Submission Deadline** on the **Bid Readiness** and **Bid Quality** tabs is read-only.
:::
