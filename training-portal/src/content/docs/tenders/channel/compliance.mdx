Bids often need the company's current compliance documents: a tax compliance certificate, a regulator's licence, a contractor or AGPO registration. Each document is kept as a **compliance item** under the body that issues it, with one **validity period** for each certificate issued. Renewing a certificate means adding a new validity period with the new file, so the history stays.

<Illustration name="tenders/ch-compliance" caption="A compliance item and the validity period that Create opens. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › Compliance › Compliance**. Items are grouped by issuing body, and each card shows **Active** or **Expired**.
2. Click the item to renew. For a new one, click **New** and type its name, for example *Tax compliance certificate*.
3. Choose the **Compliance Body**. If the body isn't there, add it first under **Tender Manager › Compliance › Compliance Organization**.
4. Click **Create** at the top of the item. A new validity period opens.
5. Set **Date Start** and **Date End** as printed on the certificate.
6. On the **Documents** tab, click **Attach a file** and choose the certificate.
7. Save, then go back to the item using the breadcrumb.

## What happens next

- The new period appears on the item's **Compliance History** tab.
- The item's dates and **Compliance Status** follow the period with the latest **Date End**: **Active** while that date is after today, **Expired** from that day on.
- **Attachment Url** on the item links to the latest certificate's file, to download or share it.
- The period appears on the compliance calendar on its **Date End**.

## The calendar and churn views

| Menu | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Tender Manager › Compliance › Compliance Calendar** | Every validity period on the day it expires, a year at a time. Switch to the list view to sort by **Date End**. The page is titled *Compliance Churn*. |
| **Tender Manager › Compliance › Compliance Churn** | A cohort chart of validity periods by start month, showing how many have lapsed month by month. The page is titled *Compliance History*. |
| **Tender Manager › Compliance › Compliance Organization** | The issuing bodies, each with a count of its compliance items. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Nothing reminds anyone before a certificate expires. Check the **Compliance Calendar** at the start of each month, and before every bid.
- A certificate whose **Date End** is today already shows **Expired**. A period with no **Date End** also counts as expired.
- The **Compliance Calendar** and **Compliance Churn** menus open pages titled the other way round (*Compliance Churn* and *Compliance History*). Go by the menu name.
- Every internal user can change or delete compliance items and periods. Archive an item you no longer need rather than deleting it, to keep its history.
:::
