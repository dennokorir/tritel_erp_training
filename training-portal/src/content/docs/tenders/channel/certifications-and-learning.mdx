OEMs often require a number of certified staff before Tritel can sell or support a product line. The Channel app keeps three things for this:

- **Courses** (**Channel › Configuration › Courses**): each OEM's course or track, the **Certification** it leads to, and the **Minimum Requirement**, the number of certified staff the OEM expects.
- **Certifications** (**Channel › Certifications**, also under **Employees**): one record per certificate a person holds, with its dates and a copy of the certificate.
- **Learning Development** (**Channel › Learning Development**): one record per person working towards a certification, shown on a board with the stages **Planning**, **Ongoing**, **Passed** and **Failed**.

<Illustration name="tenders/ch-learning" caption="A learning record and the Pass or Fail window. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps: follow someone to certification

1. Open **Channel › Learning Development** and click **New**.
2. Choose the **Employee**, the **Oem** and the **Course** (only that OEM's courses are offered). Set the **Promise Date** by which they expect to sit the exam, and **Date Start** and **Date End**, the dates the certificate will run from and to.
3. When they start studying, click **Ongoing** on the status bar.
4. When the result is known, click **Pass/Fail Certification**. It shows only while the status is **Ongoing**.
5. In the **Pass or Fail** window, choose the **Certification Status** (**Pass** or **Fail**) and the **Date**.
6. Click **Submit**.
7. On a pass, open the new certificate with the certificate button at the top right of the record. Choose its **Course**, check the dates and, on the **Documents** tab, click **Attach a file** to add the certificate.

## What happens next

- The status becomes **Pass** or **Fail** and the card moves to **Passed** or **Failed** on the board.
- On a pass, if the record has no certificate yet, one is created for the employee with the record's **Date Start** and **Date End**. It appears under **Channel › Certifications** and on the employee's **Certifications** smart button.
- On the board, an **Ongoing** card shows *To be submitted* with the days left to the **Promise Date**.

## Record a certificate someone already holds

1. Open **Channel › Certifications** and click **New**.
2. Choose the **Employee** and the **Course**. The name and **Oem** come from the course.
3. Set **Date Start** and **Date End**, and attach the certificate on the **Documents** tab.

Use the **Attached** and **Not Attached** filters to find certificates with no copy on file.

:::note[Good to know]
- Change a learning record's status with the status bar on the form. Dragging its card to another column moves the card but doesn't change the status, so **Pass/Fail Certification** may not appear.
- A certificate created by a pass has no **Course** until you set it, so it has no name or OEM and is listed apart from the others. Do step 7 every time.
- The **Date** in the **Pass or Fail** window is not kept anywhere. Note the exam date in the record's chatter if you need it.
- Certificates don't expire by themselves: the **Active** label never changes and there is no reminder. Check **Date End** when an OEM asks for your certified headcount.
- Every internal user can add, change and delete courses and certificates.
:::
