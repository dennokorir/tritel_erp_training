When someone cancels a purchase order, its lines can no longer back an OEM booking. The system deals with **draft** bookings by itself. A **confirmed** booking was agreed against those lines, so it keeps them until you correct it.

| Booking status when the order is cancelled | What the system does |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Removes the cancelled order's lines from the booking and notes it in the chatter: *PO-2026-002 was cancelled, so its lines were removed: PO-2026-002 · Support 1 year · USD 4,000.00. By Employee A.* Nothing else to do; check the amount before you confirm. |
| **Confirmed** | Keeps the lines, shows a red banner, notes it in the chatter, and gives whoever created the booking a to-do: *PO-2026-002 was cancelled: correct this booking*. Follow the steps below. |

<Illustration name="tenders/ch-cancelled-order" caption="A confirmed booking that still includes a cancelled order. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the booking from your to-do, or from **Channel › OEM Bookings**. The red banner reads *Includes lines from purchase orders that are no longer confirmed: PO-2026-002. Reset to draft, remove them, check the amount and confirm again.*
2. Click **Reset to Draft**.
3. On the **Purchase lines** tab, click the red **Remove** (×) on each line from the cancelled order. Each removal is noted in the chatter.
4. Check the **OEM-Recognised Amount** against the OEM's portal. If the OEM has reduced its figure, type the new one.
5. Click **Confirm**, then mark the to-do as done in the chatter.

## What happens next

- The red banner goes away and the booking's **Purchase Cost (USD)**, margin and **Booking Date** are worked out again from the lines that are left.
- If the corrected booking's booking date moves, it may now count in a different month or review window.
- If the cancelled order was the only one behind the booking, there are no lines left. Either add the replacement order's lines, or delete the draft booking.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *'BK-1001' is confirmed. Reset the booking to draft before removing lines.* | You tried to remove a line from a confirmed booking. | Click **Reset to Draft** first. |
| *'BK-1001': remove the lines from purchase orders that are no longer confirmed before confirming: PO-2026-002.* | You clicked **Confirm** with the cancelled order's lines still linked. | Remove them, then confirm. |
| *'BK-1001': link the purchase lines this booking covers before confirming.* | You removed every line. | Add the replacement lines, or delete the draft booking. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Until you correct it, the confirmed booking still counts **in full** in the OEM's figures and in Accountability's OEM-recognised revenue, cancelled lines included.
- Only the person who created the booking gets the to-do, and only if they can still see bookings. Others see the red banner and the chatter note.
- The to-do is not closed for you when the booking is fixed. Mark it done yourself.
- The **Purchase Orders** count on the OEM form still counts the cancelled order. Bookings and the channel figures don't.
:::
