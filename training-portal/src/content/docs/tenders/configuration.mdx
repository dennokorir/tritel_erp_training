**Tender Manager › Configuration** holds the lists that the rest of the app picks from: tender tags and reasons, the bid desk's lists, the summary headings, and the types and bands used for customer touches. Most are set up once and rarely change.

<Illustration name="tenders/config-activity-types" caption="The Configuration menu, with Activity Types open. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Tenders and bids

| Menu | What it holds | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Target Periods** | The quarters or years tenders and opportunities are filed into. | [Target periods](/tenders/commercial-targets/target-periods/) |
| **Stages** | The columns of the tender board. | [Tenders, statuses and the board](/tenders/concepts/tenders-and-statuses/) |
| **Tags** | Free labels for tenders, each with a colour. You pick them in the tender's **Tags** field. Two tags can't have the same name (*Tag name already exists!*). Archive a tag rather than deleting it. | — |
| **Lost Reasons**, **Drop Reasons** | The reasons offered when a tender is lost or dropped, and which ones count as a disqualification. | [Record the result](/tenders/how-to/bids/record-the-result/) |
| **Cancel Reasons** | The reasons offered when a tender is cancelled. | [Tender status workflow](/tenders/workflows/tender-status/) |
| **Standard Bid Documents**, **Bid Desk Team**, **Bid Quality Guidelines** | The bid readiness checklist, the people counted for fair workload, and the bid quality questions. Head of Commercial and Tender administrators only. | [Bid readiness](/tenders/concepts/bid-readiness/), [The bid desk](/tenders/concepts/bid-desk-workload/) |
| **Pillars**, **Summary Headings** | The pillars and the headings of a project summary. | [Project summaries](/tenders/concepts/project-summaries/) |

## Customers and touches

| Menu | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Customer Tags** | The tags on customer records (the same tags as in Contacts), such as a sector or *Government*. They show in the customer's **Tags** field, and you can search customers by them. |
| **Activity Types** | The kinds of customer touch, such as **Procurement Plan**, **Customer Meeting** and **Workshop**. Each type has a **Channel**. A touch of that type takes that channel unless the person logging it changes it. |
| **Last Seen Stages** | The bands a customer's **Last Seen** date is sorted into, from **Last Seen 30 Days** to **Never Seen**. |

## Steps: add an activity type

1. Open **Tender Manager › Configuration**.
2. Click **Activity Types**.
3. Click **New**. A blank row opens at the bottom of the list.
4. Type the name, for example *Site Survey*. Leave **Channel** empty to let the system choose from the name (*Site Survey* becomes **Visit**), or choose one. Drag the handle at the start of a row to change the order the types are offered in.

:::note[Good to know]
- Every member of staff who can open **Tender Manager › Configuration** can change **Tags**, **Customer Tags**, **Activity Types** and **Last Seen Stages**. Agree changes with the Head of Commercial first.
- **Last Seen Stages** are matched to customers by their **Name** (for example *31_60*), and the bands are fixed at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days. You can change the **Description** that people see, but don't rename, add or delete stages: customers would stop being sorted into them.
- Changing an activity type's **Channel** also changes it on every touch of that type already logged, including touches where someone picked a different channel by hand.
:::
