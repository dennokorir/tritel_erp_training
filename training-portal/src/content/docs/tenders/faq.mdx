## Why can't I see a tender on the portal?

The portal shows a tender only if you are its **Account Manager**, **Bid Liaison** or **Technical Pre-sales Engineer**, or if you head that person's role (the Head of Commercial for account managers and bid liaisons, the Head of Channel for presales). A tender with none of the three filled in is visible only to Tender administrators. Your user must also be linked to an employee: *You must be linked to an employee to access the tender portal.* In the back end, every internal user can see every tender. See [Who can do what](/tenders/who-can-do-what/).

## Why can't I leave Draft?

A tender needs both a **Bid Liaison** and a **Technical Pre-sales Engineer** before its status can change: *Missing bid liaison. Please assign one before proceeding!* This applies to **Drop** and **Cancel** too. Ask the Head of Commercial to assign the Bid Liaison and the Head of Channel to assign presales. See [Nominate the team](/tenders/how-to/bids/nominate-the-team/).

## Why can't I change the Status, or drag the card where I want?

The status changes only through the buttons: *Use the buttons on the tender to change its status.* On the board you can drag forward one step; moves that need details open the same window as the button. Dragging backwards is a reopen, for Tender administrators only. See [Tender status workflow](/tenders/workflows/tender-status/).

## Why don't I see Mark Submitted?

Only the tender's Bid Liaison, the Bid Liaison's manager and Tender administrators see it: *Only the Bid Liaison or their manager can mark a bid as submitted.* See [Mark a tender submitted](/tenders/how-to/bids/mark-submitted/).

## Who can reopen a tender?

Only a Tender administrator, one step back at a time, with a reason. **Won**, **Prequalified** and **Lost** go back to **Evaluation**; **Dropped** and **Cancelled** go back to the status they were closed from. The CRM opportunity is not reopened. See [Reopen a tender](/tenders/how-to/bids/reopen-a-tender/).

## Can I delete a tender?

No. Nobody can delete a tender from the screen. **Drop** or **Cancel** it instead.

## Why can't I flag a tender as must-win?

Only the Head of Commercial and Tender administrators can: *Only Head of Commercial or a Tender Tracker Administrator can change must-win.* See [Flag a must-win tender](/tenders/how-to/bids/flag-a-must-win/).

## Why can't I score this bid?

Only the Bid Liaison's manager scores it, or, if there is none, the Head of Commercial, then Tender administrators. The bid must have been submitted first: *This bid has not been submitted yet.* See [Score a bid's quality](/tenders/how-to/bids/score-bid-quality/).

## Why is my quality score 85.7% when I met 6 of 8 guidelines?

Guidelines marked **Not applicable** are left out. With one of the eight not applicable, the score is 6 ÷ 7. See [Bid quality score](/tenders/calculations/bid-quality-score/).

## Why did a disqualification count in a later quarter?

A confirmed error counts at 0% in the quarter it was **confirmed**, not the quarter the bid went in. See [Bid quality score](/tenders/calculations/bid-quality-score/).

## Why does Confirm Ready say the bid isn't ready?

At least one item is **Missing** (no file, or no **Valid Until** on a document that expires) or **Expired** (valid until a day before the deadline). Fix it or tick **Not Needed**. See [Readiness progress](/tenders/calculations/readiness-progress/).

## Why is there no bid bond on my checklist?

The *Bid bond* item appears only when **Bid Bond Required** is ticked on the tender, and that tick isn't on the tender form at the moment. Ask a Tender administrator. See [Work the bid readiness checklist](/tenders/how-to/bids/work-the-readiness-checklist/).

## Why does my bid show Submitted Late?

**Actual Submission** is later than the **Submission Deadline**, even by a minute. If the time was entered wrongly, the Bid Liaison's manager can correct it. See [Late submission and submission dates](/tenders/calculations/late-submission/).

## Why can't I add the receipt again?

Once a submission is recorded, the Bid Liaison can't record it again: *You can't record this submission.* Ask your manager or a Tender administrator. See [Late submission and submission dates](/tenders/calculations/late-submission/).

## Why is a Bid Liaison marked "Over fair share"?

Their effort points this quarter, plus this tender's, are more than 15% above the team average. You can still choose them if you give a reason. If the table is empty, nobody is on the **Bid Desk Team** list yet. See [Bid desk workload points](/tenders/calculations/workload-points/).

## Why can't I write the project summary?

Only the person named as **Summary Writer** can, and only while it is **Writing** or **Changes requested**: *Only the assigned writer can edit a project summary, while it's being written.* See [Write and submit a project summary](/tenders/how-to/customers/write-a-project-summary/).

## Why can't I approve a summary I wrote?

Someone else must review it: *You wrote this summary, so someone else needs to review it.* See [Review, send back or approve a summary](/tenders/how-to/customers/review-a-project-summary/).

## Why is my customer "Contested" when we still have a live opportunity?

An opportunity counts as open only in the CRM's three standard early stages. In a later stage, or archived, it counts like a lost one. See [The market coverage ladder](/tenders/calculations/coverage-ladder/).

## Why can't I see other people's customers on Customer Activities?

You see your own touches and accounts and those of the people who report to you. The Head of Commercial, Tender administrators and HR administrators see everyone: *You can only view yourself and the people who report to you.* See [Read the Customer Activities screen](/tenders/how-to/customers/read-the-customer-activities-screen/).

## Why can't I claim a customer?

Only account managers can claim, and only customers with no account manager: *Only account managers can claim customers.* See [Claim, assign and classify accounts](/tenders/how-to/customers/account-ownership/).

## Why is the actual gross profit on the business case higher than estimated?

The actual figures use the orders' totals including VAT, while cost lines are usually entered before tax. Compare the margin percentages instead. See [Business case margin](/tenders/calculations/business-case-margin/).

## Why didn't my OEM booking count?

Check three things: the booking is **Confirmed** (drafts never count), its **Booking Date** (the latest confirmation date of its purchase orders, not the portal date) falls in the review window, and its **OEM** is the one the target measures. See [OEM booking value and booking date](/tenders/calculations/oem-bookings/).

## Why did my booking move to another month?

A line from a later purchase order was added, or an order was cancelled and confirmed again, which moved the **Booking Date**. See [OEM booking value and booking date](/tenders/calculations/oem-bookings/).

## Why can't I see OEM Bookings or Untagged Purchases?

**OEM Bookings** needs a Channel role or purchasing administrator rights. **Untagged Purchases** is for Channel Managers only. See [Who can do what](/tenders/who-can-do-what/).

## What is the weekly Tenders/Commercial digest email?

Odoo sends a weekly summary email called **Tenders/Commercial Periodic Digest** to the people subscribed to it. Next to the usual CRM and revenue figures, it has two Tender Manager figures: **Activities Logged** (customer touches dated in the period) and **Tenders** (tenders created in the period). A system administrator chooses the recipients and figures under **Settings › Technical › Digest Emails** (developer mode), in the **Commercial/Tenders** group.

## Why can't I find the tender when I link a sales order?

The **Tender** field on a sales order only offers **Won** tenders for the order's customer. Check the tender's status and customer. See [Link orders and invoices to a tender](/tenders/how-to/bids/link-orders-and-invoices/).
