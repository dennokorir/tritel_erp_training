A customer's **organogram** shows the roles at the customer (for example *Director, ICT* or *Head of Procurement*), who holds each one and who reports to whom. A role can be empty until you know who holds it. The organogram belongs to the customer, so every year's account plan uses the same one. Each plan adds its own view of the people: their influence, how warm the relationship is and who at Tritel covers them.

## Steps

1. Open the account plan and go to the **Key people** tab. The plan must be saved first; until then the tab reads *Save the plan to see the organogram.*
2. If the chart is empty (*No roles yet. Use Build from Contacts, or open the full screen to add roles.*), click **Build from Contacts**. It adds one role for each of the customer's contacts that has none, named after their job title: *3 role(s) added from contacts.*
3. Read the compact view beside the chart:
   - ***x* of *y*** **roles with a named contact**;
   - ***x* of *y*** **decision makers and approvers warm** (from the **Influence** and **Relationship** you set on the key people list);
   - **Gaps** (up to eight): *No contact at Head of Finance.*, *Contact B (Head of Procurement) has not been met this year.* (for decision makers and approvers), *Contact C is cooling: last met 2026-03-15.*
4. To change the organogram, click **Open Full Screen** (or click the chart). From the customer's form, click the **Organogram** button (it reads, for example, **3 of 4 roles**, or **Not started**).
5. Choose what to see:
   - **Show**: **Everyone at the customer**, or **This plan's key people** (with the roles above them, so the structure still reads). The second choice is only there when you open the screen from a plan.
   - **Colour by**: **Relationship** (warm, neutral, cooling), **Last met** (in the last 90 days, in the last year, longer or never) or **Covered by** (someone at Tritel covers them, or nobody yet). The legend under the chart explains the colours. **−** and **+** zoom.
6. To add someone new, click **Add Person**. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Role (job title)** and **Reports to** (**Nobody (top)** for the most senior), then click **Save**. This also adds the person as a contact of the customer.
7. To add a role you haven't filled yet, click **Add Role**, give the **Role (job title)** and **Reports to**, and click **Save**. The card reads *No contact yet*.
8. To change who reports to whom, drag a card onto the card of their new manager. Drop it on the empty space of the chart to put it at the top.
9. Click a card to see the person in the panel on the right: **Role**, **Reports to**, **Influence**, **Relationship**, **Covered by**, **Last met**, **Email**, **Phone**, the plan's note and **Recent activity**. **Edit** opens the contact (or the empty role) to change it.
10. Click **Log Activity** to record a call, meeting or visit with this person. The *Log a customer touch* window opens with the customer and the person under **Contacts met** already filled in. See [Log a customer activity](/tenders/how-to/customers/log-a-customer-activity/).
11. To remove a role, select it, click **Remove Role**, then **Confirm: remove role**. The people who reported to it move up a level.

## What happens next

- The **Organogram** button on the customer shows how many roles have a named contact, for example **3 of 4 roles**.
- On the customer's **Contacts & Addresses** tab, each contact who holds a role shows it, for example *Head of Procurement · reports to Director, ICT*.
- Changing **Reports to** on the plan's **Key people** list changes the organogram too (and adds a role for that person if they had none).
- **Last met** comes from customer activities: each time you log a touch, pick the people you met in **Contacts met** (the box reads *Who you met (sets Last met on the organogram)*). It updates **Last met**, the **Last met** colours and the *not met this year* and *cooling* gaps.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *A role cannot report to itself or to one of its own reports.* | You dropped a card onto itself or onto someone below it. | Drop it onto someone above or beside it. |
| *A role can only report to a role at the same customer.* | The role chosen in **Reports to** belongs to another customer. | Choose a role at this customer. |
| *This contact already holds a role at this customer.* | A person can hold only one role at a customer. | Use the role they already hold, or change it. |
| *Contact D is not a contact of Customer A.* | The person you put in the role belongs to another company. | Add them as a contact of this customer first, or use **Add Person**. |
| *Give the person's name and role.* / *Give the role a title.* | **Name** or **Role (job title)** is empty in **Add Person** or **Add Role**. | Fill them in and click **Save**. |
| *Only Customer A's account manager or the Head of Commercial can change its organogram.* | You are not the customer's account manager or the Head of Commercial. | Ask the account manager to make the change. |
| *Customer A's account plan and organogram are only visible to its account manager, their managers and the Head of Commercial.* | You aren't in the customer's plan audience. | Ask the account manager or the Head of Commercial. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Influence**, **Relationship**, **Covered by** and the note come from a plan's **Key people** list. Opened from the customer's **Organogram** button there is no plan, so those stay empty, every card is drawn as *not in this plan*, and the only gaps listed are empty roles. Open the organogram from the plan to see the full picture.
- In the side panel, **Relationship** shows as *warm*, *neutral* or *cool* (on the plan it reads **Warm**, **Neutral** or **Cooling**).
- Removing a role removes the role only: the contact stays on the customer and on the plan's key people list.
- If an old browser tab shows text such as *5 of 7 roles* instead of the chart, reload the page.
:::
