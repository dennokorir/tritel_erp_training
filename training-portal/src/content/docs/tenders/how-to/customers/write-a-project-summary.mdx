A project summary is a short case study of a won tender, written under fixed headings. You write it, a manager reviews it, and once approved it goes into the summary library for sales and marketing. Pre-sales engineers are asked by default, but anyone can be named as the writer.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-summary-write" caption="A summary being written. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the summary. Use the link in the email, or open **Tender Manager › My Actions** and click the tender under **Summaries to write**. (**Tenders › Summary Tracker** also lists the summaries assigned to you.)
2. Check the **Due** date and the **Headings** bar, which shows how many required headings are filled, for example *6 of 8*.
3. In the **Classification** box, choose the **Primary Pillar**, any **Secondary Pillars**, **Tritel's Role** (**Prime contractor**, **Subcontractor** or **Partner**) and the **Motion** (**Project** or **Channel**).
4. Write in the text box under each heading. The headings are already there. Keep each heading on its own line, and type your text on the lines below it. **What to cover** on the right says what each heading needs.
5. Watch the **Headings** list on the right: a tick appears beside each heading once it has text. Headings marked **(optional)** can stay empty. Save as you go.
6. When every required heading is ticked, click **Submit for review**.

### If it was sent back

The status shows **Changes requested** and a yellow **Reviewer's note** says what to change. You get a new to-do and a new due date (7 days). Make the changes, then click **Submit for review** again. It appears in **My Actions** under **Summaries sent back to you**.

### On the portal

Open **Summaries** on the portal (**/my/summaries**) and pick the tender, or follow the portal link in the email. Choose the **Primary pillar**, **Secondary pillars**, **Tritel's role** and **Motion**, write in the box, then click **Save draft** or **Submit for review**.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-summary-portal-write" caption="Writing the same summary on the portal." />

## What happens next

- The status moves to **In review**. The review is due 5 days later.
- The manager who asked for it gets an email *Project summary to review* and a to-do *Review Project Summary*. If you asked for it yourself, it goes to another manager.
- A copy of the text as submitted is kept in the **History** at the bottom of the page.
- You can no longer edit it, unless the reviewer sends it back or reopens it.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Choose the primary pillar, Tritel's role, the motion. Add content under: The client's problem, Outcome.* | Something required is missing. The message lists exactly what. | Fill in what it names. If it names a heading you did write under, check the heading line is still spelled the same and on its own line. |
| *Only the assigned writer can edit a project summary, while it's being written.* | You are not the writer, or it is already in review or complete. | Ask the manager to reassign it, or wait for it to be sent back. |
| *Only the assigned writer can submit this summary, while it's being written.* | Same as above. | As above. |
| *Productivity is the primary pillar, so it can't be a secondary one as well.* | You ticked the primary pillar as a secondary too. | Untick it under **Secondary Pillars**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The **Pillars** heading is ticked by choosing the **Primary Pillar**, and **Tritel's role** by choosing both the role and the motion. You don't need to type under them.
- **Reference status** (whether the client can be named, contacts, quotes, photos) is required, but it never appears in the library or in marketing packs. Contact details stay internal.
- Text typed above the first heading doesn't count for any heading.
- On the portal, if **Submit for review** is refused, your draft is still saved. Read the red message, fix it and submit again.
- Overdue summaries get a reminder email the day after the due date, then every 7 days.
:::
