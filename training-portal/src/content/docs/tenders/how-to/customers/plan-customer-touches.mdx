Plan the customer touches you intend to make, so they show on your list until they are done. When the touch has happened, you complete it with the outcome: it then counts like any logged touch.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-touch-planned" caption="The Planned tab. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › Tender Customers › Customer Activities** and click **Plan touch**.
2. In *Plan a customer touch*, fill in the **Customer**, **Planned for** (the date), **Type**, **Who will make it** and the **Account Manager** (filled in from the customer). Link the **Tender** or **Lead/Pipeline** if there is one. Under **Notes**, say what the touch is for. Click **Save**. *Touch planned* appears.
3. Click the **Planned** tab. The number beside it counts your planned touches. They are grouped under **Overdue** (with how many days late), **This week**, **Later** and **No date**.
4. To move one, click **Reschedule**, pick the new date and click **Save**.
5. When the touch has happened, click **Complete**. In *Complete planned touch*, set the **Date** to the day it happened, write the outcome under **What happened**, and click **Save**.

**Open** shows the full record. It also has a **Mark as Done** button.

## What happens next

- A completed touch becomes **Done**: it leaves the **Planned** tab, its note is posted on the linked tender or opportunity, and it counts on the heatmap and timeline.
- A planned touch whose date has passed is **Overdue**: it shows in red in the activity list and counts as an **overdue plan** on the person's **People** profile.
- **My Actions** shows **Overdue planned customer touches** with a count of yours, and an **Open Customer Activities** button.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Activity date cannot be greater than today!* | You clicked **Complete** on a touch whose date is still in the future. | Change the **Date** to the day it really happened, then save. |
| *This planned touch is not one you can reschedule.* | It isn't on your accounts or your team's, or it was done meanwhile. | Refresh the page. |
| *This touch is not one you can complete.* | It isn't on your accounts or your team's. | Ask its owner or their manager. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Showing** at the top decides whose planned touches you see: **Me**, **My team**, a person who reports to you, or (for the Head of Commercial, Tender administrators and HR) **Everyone**.
- A planned touch counts nowhere until it is completed. Planning many touches doesn't raise anyone's figures.
:::
