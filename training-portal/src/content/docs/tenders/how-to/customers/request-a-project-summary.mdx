Every won tender should end up with a **project summary**: a short case study that sales and marketing can reuse as a reference. You start it by asking someone, usually the pre-sales engineer on the tender, to write it by a due date.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-summary-tracker" caption="The Summary Tracker. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › Tenders › Summary Tracker**. You see every won tender. To find the ones still waiting, click **Not requested** in the **Status** panel on the left.
2. Click the tender. Its project summary page opens. (From the tender itself, open the **Project Summary** tab and click **Open summary workspace**.)
3. Click **Request summary**.
4. In the window that opens:
   1. Check the **Writer**. It starts as the tender's pre-sales engineer. You can choose any staff member.
   2. Check the **Due** date. It starts 7 days from today.
   3. Click **Confirm**.
5. To request several at once, go back to the list, tick the tenders and click **Request summaries**. Pick a **Writer** on each row and one **Due** date for them all, then click **Request summaries**.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-summary-request" caption="Requesting one summary from its page." />

To hand an open request to someone else, or move its date, open the summary and click **Reassign**. The same window opens. **Reassign** is there while the summary is **Writing** or **Changes requested**.

You can do the same on the portal: open **Summaries** (**/my/summaries**), pick the tender, choose the **Writer** and **Due** date and click **Request summary** (or **Reassign**).

## What happens next

- The status moves to **Writing**. The writer gets a to-do, *Write Project Summary*, due on the date you chose, and an email *Project summary to write* that lists the headings to cover.
- The summary text is started for them: every heading is already in place, and **Client and sector** is filled in with the customer's name and sector where the system knows them.
- After a request for several tenders, a message confirms it, for example *Requested 3 summaries. Skipped 1 with no writer.* Each writer gets one email listing all their tenders.
- If you reassign, the new writer is emailed, and the old writer is told *This project summary is now with Employee B. You no longer need to write it.* If you only change the date, the writer gets *Project summary due date changed*.
- If the summary is not submitted by the due date, the writer and you get *Overdue: project summary* the day after, then every 7 days.
- Your **My Actions** shows a link, **Won tenders with no summary requested**, while any are left.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *A project summary can only be requested for a won tender.* | The tender is not **Won** (for example it was reopened). | Request it once the tender is won. |
| *This summary is under review or approved, so it can't be reassigned now.* | The writer has already submitted it, or it is complete. | Review it instead. See [Review, send back or approve a summary](/tenders/how-to/customers/review-a-project-summary/). |
| *Choose an active internal user as the writer.* | The writer is empty, archived or a portal-only user. | Pick a current staff member. |
| *Only an Administrator, Head of Commercial or Head of Channel can do this.* | You don't have one of those roles. | Ask the Head of Channel or Head of Commercial. |

:::note[Good to know]
- In the **Request summaries** window, tenders that already have a request, or aren't won, are left out. A blue line says how many, for example *2 selected tender(s) already have a request, or aren't won, and are left out.* Rows left without a writer are skipped.
- If a tender stops being **Won**, its summary to-dos are paused and the text is kept. When it is won again, the work picks up and the person it is waiting on is emailed.
- The workflow, with every role, is in the [project summary workflow](/tenders/workflows/project-summary/).
:::
