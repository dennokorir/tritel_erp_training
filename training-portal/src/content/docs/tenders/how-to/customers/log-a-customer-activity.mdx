A **customer touch** is any contact with a customer: a call, a meeting, a visit, a demo, an email. Log each one soon after it happens, and link it to the tender or opportunity it was about, so the team can see who is talking to which customers.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-touch-log" caption="Logging a touch. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › Tender Customers › Customer Activities**.
2. Click **Log touch**. The window *Log a customer touch* opens, with today's date.
3. Choose the **Customer**, check the **Date** and pick the **Type**. The **Channel** (for example **Call**, **Meeting**, **Visit**, **Workshop / Demo**) fills in from the type; change it if it's wrong.
4. **Touch made by** starts as you. If you are logging for someone who reports to you, pick them. The **Account Manager** fills in from the customer.
5. If the touch was about a deal, pick the **Tender** or the **Lead/Pipeline** (an opportunity). Only that customer's deals are listed. You can link one or the other, not both.
6. Under **Contacts met**, pick the customer's people you met or spoke to. Only that customer's contacts are listed, and you can pick several. This is what sets **Last met** for those people on the customer's organogram.
7. Under **What happened**, write what the customer said, the outcome and the next step. Click **Save**.

You can also click **Log touch** on a customer's touchpoint timeline (open it from the heatmap, or with the **Touchpoints** button on the customer). The customer is then filled in for you. **Log Activity** on a person's card in the customer's organogram opens the same window with the customer and that person under **Contacts met** already filled in.

**Contacts met** is also on the full customer activity form and as a column in the activity list, so you can add the people to a touch you logged earlier.

## What happens next

- The touch is saved as **Done** and *Touch logged* appears.
- If you linked a tender or opportunity, your note is posted on its chatter as *Feedback from customer touch dated 2026-11-16*.
- If it is the customer's latest touch, the customer's **Last Seen** date moves to it.
- Each person under **Contacts met** shows this date as **Last met** on the customer's organogram and on the **Key people** tab of its account plan, and the touch appears under their **Recent activity**. See [Map the customer organogram](/tenders/how-to/account-plans/map-the-customer-organogram/).
- It counts straight away on the **Customers** heatmap, the customer's timeline, your **People** profile and the **Customer touches** strip at the top of the tender or opportunity.
- A touch linked to a tender or opportunity can also count towards the people's achievements on that deal.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Activity date cannot be greater than today!* | The **Date** is in the future. A touch can't be done before it happens. | Set the date it happened. For a future touch, use **Plan touch**. |
| *Date is Mandatory!* | The date was cleared on a touch being marked done. | Fill in the **Date**. |
| *This touch is not one you can complete.* | The touch isn't on your accounts or your team's, so you can't mark it done. | Ask the account manager, or their manager, to log it. |

:::note[Good to know]
- If saving is refused with *Activity date cannot be greater than today!*, the touch has already been saved, but as **Planned**. Find it on the **Planned** tab and click **Complete** with the right date, so you don't log it twice.
- A touch entered more than 7 days after it happened is flagged **Entered Late**.
- There are two different "phase" ideas on a touch:
  - **Touch Phase** (**Account-level**, **Pipelining**, **Bid Support**, **Post-submission**, **Delivery & Collection**) follows the **date of the touch**. It is what the Customer Activities screens show.
  - **Valid** and **Activity Phase** (**Pipelining**, **Bid Submission Support**, **Post-Submission Influence**) follow the **day you entered it** in Odoo. They are what target achievements and the portal dashboard count. A touch with no tender or opportunity, or entered before the deal was registered, or after the award date, is not valid for them.
- So log touches on the day, and always link the deal.
- **Contacts met** is optional, but a person nobody has logged as met has no **Last met** date. The organogram's gaps then list them as not met this year.
:::
