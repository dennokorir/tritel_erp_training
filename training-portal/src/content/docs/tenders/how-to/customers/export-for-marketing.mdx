A **marketing pack** is one text file holding the complete project summaries you choose, ready for marketing to turn into case studies or web pages. Each summary's **Customer naming** decides whether its client is named or anonymised.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-marketing-export" caption="The Export for Marketing window. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › Tenders › Export for Marketing**. (You can also click **Export for Marketing** in the summary library or the Summary Tracker list. In the library's **Gallery**, tick cards first, then click **Export 3 for Marketing** to start with just those.)
2. Under **Which summaries**, narrow the pack. Leave **Only these tenders** empty to take every complete summary that matches the other choices: **Pillars**, **Sectors**, **OEMs**, **Awarded** (from and to year), **Tritel's role** and **Motion**.
3. Under **What goes in**:
   - Tick **Include "Not yet asked" as anonymised** to add summaries whose customer hasn't been asked about naming. Otherwise they are held back.
   - Untick **Contract value** to leave the value out.
   - The badges count what the pack will hold, for example *4 named*, *2 anonymised*, *1 held back: not yet asked*, *3 not complete*.
4. Read the **Preview** tab. In **Brief for the writer** you can change the instructions printed at the top of the pack for this export.
5. Click **Download pack (.md)**.

## What happens next

- A file named like *tritel-marketing-pack-20261116.md* downloads.
- The export is logged under **Tender Manager › Tenders › Marketing Exports**: **Exported on**, who exported it, the number of cases and anonymised cases, the filters and the **Pack** file. You see your own exports; Tender administrators see everyone's.
- Anonymised cases have the client's name and the tender's own reference removed, and the client is described by its sector instead.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *No Complete summaries match these filters.* | Nothing complete fits your choices, or every match is held back as *Not yet asked*. | Widen the filters, or tick **Include "Not yet asked" as anonymised**. |
| *Only people who can open the project summary library can export it.* | You have no tender role or library access. | Ask for library access. |
| *Only a Tender Tracker Administrator can change the default brief.* | You clicked **Save as default brief** without the Tender administrator role. | Change the brief for this export only, or ask a Tender administrator. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The pack never includes drafts, reviewer notes, people's names or the **Reference status** section. Email addresses and phone numbers are removed.
- Contract values are exact. In public material, don't put an exact value next to an anonymised client: round it or leave it out.
- **Save as default brief** (Tender administrators only) keeps your brief for everyone's future exports.
:::
