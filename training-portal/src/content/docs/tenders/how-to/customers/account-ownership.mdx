Every customer Tritel has bid for, pitched to or touched should have an **account manager**. **Account Ownership** lists the ones that don't, with a suggested owner. **Classify Accounts** is where sectors are set and **target accounts** (the customers the company has chosen to win) are proposed, approved and tiered.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-account-ownership" caption="Account Ownership, with the Assign window open. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps: owners

1. Open **Tender Manager › Tender Customers › Account Ownership**. It opens on **Needs Owner (engaged, no AM)**: customers with a tender, opportunity or done touch but no account manager. **Suggested Owner** and **Why** say who should probably own each one.
2. **Account managers**: tick customers that are yours and click **Claim for Me**.
3. **Head of Commercial**: tick customers and click **Accept Suggestion** to give each one its **Suggested Owner**. Customers with no suggestion are skipped.
4. **Head of Commercial**: to choose the owner yourself, tick customers and click **Assign To…**.
5. In *Assign account manager*, pick the **Account Manager**, add a **Note** if you like (it goes on each customer's chatter), and click **Assign**.

To tidy records, use the other filters: **Owner Archived**, **Organisation Saved as Individual** (tick them and click **Mark as Company**) and **Possible Duplicate** (click the merge icon on the row, *Merge with look-alikes*). These buttons are for the Head of Commercial and Tender administrators.

<Illustration name="tenders/cust-classify-accounts" caption="Classify Accounts." />

## Steps: sectors and target accounts

6. Open **Tender Manager › Tender Customers › Classify Accounts**. It opens on **Unclassified (no sector)**. You can type the **Sector** and **Segment** on each row. To take the system's suggestions, tick rows and click **Apply Suggested Sector** (Head of Commercial).
7. **Account managers**: to suggest a target account, tick it and click **Propose as Key Account** or **Propose as Major Growth**. You can also use the same two buttons next to **Target** on the customer's form. The form then reads *Proposed as Key account, awaiting approval* (or *Major growth*), and the list shows the tier in **Proposed Tier**.
8. **Head of Commercial**: to make customers target accounts directly, tick them and click **Target: Key Account**, **Target: Major Growth** or **Target: Maintain**.
9. **Head of Commercial**: use the **Proposed Targets** filter, check **Proposed Tier**, tick proposals and click **Approve Proposed** (each gets the tier that was proposed, or **Major growth** if none was) or **Decline Proposed**. On a customer's form, a blue line *Proposed as a target by Employee A* has **Approve** and **Decline** buttons.

**Awarded, gross (4Q)** and **Awarded, attributed (4Q)** show the tender value awarded to each customer over the last four quarters (in total, and the account manager's credited share), to help you choose. **Tier Since** (hidden by default; add it from the column picker) is when each customer's current tier was set.

## What happens next

- A message confirms each action, for example *2 customer(s) claimed*, *3 customer(s) assigned*, *Proposed as target account(s)* or *1 target account(s) approved*. Each change is noted on the customer's chatter.
- When a customer gets an account manager, that person also moves onto the customer's draft and bidding tenders, planned touches and early-stage opportunities.
- Owned customers leave the **Needs Owner** list. In **Market Coverage** they count for their account manager, and **Reached, no account manager** goes down.
- Approved target accounts get a **Target Since** date and count in **Market Coverage** under **Target accounts**.
- Setting or changing a tier sets **Tier Since** to today. A tier counts towards the account manager's **Account plans reviewed** KPI only from the quarter after **Tier Since**: *A tier counts for a quarter only if it was set before the quarter started.* So tier accounts before the quarter begins.
- **Key account** and **Major growth** accounts need an [account plan](/tenders/concepts/account-plans/) for the year. On a company customer's form, the account manager, their managers and the Head of Commercial see three buttons: **Account Plan** (this year's plan and its status, for example *2026 · reviewed 14 Apr*, or *No plan*; it opens the plan, or starts one), **Organogram** (for example *5 of 7 roles*, or *Not started*) and **Awarded, 4Q** (the account manager's credited share of tenders awarded in the last four quarters). See [Write an account plan](/tenders/how-to/account-plans/write-an-account-plan/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Only account managers can claim customers.* | You don't hold the account manager role. | Ask the Head of Commercial to assign the customer. |
| *Customer A already has an account manager.* | One of the ticked customers is already owned. | Untick it. To change an owner, ask the Head of Commercial. |
| *Only the Head of Commercial or a Tender Administrator can assign account managers.* | You clicked **Accept Suggestion**, **Assign To…** or **Mark as Company** without that role. | Use **Claim for Me**, or ask the Head of Commercial. |
| *Only the Head of Commercial or a Tender Administrator can set target accounts. Use "Propose as Target" instead.* | You ticked **Target Account** or changed the tier yourself. | Click **Propose as Key Account** or **Propose as Major Growth**. |
| *Only the Head of Commercial or a Tender Administrator can approve target accounts.* (also *…decline proposed targets.*, *…set target tiers.*, *…classify customers in bulk.*) | You clicked a Head of Commercial button. | Ask the Head of Commercial. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Customers also get an owner on their own: when a tender or opportunity is saved for a customer with no account manager, the deal's account manager becomes the customer's.
- **Why** explains the suggestion: *Account manager on 2 of its deals*, *Most recent of 3 account managers on its deals*, *Made 4 of its customer touches*, or no suggestion with *Previous account manager archived* or *No deals or touches by an account manager*.
- **Accept Suggestion** that finds nothing to do shows *0 customer(s) assigned*.
- **Approve Proposed** makes every ticked customer a target account, whether it was proposed or not. Tick only the proposals.
- The message *…Use "Propose as Target" instead.* still names the old button. The buttons are now **Propose as Key Account** and **Propose as Major Growth**.
- On the customer's form, the two **Propose** buttons appear only to people who can't set targets themselves. The Head of Commercial sets the tier directly.
- **Key account** and **Major growth** were called **Strategic** and **Growth** until November 2026. Existing target accounts kept their tier under the new name.
- The **Awarded** columns, and the three account plan buttons, are empty for anyone outside the customer's [account plan audience](/tenders/concepts/account-plan-privacy/). A Tender administrator who is not the account manager or one of their managers sees **0** in the **Awarded** columns.
:::
