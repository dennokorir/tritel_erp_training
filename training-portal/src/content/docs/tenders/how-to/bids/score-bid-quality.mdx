Within 10 days of a bid going in, the Bid Liaison's manager checks it against the **bid quality guidelines**: signed forms, stamps, format, certificates, bid security, technical and financial completeness, and on-time delivery. Each guideline is **Met**, **Not met** or **Not applicable**. The score is the share of applicable guidelines that were met.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-quality-score" caption="Scoring a bid: one guideline not met and one not applicable gives 3 of 4, or 75%. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › My Actions**. Bids waiting for you are under **Bid quality checks to score**; click one. (In the tender list, the **Quality Check To Score** filter finds them too.)
2. On the tender's **Bid Quality** tab, click **Score Bid**.
3. In **Bid Quality Check**, check **Actual Submission**. Correct it if the recorded time is wrong; it can't be in the future.
4. For each guideline, choose the **Result**: **Met**, **Not met** or **Not applicable**. Add a **Note** on the row to say what was wrong.
5. Use the **Note** at the bottom for anything the Bid Liaison should know.
6. Click **Save Score**.

To change a score later, click **Change Score** on the **Bid Quality** tab. The window opens with your earlier answers.

## What happens next

- **Quality Check** becomes **Scored**, with **Quality Score (%)**, **Scored By**, **Scored On** and your note. The guideline results are listed on the tab.
- **Quality Score (%)** = guidelines **Met** ÷ guidelines that apply × 100, to one decimal. In the picture: 3 met out of 4 that apply = **75.0%**. See [Bid quality score](/tenders/calculations/bid-quality-score/).
- The Bid Liaison gets a *Bid quality check* email with the score and your note. The chatter records the score, or the change from the old score to the new one.
- The bid leaves your **My Actions**.
- In Accountability, the score feeds the Bid Liaison's **Bid Quality Score** (a must-win bid counts three times) and the guidelines **Not met** feed their **Repeat Error Rate**. Unscored bids are left out. See [How metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).
- If you corrected **Actual Submission**, **Submitted Late** is worked out again.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *This bid has not been submitted yet.* | The tender hasn't been marked submitted. | Wait for the Bid Liaison to mark it submitted. |
| *Only the Bid Liaison's manager can score this bid.* | You aren't the person who scores this bid (see the box at the top). | Ask the Bid Liaison's manager. If the manager is wrong, HR updates the employee record. |
| *The actual submission date cannot be in the future.* | **Actual Submission** is later than now. | Enter the real delivery time. |
| *At least one guideline must apply to this bid.* | Every row is **Not applicable**. | Mark the guidelines that apply as **Met** or **Not met**. |
| *Must-win, quality check and disqualification details can only be changed with the buttons on the tender.* | Someone tried to edit the score fields directly. | Use **Score Bid** or **Change Score**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- The check is due 10 days after the bid is marked submitted. If it's late, you get an *Overdue: bid quality check* email the day after the due date, then weekly.
- The guidelines are kept under **Tender Manager › Configuration › Bid Quality Guidelines** by the Head of Commercial. A scored bid keeps the wording it was scored against, so changing the list doesn't change past scores.
- A bid that was disqualified for something the Bid Liaison caused counts as 0% once the disqualification is confirmed, whatever its quality score. See [Confirm the cause of a disqualification](/tenders/how-to/bids/confirm-a-disqualification/).
:::
