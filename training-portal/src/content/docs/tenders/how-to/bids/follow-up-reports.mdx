Two PDF reports list each deal with the follow-up updates logged on it, ready for a pipeline or tender review meeting. The **Tender Follow Up Report** covers tenders (by default those in **Evaluation**, waiting for the customer's decision). The **Pipeline Follow Up Report** covers CRM opportunities.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-follow-up-reports" caption="The two report windows side by side. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

**Tender Follow Up Report**

1. Open **Tender Manager › Reporting › Tender Follow Up Report**.
2. Set **From Date** and **To Date**. They start at the first of this month and today. Only updates dated in this range are printed.
3. Optionally choose an **Account Manager**, and one or more **Status** values. Leave **Status** empty for tenders in **Evaluation**.
4. Click **Print Report**. The PDF lists **Ref**, **Name/Description**, **Customer**, **Account Manager**, **Status** and **Updates**: each update's date, the person responsible and their feedback.

**Pipeline Follow Up Report**

5. Open **CRM › Reporting › Pipeline Follow Up Report**.
6. Set **From Date** and **To Date** the same way.
7. Optionally choose an **Account Manager** and one or more **Stages**. Leave **Stages** empty for all stages.
8. Click **Print Report**. The PDF lists **Opportunity**, **Customer**, **Account Manager**, **Stage**, **Expected Revenue** and **Updates**.

## What happens next

- The PDF downloads; nothing in Odoo changes.
- Every tender or opportunity matching the filters is listed, even with no updates in the range. An empty **Updates** cell shows a deal nobody followed up in that period. If nothing matches, the report says *No tenders found matching the selected criteria.* (or *No pipeline items found matching the selected criteria.*).
- In Accountability, **Tender evaluation coverage** measures the same thing week by week: whether the account manager and presales logged an update on each tender in Evaluation. See [How metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).

:::note[Good to know]
- An update is any customer activity on the deal whose **Date** falls in the range, planned ones included. An activity with no feedback shows *No feedback*.
- The date range filters the updates, not the deals: a tender in the chosen status is listed whatever its dates.
- **Expected Revenue** on the pipeline report is the opportunity's figure, without a currency sign.
:::
