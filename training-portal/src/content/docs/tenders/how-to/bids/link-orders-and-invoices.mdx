Once a tender is won, the sales orders, purchase orders, invoices and bills that deliver it should point back to it. The tender's **Sales**, **Purchases**, **Invoices** and **Bills** buttons then show everything sold, bought and billed for it in one place. Link the order when you create it. Invoices made from a linked order are linked for you.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-link-orders" caption="A sales order linked to a won tender. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps: a sales order

1. Open **Sales › Orders** and open the quotation or order (or create it).
2. In **Tender**, under **Payment Terms**, choose the tender. The list only offers **Won** tenders for the order's customer.
3. Save.
4. A **Tender** smart button appears at the top of the order. Click it to open the tender.

## Steps: a purchase order

1. Open **Purchase › Orders** and open the request for quotation or order.
2. In **Tender**, next to the currency, choose the tender. The list offers every **Won** tender, whatever the customer.
3. Save. A **Tender** smart button appears.

## Steps: an invoice or bill made by hand

1. Open the customer invoice, credit note, vendor bill or refund.
2. Open the **Other Info** tab. Under **Tender**, choose the tender.
3. Save. A **Tender** smart button appears.

## What happens next

- The tender's buttons count the linked records: **Sales** (sales orders), **Purchases** (purchase orders), **Invoices** (customer invoices and credit notes) and **Bills** (vendor bills and refunds). Each button appears only once there is something to show, and opens the records.
- An invoice or bill created from a linked sales or purchase order gets the same **Tender** by itself.
- On a customer's form, the **Tenders** button lists every tender for that customer. On an opportunity, the **Tenders** button lists the tenders created from it.

:::note[Good to know]
- The **Tender** field on an invoice or bill isn't limited to won tenders, and can point to any customer's tender. Check you picked the right one.
- Changing the **Tender** on an order later doesn't change invoices already made from it. Correct those on their **Other Info** tab.
:::
