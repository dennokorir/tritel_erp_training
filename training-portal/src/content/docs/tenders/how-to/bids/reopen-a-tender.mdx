Statuses only move forward for everyone else. When an outcome was recorded by mistake, or a closed tender comes back to life, a Tender administrator **reopens** it. Reopening moves the tender back **one step** and records the reason.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-reopen" caption="Reopening a Lost tender. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open the tender from **Tender Manager › Tenders › All Tenders**.
2. Click **Reopen**. (Dragging the card one column back on the board opens the same window.)
3. In **Reopen Tender**, type the **Reason**.
4. Click **Save**.

Where the tender goes:

| Status now | Reopens to |
|---|---|
| **Won**, **Prequalified** or **Lost** | **Evaluation** |
| **Dropped** or **Cancelled** | The status it was closed from (**Draft**, **Bidding** or **Evaluation**) |
| **Evaluation** | **Bidding** |
| **Bidding** | **Draft** |
| **Draft** | Can't be reopened |

To go back more than one step, reopen again.

## What happens next

- The status and board column change, and the chatter notes *Reopened to Evaluation by Employee E. Reason: …*. No email goes out.
- **Leaving Lost or Dropped** clears any disqualification on the tender (the chatter notes *Disqualification cleared: the tender was reopened.*).
- **Leaving Won or Prequalified:** the CRM opportunity is **not** reopened. The chatter reminds you: *The pipeline opportunity was not reopened. Reopen it in CRM if needed.* A project summary in progress is paused; it resumes, and the person it waits on is emailed, if the tender is marked Won again. Achievement lines already added stay on the tender.
- A prequalification already created stays as it is.
- The tender then moves forward again with the normal buttons. Moving forward sends the usual emails again, for example the won alert.

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Only Tender Tracker Administrators can reopen a tender.* | Someone without the Tender administrator role tried to reopen. | Ask a Tender administrator. |
| *Only Tender Tracker Administrators can move a tender back.* | Someone without the role dragged a card to an earlier column. | Ask a Tender administrator. |
| *Please give a reason for reopening.* | **Reason** is empty. | Type the reason. |
| *This tender can't be reopened.* | The tender is in **Draft**. | Nothing to do: Draft is the first step. |
| *A tender in Won can't move to Bidding.* | You dragged the card more than one step back, or to another outcome. | Reopen one step at a time, then use the buttons. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Reopening a tender from **Evaluation** to **Bidding** keeps its **Actual Submission** and quality check as they were. If the bid is resubmitted, the manager can correct **Actual Submission** with **Change Score**.
- Tenders can't be deleted, by anyone. Reopen or cancel instead, so the history stays.
:::
