When a tender is lost or dropped because of an avoidable error, such as a missing signature or an expired certificate, the person closing it records who caused it. A manager then **confirms the cause** within 7 days. Only a confirmed disqualification counts against anyone, so check the evidence and correct the cause if it's wrong.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-dq-confirm" caption="Confirming a disqualification caused by the Bid Liaison. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › My Actions**. Tenders waiting for you are under **Disqualifications to confirm**; click one. (The **Disqualification To Confirm** filter in the tender list finds them too.)
2. Open the tender's **Bid Quality** tab.
3. Read the **Disqualification** section: **Caused By**, **Evidence**, **Recorded By** and **Confirm By**. The **Tender Lost Reason** or **Drop Reason** tab and the chatter show the rest.
4. Click **Confirm Cause**.
5. In **Confirm Disqualification**, read the **Evidence** and check **Caused By**: **Bid Liaison**, **Technical Presales**, **Account Manager**, **OEM or supplier**, **Client** or **Other**. Change it if the evidence points elsewhere, and explain in **Note**.
6. Click **Confirm**.

## What happens next

- **Cause kept, or changed to someone you also answer for:** **Disqualification** becomes **Confirmed**, with **Confirmed By** and **Confirmed On**. The chatter records it. If the cause names a team member (Bid Liaison, Technical Presales or Account Manager), that person gets a *Disqualification confirmed* email.
- **Cause changed to someone another manager answers for** (for example from Bid Liaison to Technical Presales): it stays **To Confirm** and moves to that manager, who is emailed *Disqualification to confirm*. The chatter records the change.
- The tender leaves your **My Actions**.
- In Accountability, a confirmed disqualification caused by the **Bid Liaison** counts as 0% in their **Bid Quality Score**, in the quarter it was confirmed; one caused by **Technical Presales** counts as 0% in their **Bid Compliance**. Other causes don't count against either. Must-win tenders count three times. See [Bid quality score](/tenders/calculations/bid-quality-score/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *There is no disqualification waiting to be confirmed on this tender.* | It was already confirmed, or the tender was reopened, which clears it. | Refresh the page and check the **Bid Quality** tab. |
| *You are not the person who confirms this disqualification.* | You aren't the manager of the person named, or you are the person at fault. | Leave it to the manager it is waiting on. |
| *Must-win, quality check and disqualification details can only be changed with the buttons on the tender.* | Someone tried to edit the disqualification fields directly. | Use **Confirm Cause**. |

:::note[Good to know]
- **Confirm By** is 7 days after the disqualification was recorded. If it's late, the confirmer gets an *Overdue: disqualification to confirm* email the day after, then weekly.
- If a Tender administrator reopens the tender, the disqualification is cleared, confirmed or not.
- Choosing a disqualification reason is the only way a disqualification is recorded. The **Submitted Late** tick on its own doesn't create one.
:::
