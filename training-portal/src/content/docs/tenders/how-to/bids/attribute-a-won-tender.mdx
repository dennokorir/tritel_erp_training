When a tender is won, each person on it is credited with a share of its value through an **achievement line** on the tender's **Target Achievements** tab. The share comes from **attribution points** for three stages of the work: **Pipelining**, **Bid Submission Support** and **Post-Submission Influence**. This is live: the Accountability revenue measures read these lines, and a win with no points counts as nothing.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-attribute-tab" caption="A won tender with lines for all three roles; the presales line still has 0 points. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › My Actions**. Won tenders waiting for you are under **Achievements to attribute**; click one. (The **HoC Attribution Pending** and **HoCh Attribution Pending** filters in the tender list find them too.)
2. Open the **Target Achievements** tab and click **Add a line**.
3. In **Target**, choose the person's individual target (it shows as *target name - person*), and choose the **Role** they played on this tender. **Name**, **Date From** and **Date To** fill in with the quarter of the award date, for example *Q4 2026*. Save the tender. **Gross Value** shows the tender's value in KES.
4. Click the open icon (↗) at the start of the line. On the achievement, under **Stage Attribution**, type the points for each stage. The box shows each stage's maximum for the role.
5. Or click **Compute Related Activities** to work the points out from the customer activities this person logged on the tender and its opportunity: 3 valid activities in a stage give the full points for that stage.
6. Check **Overall Achievement** (the sum of the stages, out of 100) and save. Repeat for each person you attribute.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-attribute-form" caption="Steps 4 to 6: stage points for a Technical Presales line." />

The maximum points per stage, which always add up to 100:

| Role | Pipelining | Bid Submission Support | Post-Submission Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account Manager** | 40 | 20 | 40 |
| **Bid Liaison** | (none) | 60 | 40 |
| **Technical Presales** | 60 | 40 | (none) |

## What happens next

- **Achieved Value** = **Gross Value** × **% Achievement** ÷ 100. In the first picture the account manager's 80 points on KES 12,000,000 give KES 9,600,000. If two lines share a role on the same tender, the value is split equally between them.
- Once the Account Manager and Bid Liaison both have a line, the tender leaves the Head of Commercial's **Achievements to attribute**; once the Presales has one, it leaves the Head of Channel's. The tender's card on the board is no longer amber for it.
- **Compute Related Activities** lists the activities it used on the achievement's **Activities** tab. An activity counts when it was logged (entered) after the opportunity or tender was created and not after the award date. Activities on the opportunity count as **Pipelining**; on the tender, as **Bid Submission Support** up to the deadline date and **Post-Submission Influence** after it.
- In Accountability, the lines feed **Achieved revenue (attributed)** for each role, plus the Account Manager's **Gross awarded value**, **Awarded tenders**, **Attribution rate** and **Awarded value growth vs same quarter last year**, all counted by award date. See [How metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/). How the split works is on [How achievements are split and counted](/tenders/commercial-targets/achievements/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *The 'Award Date' field is missing. Please provide a value before executing this action.* | The tender has no **Award Date**. | Ask a Tender administrator to check the tender; a won tender should have one. |
| *'Pipelining' cannot exceed 0 for role 'Bid Liaison'.* | You typed points in a stage above that role's maximum. | Keep each stage within the maximum in the table. |
| *'Post-Submission Influence' cannot exceed 40 for role 'Account Manager'.* | Same as above. | Lower the points. |
| *'Bid Submission Support' cannot be negative.* | A negative number was typed. | Enter 0 or more. |

:::note[Good to know]
- A line with 0 points clears the "pending" flag but earns nothing. Accountability flags these as *award(s) awaiting attribution*, so always enter the points.
- **Compute Related Activities** replaces any points typed by hand, and doesn't rerun by itself. Click it again after more activities are logged, or retype the points afterwards.
- Only activities where the person is **Responsible** count. Activities logged by someone else on their behalf don't.
- Only the Head of Commercial, the Head of Channel and Tender administrators can add or change lines, and they can change any line, whatever the role. The **Role** isn't checked against the person's role on the tender, so each head should attribute only their own side.
- Everyone else can only read the lines that credit them or the people who report to them. On someone else's won tender, the **Target Achievements** tab shows only those lines.
- A **Prequalified** tender also shows the **Target Achievements** tab but never asks for attribution: its RFQs earn instead when won.
- Commercial Targets role and individual targets are being replaced by Accountability revenue targets, but attribution lines still need an individual target to attach to. See [Commercial Targets: what's live and what's being replaced](/tenders/commercial-targets/).
:::
