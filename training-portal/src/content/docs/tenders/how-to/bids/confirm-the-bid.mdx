**Confirm Bid** records the decision to bid: the tender moves from **Draft** to **Bidding** and the bid team starts work. If you decide not to bid, use **Drop** instead (see [Record the result](/tenders/how-to/bids/record-the-result/)). Make the decision early: confirming or dropping at least 5 days before the deadline is measured.

<Illustration name="tenders/bid-confirm-bid" caption="A Draft tender ready to confirm. The numbers match the steps below." />

## Steps

1. Open **Tender Manager › Tenders › All Tenders** and click the tender's card in the **Draft** column.
2. Check that **Bid Liaison** and **Technical Pre-sales Engineer** are filled in. If not, ask the Head of Commercial or Head of Channel to [nominate them](/tenders/how-to/bids/nominate-the-team/).
3. On the **Tender Info** tab, check the **Submission Deadline**. Correct it there if the customer has moved it, then save.
4. Click **Confirm Bid**.

You can also drag the card from the **Draft** column to **Bidding** on the board: it does the same thing.

## What happens next

- The status changes to **Bidding** and the card moves to the **Bidding** column.
- **Bid Decision Made** is stamped with the date and time (the first time only).
- A **Bid Readiness** tab appears with the document checklist, built from the standard bid documents, and **Readiness** shows **In Progress**. The Bid Liaison and Presales work through it: see [Work the bid readiness checklist](/tenders/how-to/bids/work-the-readiness-checklist/).
- From 5 days before the deadline the tender appears in the Bid Liaison's and Presales' **My Actions** under **Bids to confirm ready**. If it still isn't confirmed ready, they get reminder emails 5 and 3 days before the deadline.
- No email goes out and the CRM opportunity doesn't change.
- In Accountability, **Bid Decision Lead Time** counts the tender as met when it was confirmed (or dropped) at least 5 days before its deadline. See [How metrics count](/accountability/calculations/metric-families/).

## Common mistakes

| Message | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Missing bid liaison. Please assign one before proceeding!* | **Bid Liaison** is empty. | Ask the Head of Commercial to nominate one. |
| *Missing technical presales engineer. Please assign one before proceeding* | **Technical Pre-sales Engineer** is empty. | Ask the Head of Channel to nominate one. |
| *A tender in Draft can't move to Evaluation.* | You dragged the card past **Bidding** on the board. | Drag it to **Bidding** first. It moves to Evaluation only when the Bid Liaison marks it submitted. |

:::note[Good to know]
- Anyone who can open the tender can click **Confirm Bid**; there is no role check on the button itself.
- The team rule applies to every status change after Draft, not only this one: until a Bid Liaison and a Technical Presales are assigned, nobody except a Tender administrator can drop, cancel or otherwise move the tender either.
:::
