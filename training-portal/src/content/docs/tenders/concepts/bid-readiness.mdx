Every bid needs the same core documents: a signed form of tender, company registration, tax compliance, licences, a manufacturer's authorisation, the technical response and the financial proposal. A missing signature or an expired certificate can disqualify an otherwise good bid. The **bid readiness checklist** makes sure each one is there, and in date, before the bid goes in.

When a tender moves to **Bidding** (with **Confirm Bid**), the system builds its checklist from the **Standard Bid Documents** list and sets **Readiness** to **In Progress**. Each row is one document, prepared either by the **Bid Liaison** or by **Technical Presales**. Attach the file to its row; for documents that expire, also fill in **Valid Until**. Tick **Not Needed** for anything this tender doesn't ask for.

When every row is **Ready** or **Not Needed**, the Bid Liaison clicks **Confirm Ready**. **Readiness** becomes **Confirmed Ready** and the rows are locked. The aim is to confirm at least 72 hours before the **Submission Deadline**.

<Illustration name="tenders/concept-readiness-tab" caption="The Bid Readiness tab while the checklist is open. The numbers match the list below." />

1. **Readiness**: **Not Started**, **In Progress** or **Confirmed Ready**.
2. **Progress**: how many of the rows that count are ready, such as *4 of 6 ready*. Rows marked **Not Needed** don't count.
3. **Confirm Ready** locks the checklist. Once confirmed, **Reopen** unlocks it again.
4. The rows: **Document**, **Prepared By**, **Document Files**, **Valid Until** (only for documents that expire), **Not Needed** and **Status**.
5. A row ticked **Not Needed** is left out of the count.

## What each row status means

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| **Ready** | A file is attached and, if the document expires, **Valid Until** is on or after the deadline day. |
| **Missing** | No file is attached, or the document expires and **Valid Until** is empty. |
| **Expired** | **Valid Until** is before the deadline day (Nairobi time). |
| **Not Needed** | **Not Needed** is ticked. |

## The standard documents

| Document | Prepared By | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| Signed form of tender | Bid Liaison | No |
| Certificate of incorporation and CR12 | Bid Liaison | No |
| Tax compliance certificate | Bid Liaison | Yes |
| Licences and accreditations, such as ICTA | Bid Liaison | Yes |
| Manufacturer authorisation letter | Technical Presales | Yes |
| Technical compliance response | Technical Presales | No |
| Financial proposal | Bid Liaison | No |
| Bid bond | Bid Liaison | Yes, and only when the tender needs a bid bond |

The Head of Commercial and Tender administrators keep this list at **Tender Manager › Configuration › Standard Bid Documents**. Changes apply to checklists built from then on.

## Who can work the checklist

| Who | Can |
|---|---|
| The tender's **Bid Liaison**, their manager, Tender administrators | Create the checklist, add and remove rows, edit any row, **Confirm Ready** and **Reopen**. |
| The tender's **Technical Presales** engineer | Edit the rows prepared by Technical Presales, while the checklist is open. |
| Anyone else | Read it. Editing stops with *Only the person preparing this document, the Bid Liaison or their manager can change it.* |

**Create Checklist** builds the checklist by hand, for example on a tender that was already in **Bidding** before checklists existed.

## Reminders

- **Emails:** 5 days and 3 days before the deadline, if a **Draft** or **Bidding** tender isn't confirmed ready, the Bid Liaison and the Technical Presales engineer get *Bid not yet confirmed ready*: *This bid closes in 3 day(s) and isn't confirmed ready. It should be confirmed at least 72 hours before the deadline.*
- **My Actions:** the Bid Liaison and the Technical Presales engineer see **Bids to confirm ready** from 6 days before the deadline. An item is overdue once there are less than 72 hours left.

:::note[Good to know]
- **Confirm Ready** checks again for a bid bond if the tender now needs one, so a late change can add a row and stop the confirmation.
- Only the Bid Liaison, their manager or a Tender administrator see **Mark Submitted**. Confirming ready is not required to submit, but the Accountability measure **Ready at 72 Hours (Bid Liaison)** counts the bids confirmed ready at least 72 hours before the deadline.
:::
