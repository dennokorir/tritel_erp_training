The **bid desk** is the team of Bid Liaisons who prepare and submit Tritel's bids. Bids arrive unevenly, and some take far more work than others. To keep the load fair, each tender carries **effort points**, and when the Head of Commercial assigns a Bid Liaison, the window shows how many points each person already has this quarter and whether the new deadline clashes with one of theirs.

The people counted are those on the **Bid Desk Team** list (**Tender Manager › Configuration › Bid Desk Team**) with **Full-time on Bids** switched on. The Head of Commercial and Tender administrators keep this list.

## Effort points

Each tender is worth:

| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| Base | 1 (0.5 if **Dropped**; a **Cancelled** tender is worth 0 in total) |
| Short notice: captured less than 7 days before the submission deadline | +1 |
| A bid bond is required | +1 |
| Flagged must-win before it was submitted | +2 |

A tender recorded 5 days before its deadline, needing a bid bond, and flagged must-win while in **Bidding** is worth 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 = **5 points**. See [Bid desk workload points](/tenders/calculations/workload-points/).

## The Assign Bid Liaison window

<Illustration name="tenders/concept-fair-workload" caption="Assigning a Bid Liaison. The numbers match the list below." />

1. The load table for the current calendar quarter. For each full-time Bid Liaison: **Points** (the effort points of their tenders with a submission deadline in this quarter), **Open now** (their tenders in **Draft** or **Bidding**) and **Deadlines within 3 days** of this tender's deadline. The badges:
   - **Over fair share**: their points plus this tender's points would be more than 15% above the team average. The average includes this tender's points.
   - **Clash**: another of their open tenders closes within 3 days of this one.
   - **Suggested**: of the people neither over nor clashing, the one with the fewest points (then the fewest open bids). The list is sorted with them first.
2. **Bid Liaison**: the person you choose.
3. If they are over their fair share or clashing, a red line says why, for example *Employee A is over their fair share this quarter and has another deadline within 3 days.*
4. **Why This Person?** is then required. Without it, assigning stops with *Please say why this person should take the bid.* The reason is written in the chatter and shown on the **Bid Readiness** tab as **Why Assigned Despite Load**.

Worked example: Employee A has 6 points, Employee B 4 and Employee C 2, and the new tender is worth 2. The average is (6 + 4 + 2 + 2) ÷ 3 = 4.67, so the limit is 4.67 × 1.15 = 5.37. With the new tender, A would have 8 and B 6 (both over), and C 4. If C has no clash, C is **Suggested**.

If nobody is marked full-time, the table says *Nobody is marked as full-time on bids yet.*

## Bid decision

The first time a tender moves to **Bidding** or is dropped, the system stamps **Bid Decision Made** with the date and time. It shows on the **Bid Readiness** tab.

## How it counts in Accountability

The Accountability Framework reads the bid desk to measure, among others: **Fair Workload Balance (Head of Commercial)**, **Deadline Clash-Free (Head of Commercial)**, **Bid Decision Lead Time (Account Manager)** and **(Head of Commercial)**, **Submission Buffer (Bid Liaison)**, **Ready at 72 Hours** and **Repeat Error Rate (Bid Liaison)**. Tenders recorded before the bid desk was introduced are not counted.

:::note[Good to know]
- The window only checks the load; it doesn't stop you assigning someone over their share. It just asks why.
- When you reassign a tender, its own points are still added to the team average, even though they are already counted for the current Bid Liaison.
- **Bid Bond Required** adds a point but can't be set on the back-office form today.
:::
