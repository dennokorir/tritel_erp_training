A customer's account plan holds sensitive thinking: strategy, risks, who at the customer is warm or cooling. So only the plan's **audience** can see it:

- the customer's **account manager**,
- every manager above them in the org chart (their **Manager**, that person's manager, and so on up), and
- everyone who is **Head of Commercial**.

Everyone else, including other account managers, doesn't see the plan at all. The audience follows the employee records: when an account manager changes, or someone's **Manager** changes, the audience is updated straight away.

The same rule covers everything that hangs off the plan: its quarterly reviews, key people and actions, and the customer's organogram.

<Illustration name="tenders/matrix-account-plan-privacy" caption="Who sees and does what with one customer's account plan." />

## Who can do what

| Person | See | Edit the plan and organogram | Approve or return |
|---|---|---|---|
| The customer's account manager | Yes | Yes. Writes the plan, submits it, starts and submits quarterly reviews, uses **Revise Plan**. | No |
| Their direct supervisor (the plan's **Approver**) | Yes | No | Yes, plans and reviews |
| Managers higher up | Yes | No: view only | No |
| Head of Commercial | Every plan | Yes | Yes, every plan and review |
| Tender administrators, Head of Channel, other staff | No (unless they are in the audience) | No | No |

Only the Head of Commercial can change a plan's **Due date**. Tender administrators keep the **Account Plans** settings (see [Tender Manager configuration](/tenders/configuration/#account-plans-settings)), and they still set tiers on target accounts, but **they no longer see every plan**. A Tender administrator sees a plan only if they are in its audience, for example as the account manager's manager.

## What is hidden outside the audience

On a company customer's form, someone outside the audience doesn't see:

- the **Account Plan**, **Organogram** and **Awarded, 4Q** buttons (and their figures stay empty in lists and exports),
- the organogram role shown under each contact's name, and
- the recent activity in the organogram's side panel.

If they reach the customer's organogram another way, for example from a link someone sent them, they see: *Customer A's account plan and organogram are only visible to its account manager, their managers and the Head of Commercial.* A link to the plan itself shows Odoo's general message that they aren't allowed to see it.

## Tender attribution

**Tender attribution** (who is credited with a won tender, and how much) is also tighter than before. Everyone used to be able to read and change it. Now:

- the person credited, and every manager above them, can **see** their lines;
- the Head of Commercial, the Head of Channel and Tender administrators can **see and change** all of it;
- nobody else can see it.

See [Attribute a won tender](/tenders/how-to/bids/attribute-a-won-tender/).

## Messages you may see

| Message | Why |
|---|---|
| *Only the account manager of Customer A can change its plan.* | You are in the audience, but only the account manager or the Head of Commercial can edit the plan. |
| *Only the account manager's supervisor can approve or return this.* | Only the **Approver** or the Head of Commercial can approve or return. |
| *Only the Head of Commercial can change a plan's due date.* | The **Due date** is set from Settings; only the Head of Commercial can change it for one plan. |
| *Only Customer A's account manager or the Head of Commercial can change its organogram.* | Managers can look at the chart but not change it. |
| *Customer A's account plan and organogram are only visible to its account manager, their managers and the Head of Commercial.* | You are outside the audience. |

:::note[Good to know]
- A Head of Commercial who is also a customer's account manager can approve their own plan and reviews, because the Head of Commercial can approve any plan.
- The **Tender Customers** menus **Account Plans**, **Plan Reviews**, **Plan Review Board** and **My Portfolio** are shown to all staff. Outside the audience they are simply empty.
- The audience goes by the plan's **Account manager**. If the customer gets a new account manager, the Head of Commercial needs to change the **Account manager** on the existing plan, or the new account manager won't see it.
:::
